Current status

  • No active billable subscriptions exist. Checkout is switched off in this preview. No card has been charged and none can be.
  • If you left your email, we saved only your email, sport and class year to send the free guide and the monthly rules briefing. You can ask us to remove it at any time.
  • Nothing here is a purchase, a trial or an offer to sell. Prices on the price page are proposed.

Look up or remove your information

There is no login yet, so lookup is by email. Write from the address you signed up with and tell us what you want: see what we saved, change your sport or class year, unsubscribe, or delete it. Every email we send also has an unsubscribe link.

Support contact: this preview has no monitored support mailbox yet. A real address will be published here before any paid launch. Draft for review. Not yet in effect.

Cancel a subscription (once live)

  1. Open your billing page from the link in your receipt email.
  2. Choose Cancel. It takes one click, with no survey and no call.
  3. Your page shows the date your membership ends. Cancelling stops the next renewal.

The 30-day full refund is described in the refund policy and the auto-renewal disclosure.
