Billing

Billing and cancellation

Billing is switched off in this preview. There are no active billable subscriptions, no trials and no payment methods on file, so there is nothing to manage or cancel today.

How billing will work once live

  • Manage or cancel: a billing link in your receipt email opens a secure Stripe page tied to your own purchase. It cannot open anyone else's.
  • Cancel in one click. No survey gate, no call. Cancelling stops the next renewal and your page shows the end date.
  • Your renewal date is shown on that page and in the notice we send before any renewal.
  • Refunds: ask by email within 30 days. See the refund policy.

If you think you were charged

You should not have been; checkout is off. If you see a charge you do not recognise, contact your card issuer and email us from your signup address. Support contact: no monitored mailbox is published yet. Draft for review. Not yet in effect.
