Roster math

How many spots open in your kid's class, and how much aid can follow them.

Since July 1, 2025, Division I schools that opted into the House settlement use roster limits instead of scholarship caps. Division II still uses equivalency caps. Division III gives no athletic aid.

*Pre-2025 limits; non-opted-in schools follow settlement terms. See the NCAA Feb 2026 Division I Q&A. Opted-in roster limits: 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual, Bylaw 17.2 (adopted 6/6/25, effective 7/1/25). Division II: Bylaw 15.5. Division III: Bylaw 15. Verified 2026-11-05.
