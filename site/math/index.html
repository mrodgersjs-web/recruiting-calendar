Roster math

How many spots open in your kid's class, and how much aid can follow them.

Since July 1, 2025, Division I schools that opted into the House settlement use roster limits instead of scholarship caps. Division II still uses equivalency caps. Division III gives no athletic aid.

*Pre-2025 limits; non-opted-in schools follow settlement terms. See the NCAA Feb 2026 Division I Q&A.   2. Is your program opted in to the House settlement roster limits?
