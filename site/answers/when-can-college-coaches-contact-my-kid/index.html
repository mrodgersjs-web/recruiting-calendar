Parent answers

When can college coaches contact my kid?

The short answer: Most D1 sports: June 15 after sophomore year. D1 baseball: August 1 of junior year. D2: calls any time, in-person June 15.

The rule, the date, the bylaw

  • Football, Class of 2029: D1 football coaches may start calling, texting and emailing
    Date: 2027-06-15
    After sophomore year, coaches may call and send recruiting messages. Unofficial visits involving the athletics department were already allowed at any age, subject to the calendar.
    Rule: D1 Bylaws 13.1.3.1, 13.4.1 · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
  • Baseball, Class of 2029: D1 baseball coaches may start calling, texting and emailing
    Date: 2027-08-01
    Baseball is different: D1 baseball opens August 1 of junior year, not the June 15 date most sports use. Before this, a coach can't answer a recruiting message, even if your kid sends it first.
    Rule: D1 Bylaws 13.1.3.1.1, 13.4.1.1 · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
  • Track & XC, Class of 2029: Calls, messages and off-campus contact open together
    Date: 2027-06-15
    Changed October 7, 2026: track and cross country off-campus contact now opens June 15 after sophomore year, the same day as calls and texts. Older guides still say August 1.
    Rule: D1 Bylaws 13.1.1.1.9, 13.1.3.1, 13.4.1 · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
  • Baseball, Class of 2029: Division II in-person contact and official visits may begin
    Date: 2027-06-15
    D2 coaches may make in-person contact and host paid visits. D2 calls, texts and emails are already allowed at any time.
    Rule: D2 Bylaws 13.1.1.1, 13.3.1.2 · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
