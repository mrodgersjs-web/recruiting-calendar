When can a college make a written scholarship offer?

The short answer: D1 football: not before November 15 of senior year (rule adopted October 2025). D1 track: not before August 1 of senior year. Verbal offers are not binding.

The rule, the date, the bylaw

    Date: 2028-11-15
    Under a rule adopted October 2025, a written athletics aid offer can't be made before November 15 of senior year. A verbal offer isn't binding on either side.

    Date: 2028-08-01

    Rule: D1 Bylaw 13.9.3.1 · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
