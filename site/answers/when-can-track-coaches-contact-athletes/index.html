When can track and cross country coaches contact athletes?

The short answer: June 15 after sophomore year for calls, messages and off-campus contact (changed October 7, 2026). Visits open August 1 of junior year.

The rule, the date, the bylaw

  • Track & XC, Class of 2029: Calls, messages and off-campus contact open together
    Date: 2027-06-15
    Changed October 7, 2026: track and cross country off-campus contact now opens June 15 after sophomore year, the same day as calls and texts. Older guides still say August 1.
    Rule: D1 Bylaws 13.1.1.1.9, 13.1.3.1, 13.4.1 · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
  • Track & XC, Class of 2029: Official and unofficial visits may begin
    Date: 2027-08-01
    August 1 of junior year for official visits and unofficial visits involving the athletics department.
    Rule: D1 Bylaws 13.6.2.1.1, 13.7.1.1 · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05

Rules checked 2026-11-05 against the 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual. NCAA rules change; follow the bylaw link for the current text. Projected dates are calculated from current rules and are not published NCAA dates.
