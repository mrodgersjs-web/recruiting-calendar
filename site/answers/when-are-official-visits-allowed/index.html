When are NCAA official visits allowed?

The short answer: It depends on sport and division: D1 football April 1 of junior year, D1 track August 1, D1 baseball September 1, D3 January 1 of junior year.

The rule, the date, the bylaw

  • Football, Class of 2029: Junior-year official visit window opens
    Date: 2028-04-01
    Paid visits run April 1 through the Sunday before the last Wednesday in June (2028-06-25 by the formula). They resume September 1 of senior year. Each visit needs a transcript, an Eligibility Center account and a spot on the school's list.
    Rule: D1 Bylaws 13.6.2.1.7, 13.6.3 · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
  • Track & XC, Class of 2029: Official and unofficial visits may begin
    Date: 2027-08-01
    August 1 of junior year for official visits and unofficial visits involving the athletics department.
    Rule: D1 Bylaws 13.6.2.1.1, 13.7.1.1 · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
  • Baseball, Class of 2029: D1 baseball off-campus contact and visits may begin
    Date: 2027-09-01
    Official visits, unofficial visits involving the athletics department, and off-campus contact all open September 1 of junior year. Recruiting talk at college camps opens the same day. The recruiting calendar can still close a specific week.
    Rule: D1 Bylaws 13.1.1.1.1, 13.6.2.1.2, 13.7.1.2 · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
  • Baseball, Class of 2029: Division III official visits may begin
    Date: 2028-01-01
    D3 paid visits open January 1 of junior year. D3 offers no athletic scholarships; aid there is academic and need-based.
    Rule: D3 Bylaw 13.6.1.1.1 · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
