When does D1 baseball recruiting start?

The short answer: D1 baseball coaches may start calling, texting and emailing August 1 of junior year; off-campus contact and visits open September 1.

The rule, the date, the bylaw

  • Baseball, Class of 2029: D1 baseball coaches may start calling, texting and emailing
    Date: 2027-08-01
    Baseball is different: D1 baseball opens August 1 of junior year, not the June 15 date most sports use. Before this, a coach can't answer a recruiting message, even if your kid sends it first.
    Rule: D1 Bylaws 13.1.3.1.1, 13.4.1.1 · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
  • Baseball, Class of 2029: D1 baseball off-campus contact and visits may begin
    Date: 2027-09-01
    Official visits, unofficial visits involving the athletics department, and off-campus contact all open September 1 of junior year. Recruiting talk at college camps opens the same day. The recruiting calendar can still close a specific week.
    Rule: D1 Bylaws 13.1.1.1.1, 13.6.2.1.2, 13.7.1.2 · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05

Rules checked 2026-11-05 against the 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual. NCAA rules change; follow the bylaw link for the current text. Projected dates are calculated from current rules and are not published NCAA dates.
