When is signing day for the Class of 2029?

The short answer: Projected from current rules: D1 football early period Nov 29 to Dec 1, 2028 and regular date Feb 7, 2029; D1 baseball and track open Nov 8, 2028. Not yet published by the NCAA.

The rule, the date, the bylaw

  • Football, Class of 2029: Projected early signing period
    Date: 2028-11-29 (projected)
    Calculated from current rules: Wednesday before the first Saturday in December, through Friday (Nov 29 to Dec 1, 2028). Not yet published.
    Rule: D1 Bylaw 13.02.13.1(c) · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
  • Football, Class of 2029: Projected regular signing date
    Date: 2029-02-07 (projected)
    First Wednesday in February, calculated from current rules. Each school sets its own final date. There's no universal April 1 cutoff.
    Rule: D1 Bylaw 13.02.13.1(e) · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
  • Baseball, Class of 2029: Projected D1 baseball signing opens
    Date: 2028-11-08 (projected)
    Calculated from current rules: the second Wednesday in November. Not yet a published calendar date. We recheck it when the NCAA publishes.
    Rule: D1 Bylaw 13.02.13.1(f) · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05
  • Track & XC, Class of 2029: Projected signing opens
    Date: 2028-11-08 (projected)
    Second Wednesday in November, calculated from current rules. Not yet published.
    Rule: D1 Bylaw 13.02.13.1(f) · 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual · checked 2026-11-05

Rules checked 2026-11-05 against the 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual. NCAA rules change; follow the bylaw link for the current text. Projected dates are calculated from current rules and are not published NCAA dates.
