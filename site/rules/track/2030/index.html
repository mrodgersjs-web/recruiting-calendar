Track & field / cross country · Class of 2030

Track & field / cross country recruiting dates for the Class of 2030.

9 dates, verified 2026-11-05 against the 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual. Dates are calculated from current rules for this graduating year; anything not yet on a published NCAA calendar is labeled Projected.

  1. 15
    Nov 2027Our recommended date

    Have the counselor check every course against the NCAA list

    Map completed and planned classes against your school's NCAA-approved course list. Not every class counts. This is our recommended date, not an NCAA one.

    D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.1 · source

    If you waitProblems found in 12th grade are often too late to fix.
  2. 15
    Jun 2028Opening

    Calls, messages and off-campus contact open together

    Changed October 7, 2026: track and cross country off-campus contact now opens June 15 after sophomore year, the same day as calls and texts. Older guides still say August 1.

    D1 Bylaws 13.1.1.1.9, 13.1.3.1, 13.4.1 · source

    If you waitNone for starting later.
  3. 1
    Aug 2028Opening

    Official and unofficial visits may begin

    August 1 of junior year for official visits and unofficial visits involving the athletics department.

    D1 Bylaws 13.6.2.1.1, 13.7.1.1 · source

    If you waitVisiting too early is a violation.
  4. 15
    Aug 2028Our recommended date

    Open the NCAA Eligibility Center certification account

    D1 and D2 recruits need a certification account ($110 domestic, fee waivers possible) before any D1 official visit. The free Profile Page isn't enough. Recommended date, not an NCAA deadline.

    D1 Bylaw 13.6.3 · source

    If you waitWithout it, a school can't host you on an official visit.
  5. 1
    Aug 2029Opening

    Written scholarship offers may arrive

    A written athletics aid offer can't come before August 1 of senior year.

    D1 Bylaw 13.9.3.1 · source

    If you waitNone. Compare any offer against the full net price.
  6. Your school's date
    Hard limit

    Finish 10 of your 16 core courses before senior year

    Division I requires 10 NCAA-approved core courses, 7 of them English, math or science, finished before the first day of the seventh semester. Those grades lock. Your school sets the exact date.

    D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.1(b) · source

    If you miss itCan block qualifier status and first-year competition. Senior-year courses can't repair it.
  7. 8
    Nov 2029Projected

    Projected signing opens

    Second Wednesday in November, calculated from current rules. Not yet published.

    D1 Bylaw 13.02.13.1(f) · source

    If you waitNo penalty for signing later.
  8. 1
    Apr 2030Window opens

    Final amateurism certification request opens

    Fall 2029 enrollees can request final amateurism certification in their Eligibility Center account from April 1. It's when the window opens, not a cutoff.

    Eligibility Center procedure · source

    If you waitNot an automatic loss, but waiting can delay clearance to compete.
  9. Your school's date
    Hard limit

    Graduate and get final certification

    Finish all 16 core courses with at least a 2.3 core GPA, graduate, and have the school send the final transcript to the Eligibility Center. A transcript GPA isn't the same as a core GPA.

    D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.3 · source

    If you miss itCan delay or block first-year competition. A verbal commitment isn't clearance.
