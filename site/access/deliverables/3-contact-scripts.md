# Worksheet 3: Contact scripts (athlete's voice, parent copied)

*Rules and scripts for families to use themselves. The Commit Book does not contact coaches, place athletes or promise introductions, replies or offers. Verify every date on the NCAA page for your sport.*
Draft in your own words. Keep each note short, specific to ONE program, and send from the athlete's own account. Parents organize; athletes write. Do not mass-send: coaches delete blasts.

## intro_email
Needs: coach_last, athlete_name, sport, position, team, class_year, specific_reason, video_url, stat_line, questionnaire_date, athlete_phone_or_email

```
Subject: {sport} {position}, Class of {class_year}: {athlete_name}

Coach {coach_last},

My name is {athlete_name}. I play {position} for {team} and I am in the Class of {class_year}. I am writing because {specific_reason}.

My highlight link: {video_url}
{stat_line}
I filled out your recruiting questionnaire on {questionnaire_date}. If there is anything else you would like from me, I will send it.

Thank you for your time.
{athlete_name}
{athlete_phone_or_email}
(My parent is copied on this email.)
```

## dm_short
Needs: coach_last, athlete_name, position, class_year, specific_reason, video_url

```
Coach {coach_last}, I am {athlete_name}, {position}, Class of {class_year}. {specific_reason}. Highlights: {video_url}. Thank you for your time.
```

## post_camp_followup
Needs: coach_last, athlete_name, event_name, takeaway, video_url, class_year

```
Subject: Thank you from {athlete_name} after {event_name}

Coach {coach_last},

Thank you for running {event_name}. One thing I took from it: {takeaway}. Here is my updated film: {video_url}.
Are you recruiting my position for the Class of {class_year}? I understand if the answer is not yet.

{athlete_name}
```

## still_recruiting_nudge
Needs: coach_last, athlete_name, position, class_year, last_touch_date

```
Subject: Quick question, {athlete_name} Class of {class_year}

Coach {coach_last},

I wanted to follow up on my note from {last_touch_date}. Are you still recruiting {position} in the Class of {class_year}? A short answer either way helps me plan. Thank you.

{athlete_name}
```

## reference_request_to_own_coach
Needs: own_coach_last, list_size, video_url, three_sentences, athlete_name

```
Subject: Could you vouch for me with a few programs?

Coach {own_coach_last},

I am building a list of {list_size} programs. Would you be willing to (1) send film to them or (2) take a short call? My list and highlight link: {video_url}. Here are three sentences you could say: {three_sentences}

Thank you,
{athlete_name}
```

## 4-touch cadence (reminders, hypothesis spacing; not a schedule we run)
| Touch | Days after first | Template |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | intro_email |
| 2 | 10 | still_recruiting_nudge |
| 3 | 24 | post_camp_followup |
| 4 | 45 | still_recruiting_nudge |

Target list cap: 30 schools. Silence is often the rule, not rejection.
