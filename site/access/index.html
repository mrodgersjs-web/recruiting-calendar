    Before you sign: questions to ask in writing

    If you already signed and want to stop

    1. Find the contract and any emails confirming it. Note the start date, the term, the cancellation clause and the notice window.
    2. Write down what you were told on calls. Keep screenshots of the plan page and billing.
    3. Send a written cancellation request by email, and by certified mail if the contract names an address. Keep the date and a copy. Ask for written confirmation of the end date and that no further charges will be made.
    4. If you paid by card, check statements for charges after your request. Your card issuer has its own dispute process; ask what it requires and by when.
    5. If you financed through a third party, contact that lender separately about what cancellation does to the payments.
    6. If the service does not respond, a complaint to your state attorney general's consumer office or the Better Business Bureau is a common next step. A lawyer can tell you what your contract allows.
