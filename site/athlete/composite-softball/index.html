Sophomore spring

The one coach who keeps writing

What was happening

One assistant coach keeps emailing. In the sourced path this composite draws on, the player visited and committed where she felt at home. The rule that mattered: Division II calls, texts and emails are allowed at any time, and in-person contact and paid official visits open June 15 after sophomore year (D2 Bylaws 13.1.1.1, 13.3.1.2). The rule guarantees nothing.

What they did

Her family keeps a grid of every coach, date and next step with the Contact Log, and checks the rule at the source. The Contact Log is coming soon.

How it helps

A written record keeps replies from slipping. It does not create interest.
