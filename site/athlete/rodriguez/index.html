What was happening

Cristelle went on to an NAIA school. The NAIA rule listed with her story says to register with the NAIA Eligibility Center. A 2.3 high school GPA makes a freshman eligible, or two of three: ACT 18/SAT 970, 2.0 GPA, top half of class.
