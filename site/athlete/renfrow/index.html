High school, c.2013

A scholarship on one table, a walk-on spot on the other

What was happening

Hunter Renfrow was a quarterback at Socastee High School in Myrtle Beach, around 150 pounds. Appalachian State recruited him with a scholarship. Clemson offered only preferred walk-on status.

The decision

Guaranteed money or the school you want

What was happening

The sourced record shows Renfrow chose the preferred walk-on spot at Clemson over a scholarship. His own reasons and what his family said at home are not in our sources, so we do not guess at them.

Questions for the coach
