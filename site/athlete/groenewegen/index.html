Not heavily recruited? Go where the coaches already are

Sara Groenewegen, a softball player from White Rock, British Columbia, later played at Minnesota. The facts below come from the sources linked in each chapter. The Commit Book did not exist then, so the steps marked 'If your family is here today' are hypothetical.

Real athlete. Facts from public sources, each linked. Sara Groenewegen and their family did not use The Commit Book; the steps shown are what a family in the same spot can do today. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Sara Groenewegen or Minnesota.

High school years

Only a couple of schools showed real interest

What was happening

Sara said she was not highly recruited. Only a couple of schools showed serious interest. Many families know this quiet stretch, where the phone does not ring and you wonder what to do.

Club season

A Minnesota coach saw her at tournaments in Seattle and Portland

What was happening

According to the sources, Minnesota coach Jessica Allister first saw Sara at club tournaments in Seattle and Portland. Sara lived in British Columbia, far from the usual softball hotbeds. The tournaments were where coaches could watch her play.

If your family is here today

If your family is here today: before you pay for a tournament, find out which college coaches attend it and what it will cost. The Camp ROI Playbook (camp-roi) covers camp types, cost budgets and follow-up after an event.

How it helps

You spend travel money on events where your targets show up. Nobody can promise a coach will notice your daughter.

Between events

Keep the film and the profile ready for when a coach looks

What was happening

The sources say coaches found Sara through club play. That is all we know about how she was seen. Nothing in them describes her film or her profile, so we can only say that coaches who watch live often look again afterward.

Junior year

An assistant coach kept emailing

What was happening

In Sara's junior year, Minnesota assistant Piper Ritter persistently emailed her. Sara visited and committed after feeling at home. Persistence from the coach's side mattered here, and the sources say nothing more about the emails.

If your family is here today

If your family is here today: write your own first email to the coaches on your list, and send it at a time the sport expects. The First Contact Pack (coach-emails) has templates and a reply-handling tree. It is coming soon.

How it helps

A clear first message makes it easy for a coach to answer. Whether they answer is up to them.

The visit

She visited a school she had never heard of

What was happening

The sources say Sara agreed to visit a school she had never heard of, and she committed after feeling at home. That is the full account we have. We do not know the details of the trip.
