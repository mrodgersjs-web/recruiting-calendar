What was happening

Fall 2022

Several options, and a school with no guarantee

What was happening

The same profile says she had several college options and chose Brown with no guarantee of a spot. She went through walk-on tryouts. The sources do not say what her family discussed.

If your family is here today

If your family is here today: learn the difference between a preferred walk-on and a tryout walk-on before you commit to a school. The Walk-On Playbook (walk-on, $15) covers both, plus roster limits and what to get in writing.

How it helps

Knowing the terms keeps you from mistaking an invitation to try out for a roster spot. It promises no outcome.
