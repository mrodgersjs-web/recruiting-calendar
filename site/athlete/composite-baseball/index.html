Junior spring

Good player, no offers

What was happening

Diego plays infield for his public high school and travel ball in the summer. He is solid. The four-year offers have not come. His parents are not sure what that means or what to do next.

What they did

They put every coach contact in one place so nothing lives in a text thread. They use the Contact Log for that grid. It is coming soon, not on sale yet.

One list shows who answered and who did not. It does not make a coach reply.

Senior winter

Keep the core courses in order

What was happening

Diego may transfer later to a Division I school. That means his high school core courses still count. D1 initial eligibility needs 16 NCAA-approved core courses (D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.1).

What they did

His mother checks his course list against the approved list early. The family uses the eligibility playbook and meets the counselor with its script.

How it helps

A gap found in senior year can still be fixed. A gap found after graduation cannot. The rule guarantees nothing. Check it at the source.

Senior spring

Pick the place where he plays right away

What was happening

Diego chooses a junior college where he will play right away. The point is innings and film now. A walk-on style path means getting things in writing, not trusting a hallway promise.

What they did

They use the Walk-On Playbook to know what to get in writing. They also build a short highlight reel from the innings he already has.

How it helps

A clear reel and a clear written role make the next conversation easier. Neither is a promise of a spot.

Sophomore year at JUCO

Two seasons, then a transfer

What was happening

After two seasons, Diego looks at four-year schools where he can play and finish his degree. Offers come with different aid, fees and terms. His parents want to compare them fairly.

What they did

They use the Offers & Signing Day playbook for verbal versus written offers and signing periods. They put two offers side by side in one grid.

How it helps

The comparison is on paper, not in memory. In this composite he transfers, plays, studies and graduates. No draft. Junior college was a route, not a detour. Nothing here proves any playbook caused it.
