No D1 offers? Division III can build an athlete

Ali Marpet, a football player from Hastings-on-Hudson, NY, went to Hobart, a D3 school, and was drafted in the second round in 2015. The facts below come from the linked sources. The Commit Book did not exist then, so the steps marked 'If your family is here today' are hypothetical.

Real athlete. Facts from public sources, each linked. Ali Marpet and their family did not use The Commit Book; the steps shown are what a family in the same spot can do today. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Ali Marpet or Hobart.

High school

Undersized, two sports, no D1 offers

What was happening

Ali was about 235 pounds in high school. He played football and basketball, and he had no D1 offers. Some families reach this point and wonder whether the door has closed.

High school

FCS interest came as walk-on only

What was happening

The sources say Fordham, Marist and Holy Cross showed FCS interest, and it was walk-on only, with no scholarship. A walk-on spot is not the same as a scholarship, and families often learn the difference late.

If your family is here today

If your family is here today: ask what a walk-on offer means in writing before you answer. The Walk-On Playbook (walk-on) covers preferred versus tryout walk-ons, roster limits and what to get in writing.

How it helps

You know what to ask. It does not tell you whether to accept.
