Free tools we recommend alongside us

  • A free FieldLevel account, for a profile coaches in some sports use.
  • Each program's recruiting questionnaire, on the school's athletics site.
  • The NCAA Eligibility Center, which D1 and D2 recruits need anyway.

What parents tell each other

The common advice in parent forums: skip paid services, email coaches directly, and keep your own spreadsheet of schools, coaches, emails and follow-ups. The Sideline tracker is that spreadsheet, already built, next to the dates that decide when each email can get an answer.
