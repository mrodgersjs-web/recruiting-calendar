    Roster math

    "How many scholarships do they have" is now the wrong question.

    On July 1, 2025, Division I schools that opted into the House settlement replaced scholarship caps with roster limits. A program can now fund every rostered player, or none. The number that decides your kid's chances is how many spots open in their class.

    Most recruiting services still sell the word "scholarship". We show you the rule, the date it changed, and the questions that get a real answer out of a coach.

    What families get wrong, and what it costs

    The expensive mistakes are dates, not effort.

    01

    Core courses

    Division I requires 16 core courses, with 10 finished before senior year. Not every class your school offers counts. Families often find out in 12th grade, when it is too late to fix.

    02

    Camps before contact

    A showcase can cost hundreds of dollars. If the coach has never heard of your kid, it is an expensive practice. Email first, then go.

    03

    Parents doing outreach

    Coaches want to hear from the athlete. The parent's job is the calendar, the paperwork and the money. We built the product around that split.
