Emerging and fastest-growing sports

The sports growing fastest are the ones the recruiting services know least about.

High school participation in girls flag football, girls wrestling and boys volleyball jumped in 2024-25. College programs are being added just as fast, and the rules change month to month. Here are the primary-source numbers, the pathway by governing body, and ten questions to ask before you sign or pay a deposit.

Every number below is from the NFHS 2024-25 High School Athletics Participation Survey or from the NCAA, NAIA or NJCAA itself. Checked 2026-11-06.

The 2024-25 numbers

8,266,244high school athletes in 2024-25, an all-time record (4,726,648 boys, 3,539,596 girls). Up 203,942 from 8,062,302. NFHS 2024-25 High School Athletics Participation Survey
68,847girls flag football participants, +60% in one year, 2,736 schools. NFHS 2024-25 survey.
74,064girls wrestling participants, +15% in one year, 8,100 schools. More than double the 31,654 of 2021-22. NFHS 2024-25 survey.
95,972boys volleyball participants, +24.2% over two years (+12.57% in one year), 4,303 schools. NFHS 2024-25 survey.
Two numbers we did not use, and why. A working draft gave the 2024-25 total as 8,215,882. That figure is not in the NFHS survey or its releases; the survey's totals table prints 8,266,244, so that is what this page shows. The draft also gave 1,781 schools for flag football and "2,900+" for boys volleyball. The NFHS table lists 2,736 and 4,303, so those are shown instead.

Girls Flag Football

NFHS 2024-25: 68,847 participants, +60%. The NCAA added flag football to its Emerging Sports for Women program in January 2026 (NCAA). The NAIA approved it as a championship sport starting 2026-27 (NAIA). The NJCAA approved championship status starting 2027-28 (NJCAA).

Girls Wrestling

NFHS 2024-25: 74,064 participants, +15%. The NCAA made women's wrestling its 91st championship and held the first National Collegiate Women's Wrestling Championships in March 2026 (NCAA). The NAIA has had a women's wrestling national championship since 2022 (NAIA); its eligibility rules are in NAIA Bylaws Article V Section C. Read Cristelle's path to NAIA Doane.

Boys Volleyball

NFHS 2024-25: 95,972 participants, +24.2% over two years. In college it is a National Collegiate championship (Division I and II together) plus a separate Division III championship, NAIA varsity, and NJCAA, which runs its first national championship in 2026 (AVCA program lists).
