# The Commit Book Contact Log
Every coach touch, in one grid. The log a D1 staff expects you to keep.

## The grid (log one row per touch; CSV provided)
Coach | Title | School | Division | Date | Channel | What you asked | Their exact answer (quote) | Next step | Owner | Due date | Athlete/parent owner

## Weekly review (5 minutes, Sunday)
1. Coach silent 10+ days after they owned the next step: one short update email with fresh film or stats.
2. New follower/view from a coach account: log it, do not react it.
3. Verbal interest without a date attached: ask one date question (when does your staff next review my film?).

## Follow-up triggers
- Unanswered 10 days -> update, not reminder: one new fact (stats, offer, camp invite).
- Film request -> within 24h, one link, unlisted, captioned position/class/school.
- Visit invite -> ask what you will see (facility, class meet, position coach) and who pays.
- New coach at a listed school -> re-introduce; they inherit the file differently.

## Rules
- If it is not in the log it did not happen.
- Numbers over adjectives in every update: 92% completion, 1.4 blocks/game, 5:38 1600.
- One grid per school; this file + email + calendar is the whole stack.
