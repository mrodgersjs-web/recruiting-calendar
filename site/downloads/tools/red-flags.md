# Red Flags: The Checklist
40 warning signs a program is overselling the spot, the scholarship or the timeline. What to ask instead of walking away is one line under each.

## Money
1. "Scholarship" never gets a dollar figure. Ask: what is my net cost per year, in writing, line by line?
2. Aid that depends on staying on the roster the staff may not have. Ask: what happens to the money if I am not retained?
3. Verbal offer, no paperwork timeline. Ask: when does this become official, and who signs it?
4. Family expected to pay for visits the coach invited. Ask: does the program cover travel under its rules?
5. "You'll earn more later" every year, never in writing. Ask: can we put the raise conditions in the offer letter?
6. Buy-in numbers change when you ask again. Ask: which figure is the one the AD approved?
7. NIL "opportunities" that are really jobs. Ask: what exactly do I do, who pays, and what is the hourly value?
8. Scholarship split across 4 years with no protection. Ask: is it a multi-year agreement?
9. Academic aid promised before admissions clears it. Ask: does financial aid confirm this in writing?
10. Anything requiring a signature the same day. Ask: can I have 48 hours? (Real offers survive 48 hours.)

## Playing time
11. "You'll redshirt unless we need you" with no definition of "need". Ask: who decides, in writing?
12. Development plan is "practice like you play". Ask: what does your depth chart look like in year 2?
13. Transfers already committed at your position, not mentioned. Ask: where do I rank against the current roster?
14. Player minutes only quoted in wins. Ask: what is the average for players my class?
15. Position switch "for flexibility" before enrollment. Ask: has anyone my size/skill won that switch at your school?
16. No film of the position coach teaching. Ask: can I see a practice clip or talk to a current player privately?
17. Coach dodges the depth question. The dodge is the answer.

## Academic fit
18. "Any major you want" at a program where every player is in the same 3 majors. Ask: who advises engineering/pre-med students on your team?
19. Class schedule built by the office with no input. Ask: can I see a sample four-year plan?
20. Eligibility center handled "for you" with no copy to family. Ask: can we register together this week?
21. Credit transfer shrugged at after you declared them. Ask: what is the written transfer policy?
22. Study hall "recommended" only for your class year group. Ask: what do players do after freshman year?
23. The major that keeps eligibility is the only one offered to athletes. Ask: what have players in my intended major graduated with?

## Treatment
24. Trainer cleared, coach overrides, no documentation. Ask: what is your written medical protocol?
25. Injuries logged in a group chat, not a medical system. Ask: who keeps the medical record?
26. Parents get the vibe, athlete gets the silence (or reverse). Ask: who is my single point of contact?
27. Team rules change mid-year without a written notice. Ask: where are the team rules published?
28. A current player asks you to "be careful" and won't say more. Ask the player one question: would you re-commit knowing what you know now?
29. Staff emails you at 1am and none on Monday. Ask: who is my coach, and when do they actually work?
30. Social posts about you before you signed. Ask: is this a commitment or marketing?
31. "We love your character" with zero stat or film reference. Ask: what did you see on tape specifically?
32. Coach cannot name your last film without looking. You are a number.
33. No answer when you asked what you need to improve. Real staffs always have the list.
34. Multiple coaches giving conflicting timelines. Ask the head coach to name one owner.
35. You are asked to commit before a visit. Visit first, always.
36. The a signed letter of intent is explained as "just a form". It is a contract. Read the exit clause.
37. "Lots of programs want you" with no names. Ask which programs, and mean it.
38. Scholarship money described as your parents' tax problem. Ask what the school reports.
39. Coach disappears after signing day spring. Ask current players about staff stability.
40. Your gut says hurry. Hurry is the flag.

## Flag report
- Ends the visit: 1, 5, 8, 24, 35 (money or medical or contract, no paperwork).
- Gets louder, not fatal: 11, 12, 17, 18 (common; the written answer is the test).
- Pattern beats incident: one flag is a sentence, three flags in one group is the culture.
