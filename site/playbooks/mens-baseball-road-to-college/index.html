Where these dates come from

Built from the NCAA rule text, the printed Division I calendar, the NAIA handbook and the NJCAA signing chart. Levels covered: D1, D2, D3, NAIA, junior college.

  • NCAA Division I Manual 2026-27, 412 pages, page footer dated 11 November 2026
  • NCAA Division I Manual 2024-25, Bylaws 15.5.4 and 15.5.4.1, for the superseded rule of 11.7 scholarships, 27 counters and a 25 percent floor

  • NCAA Division III Manual 2026-27, 254 pages
  • Division I Baseball 2026-27 NCAA Recruiting Calendar, marked "Updated: July 30, 2026." Used for every contact, quiet, dead and shutdown date and for the four labels
