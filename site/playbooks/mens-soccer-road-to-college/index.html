Where these dates come from

Built from the NCAA rule text, the printed Division I calendar, the NAIA handbook and the NJCAA procedures chart. Levels covered: D1, D2, D3, NAIA, junior college.

  • NCAA Division I Manual 2026-27, page footers dated 11 November 2026
  • NCAA Division II Manual 2026-27
  • NCAA Division III Manual 2026-27
  • "2026-27 NCAA Recruiting Calendar, Other Division I Sports," updated 4 August 2026, for the men's soccer dead periods of 9 to 12 November and 11 to 14 December and the quiet period of 23 to 25 December
  • NCAA signing dates for 2026-27: "All Other Division I and II Sports - November 11, 2026," with the final date set by each school's policy
  • NCAA 2026-27 Guide for the College-Bound Student-Athlete, for "before ninth grade," the 10/7 rule and what the Eligibility Center certifies
  • NCAA Eligibility Center fees, two readings
  • College Sports Commission roster-limit table, "Soccer (M)": old limit 9.9, roster limit 28, potential increase 18.1, and the line that scholarship limits "have been removed" at participating schools
  • The College Sports Commission prints the list of schools that opted in for 2026-27
  • Two facts were carried from this publisher's volleyball research and not re-opened for this book: that a school outside the settlement has no NCAA roster limit and keeps its 2024-25 aid ceiling (NCAA question-and-answer, May 2026), and that the National Letter of Intent ended for Division I on 9 October 2024
  • NCAA projected sport sponsorship for 2026-27, as of September 2026, final on data to 29 July 2026: men's soccer 213, 204 and 406, total 823; women's soccer 349, 261 and 416, total 1,026
  • NCAA Sports Sponsorship and Participation Rates Report, updated 4 September 2025, PDF pages 91 and 93, for 206, 211 and 410 teams, 6,901, 8,237 and 13,608 players, 28,746 in all, and every average squad size
