  2. 02 · What the family didn't knowHer family's question: will any college coach care about her times, and can she run without giving up the major she wants?
  3. 03 · A choice, not luckIn this composite, Nia asks coaches first about her intended major, then sends her marks. She visits and takes the program that says yes to both, a pattern drawn from the sources below.
  4. 04 · The rule that matteredThe rule that mattered. Division III: paid official visits open January 1 of junior year. D3 offers no athletic scholarships; aid there is academic and need-based.
