03 / D3

The decision point.

The story, in seven beats (07 is the playbooks below)

  1. 01 · The ordinary startComposite story, built from three sourced recruiting paths. Marcus is not a real person; every image of him is an illustration. He is a two-way lineman at a mid-size public school, a little light for his position, with good grades and no Division I offers.
  2. 02 · What the family didn't knowHis family asks what many football families ask: is there a real college roster for a player who is not the size the big programs list, and what does it cost if there is no athletic money?
  3. 03 · A choice, not luckIn this composite, Marcus stops waiting on Division I and asks Division III coaches about both football and his intended major. He tours campuses and goes with the coach who recruits him directly, a pattern drawn from the sources below.
  4. 04 · The rule that matteredThe rule that mattered. Division III: paid official visits open January 1 of junior year. D3 offers no athletic scholarships; aid there is academic and need-based. (D3 Bylaw 13.6.1.1.1). The rule did not cause this outcome and guarantees nothing. Check it yourself at the source. D3 Bylaw 13.6.1.1.1
  5. 05 · The fit decisionHe picks a Division III school where he can play and study what he came for. Aid is academic and need-based, not athletic. He plays four seasons, mostly as a starter in his last two, and graduates. No draft, no headline. That is the destination, not a consolation.
  6. 06 · What this story doesn't proveOne path, made of several real ones, not a formula. This person does not exist, and the ending is ordinary on purpose: most college athletes play, study and graduate. Nothing here proves a playbook, camp or service causes a result.
