03 / NAIA

The decision point.

The story, in seven beats (07 is the playbooks below)

  1. 01 · The ordinary startComposite story, built from four sourced recruiting paths. Jasmine is not a real person; her image is an illustration. She is a guard at a public high school, a steady scorer on a team that does not get much attention.
  2. 02 · What the family didn't knowHer family's question is the common one: can she play in college at all, and at what level?
  3. 03 · A choice, not luckIn this composite, Jasmine's family sends her film widely, including to NAIA and junior-college programs, and her high school coach makes calls for her. Widening the list is the choice, as the sources describe.
  4. 04 · The rule that matteredThe rule that mattered. NAIA: register with the NAIA Eligibility Center (PlayNAIA.org). A 2.3 high school GPA makes a freshman eligible, or two of three: ACT 18/SAT 970, 2.0 GPA, top half of class. NAIA publishes no contact-date calendar. (NAIA Bylaws Art. V Sec. C, 2026-27 NAIA Handbook p.70). The rule did not cause this outcome and guarantees nothing. Check it yourself at the source. NAIA Bylaws Art. V Sec. C, 2026-27 NAIA Handbook p.70
  5. 05 · The fit decisionShe chooses an NAIA school where she plays from her first season and gets a mix of aid. She graduates on time. That is a full college career, not a consolation.
  6. 06 · What this story doesn't proveOne path, made of several real ones, not a formula. This person does not exist, and the ending is ordinary on purpose: most college athletes play, study and graduate. Nothing here proves a playbook, camp or service causes a result. We do not contact coaches, and we never contact your kid.
