Why the plans are split by sport

A football timeline does not fit a softball family. The rules on when a coach can call you differ by sport. So do camp dates, signing dates, and how many spots a team can hand out. So each page lists only what we checked for that sport. Where we have not checked it yet, the page says so plain.
