What to do first

  • Set the class year, then get the dated calendar for this sport. Dates come from the rule, not from a guess.
  • Build a target list of programs that fit your level, your grades and your family's money.
  • Make one film a coach will finish watching. Keep the rest of your online presence clean.
  • Start contact when the rule says a coach may reply, and log every answer in one place.

What we do not do

  • We are not an agent. We do not pick your school, contact programs for you, or take a cut of your deals. Some state and school rules void eligibility when an agent does that, so we stay out of it.
  • We do not sell your contact list to colleges or to camps.
  • We do not promise an offer. We promise the dates, the rules they come from, and the tools to run the process.
