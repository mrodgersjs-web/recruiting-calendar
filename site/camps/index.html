Camp triage

Before you pay for a camp, answer four questions.

A camp is worth the money when a coach who can recruit your kid will be there and already knows their name. Otherwise it is an expensive practice.

  1. 1

    Has a coach from this school contacted your kid, or answered an email?

    If no, email first. Then decide.

  2. 2

    Will that program's own staff run or attend the camp?

    Third-party showcases often list colleges that send no one.

  3. 3

    Is your kid allowed to have recruiting conversations at camp yet?

    For D1 baseball, recruiting talk at camps opens September 1 of junior year. Check your sport's page.

  4. 4

    Does the price beat one tank of gas plus one email?

    Most first contact costs nothing.
