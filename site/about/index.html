About

A calendar built by a parent who got tired of guessing.

The Recruiting Calendar is published by Rodgers Intelligence Group in Denver. We read the NCAA manuals, turn each rule into a dated line, and record the day we checked it.

How we check

  • Primary sources only. NCAA Division I, II and III manuals and Eligibility Center documents.
  • A verified-on date on every page. If a rule changes, the date changes.
  • Projected means projected. Dates not yet on a published NCAA calendar are labeled.

We are not compliance officers, lawyers or recruiters. Questions: hello@therecruitingcalendar.example.
