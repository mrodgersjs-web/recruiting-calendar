## Patterns
- **Rule changes and scary numbers win** (#2-9). Parents share anything that says "this changed, here's what to do". We can beat these on sourcing: we cite the bylaw.
- **Moment hooks** ("Heading out on a campus visit?", "Sent one email and heard nothing?") get high saves and shares. Match each hook to a calendar date.
- **Comment-keyword lead magnet** (Comment PARENT / SCORE) drives #1. Outward use needs Mike's OK and an automation tool.
- **Anti-hype founder voice** (BaseballPath) gets 10x+ plays per follower. That is our brand voice already.
- **Weak format:** daily text-only carousels (Full Ride U, ~0-1 likes each). Volume without a hook doesn't work.
- No top-performer above names or pictures a minor *by necessity*: the parent-education hooks (#1-26) work without any athlete in them.
