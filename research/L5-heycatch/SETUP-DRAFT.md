**Product name:** The Recruiting Calendar

**One-liner:** Every date a college coach may contact your kid, for their sport and class year, in one sourced calendar.

**What it is:** A subscription site for parents of high school football, baseball, and track & cross-country athletes. It gives the NCAA recruiting rules that apply to your child's sport and graduation year as dated, sourced entries (every date cites its NCAA bylaw), roster and scholarship math, a hosted recruiting tracker, and help deciding which camps are worth the money.

**Who it is for (ICPs):**
1. Parent of a high school freshman/sophomore football, baseball, or track/XC athlete who has just realized recruiting has rules and deadlines and does not know them.
2. Parent of a junior getting first coach contact or camp invites, unsure what is allowed, what is real, and what is a paid pitch.
3. High school or club coach who fields the same rules questions from parents every season.

**Their pains in their own words (expected):** "When can coaches actually contact my kid?" "Is this camp invite real or a money grab?" "Are we already behind?" "Do recruiting services actually work?" "How many scholarships does this sport even have?"

**How we are different:** No sales call. Published price. 30-day refund. No scholarship promise. Every date cites its source.

**Competitors to watch:** paid recruiting services and profile sites (e.g. NCSA-style services), camp operators, NCAA Eligibility Center pages, free parent forums.

**Where they hang out:** Reddit (sports-parent, high school sport, track/XC, coaching communities), Threads and X sports-parent posts, Facebook parent groups.

**Good catches:** a parent or coach asking about contact dates, dead/quiet periods, official visits, camp value, scholarship counts, or whether a recruiting service is worth paying for, in football, baseball, or track/XC.

**Skip:** threads naming or showing a specific minor athlete (no stats, film, schools or handles), college-athlete NIL deals, pro-sports talk, posters selling recruiting services.

**Tone for drafted replies:** a parent who has read the rulebook. Answer the question with the rule and its source first; mention the site only if the poster asks for a tool. Never promise a scholarship or outcome. Never reference any specific child.

**Do not auto-post anything.** All replies and videos stay drafts for my approval.
