Baseball recruiting calendar, Class of 2029

Checked against the 2026-27 NCAA Division I Manual on 2026-11-05. Every date cites its bylaw. Dates marked PROJECTED follow last year's pattern and are not yet published by the NCAA. "Recommended" dates are ours, not NCAA deadlines. This is a planning aid, not legal advice and not a promise of any roster spot or scholarship.

2026-11-15Recommended (not NCAA)
Have the counselor check every course against the NCAA list
Map completed and planned classes against your school's NCAA-approved course list. Not every class counts. This is our recommended date, not an NCAA one.
If missed: Problems found in 12th grade are often too late to fix.
D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.1 · https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
2027-06-15Opens
Division II in-person contact and official visits may begin
D2 coaches may make in-person contact and host paid visits. D2 calls, texts and emails are already allowed at any time.
If missed: None for acting later. Earlier in-person contact is a violation.
D2 Bylaws 13.1.1.1, 13.3.1.2 · https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90010
2027-08-01Opens
D1 baseball coaches may start calling, texting and emailing
Baseball is different: D1 baseball opens August 1 of junior year, not the June 15 date most sports use. Before this, a coach can't answer a recruiting message, even if your kid sends it first.
If missed: None for starting later. It's an opening, not a race.
D1 Bylaws 13.1.3.1.1, 13.4.1.1 · https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
2027-08-15Recommended (not NCAA)
Open the NCAA Eligibility Center certification account
D1 and D2 recruits need a certification account ($110 domestic, fee waivers possible) before any D1 official visit. The free Profile Page isn't enough. Recommended date, not an NCAA deadline.
If missed: Without it, a school can't host you on an official visit.
D1 Bylaw 13.6.3 · https://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/eligibility_center/Student_Resources/Registration_Checklist.pdf
2027-09-01Opens
D1 baseball off-campus contact and visits may begin
Official visits, unofficial visits involving the athletics department, and off-campus contact all open September 1 of junior year. Recruiting talk at college camps opens the same day. The recruiting calendar can still close a specific week.
If missed: Visiting too early, or during a dead period, is a violation that wastes the trip.
D1 Bylaws 13.1.1.1.1, 13.6.2.1.2, 13.7.1.2 · https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
2028-01-01Opens
Division III official visits may begin
D3 paid visits open January 1 of junior year. D3 offers no athletic scholarships; aid there is academic and need-based.
If missed: None for visiting later.
D3 Bylaw 13.6.1.1.1 · https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90011
HARD DEADLINE
Finish 10 of your 16 core courses before senior year
Division I requires 10 NCAA-approved core courses, 7 of them English, math or science, finished before the first day of the seventh semester. Those grades lock. Your school sets the exact date.
If missed: Can block qualifier status and first-year competition. Senior-year courses can't repair it.
D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.1(b) · https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
2028-11-08PROJECTED (not yet published)
Projected D1 baseball signing opens
Calculated from current rules: the second Wednesday in November. Not yet a published calendar date. We recheck it when the NCAA publishes.
If missed: No penalty for signing later, but a school's offer can expire. Signing ends recruiting by other schools.
D1 Bylaw 13.02.13.1(f) · https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
2029-04-01Window
Final amateurism certification request opens
Fall 2029 enrollees can request final amateurism certification in their Eligibility Center account from April 1. It's when the window opens, not a cutoff.
If missed: Not an automatic loss, but waiting can delay clearance to compete.
Eligibility Center procedure · https://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/eligibility_center/Student_Resources/How_to_Request_Final_Amateurism_Certification.pdf
HARD DEADLINE
Graduate and get final certification
Finish all 16 core courses with at least a 2.3 core GPA, graduate, and have the school send the final transcript to the Eligibility Center. A transcript GPA isn't the same as a core GPA.
If missed: Can delay or block first-year competition. A verbal commitment isn't clearance.
D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.3 · https://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/eligibility_center/Student_Resources/IE_Brochure.pdf
