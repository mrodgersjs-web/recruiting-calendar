# The Commit Book: Track & Field / Cross Country Parent Playbook
### Class of 2029 · built from rules read 2026-11-05 · written 2026-11-06 · playbook id `w10-track`

> Education, not legal advice. Nothing here promises an offer, aid, a roster spot or a time. We do not contact coaches or your athlete. **RULE** = a bylaw we cite. **PROJECTED** = calculated, not yet published. **RECOMMENDED** / **YOUR PLAN** = our suggestion, not an NCAA deadline. Confirm every date with each school's compliance office.

**Who runs this:** the athlete sends the messages and takes the calls. The parent is project manager: calendar, budget, folder, Sunday check-in. Coaches describe a parent speaking for the athlete as a warning sign [S35; S40].

**How to use it:** one Stage per sitting. Each ends with a **Done when** line you can check. Worksheets are Part C; the Completeness Test is Part D.

**Before a date opens.** A family may write a coach, fill a questionnaire or attend a camp at any time; the dates limit what the coach may send or say [S26; S19]. Silence before the date is the rule, not a "no."

**Academic lines (D1).** 10 of 16 core courses finished, 7 in English, math or science, before the seventh semester [S1 14.3.1.1(b)]. 16 cores, 2.300 core GPA, graduate, final transcript [S1 14.3.1.1(a)]. D2 asks 2.2 [S7]. D3: school sets its bar [S7]. NAIA: separate center; a freshman is eligible with 2.3+ GPA, or two of three: 18 ACT / 970 SAT, 2.0 GPA, top half of class [S13; S14].

**Roster reality.** D1 settlement schools: cross country 17 per gender, indoor and outdoor track 45 per gender [S9]. A cap is a ceiling, not open spots [S8]. D2 limit: 12.6 equivalencies for track and cross country combined [S11]. D3 gives no athletic aid [S7].

**What programs publish.** Track is the one sport of the three where programs publish numbers. The spread is large and labels differ (target, scholarship consideration, walk-on consideration), so lines from different programs are not like-for-like and levels overlap. Examples as published (men's 400m): Rutgers "consider for full aid" 46.20 [S22]; Eastern Michigan scholarship 47.50 [S24]; Brown walk-on consideration 48.70 [S21]; Oral Roberts target 49.6 [S23]; MIT D3 recruiting line 50.50 [S25]. Programs say the number is not the decision: Oral Roberts states that achieving the marks does not guarantee a scholarship or roster spot [S23]; Rutgers lists academics, team need and consistency at standard [S22]. Use the program's own PDF for your event, same label to same label. Third-party "D1 top / D1 low" tables are vendor composites, not program standards [W7-recruitiq 1C].

**Context, not odds.** About 5.4% of high school track athletes appear on an NCAA roster in the same year (1.9% D1, 1.5% D2, 2.0% D3). A ratio of two headcounts for 2024-25, leaving out NAIA and JUCO; not your athlete's chance [S17].

## Part B. The Stages

### Stage 0. Home base (one evening, before 2027-06-15)
**Do:**
1. One shared folder: results links, transcript, schedule, contact log (C3), target list (C2), marks tracker (C5).
2. A fixed 20-minute Sunday slot. Athlete reads the log; parent asks "what is due this week?"
3. Write a spend ceiling in C1 before any camp or service. Parents report the most regret over paid services [S40].
**Done when:** folder exists, Sunday slot is on both calendars, C1 has a dollar figure.

### Stage 1. Lock the academics (now; recheck each semester)
**Do:**
1. Counselor checks every course against the school's NCAA core list. RECOMMENDED first check 2026-11-15 [S1 14.3.1.1].
2. Fill C4. Ask the counselor the first day of the seventh semester.
3. Open the Eligibility Center certification account, RECOMMENDED 2027-08-15 (110 USD domestic fee, waivers possible); needed before a D1 official visit [S4; S1 13.6.3]. NAIA on the list: register at PlayNAIA.org [S14].
**Done when:** C4 shows 10/7 reachable and the account number is in the folder. If not, book the counselor this week and ask in writing for the course plan that gets there.

### Stage 2. Make every mark verifiable (spring 2027, YOUR PLAN)
**Why:** coaches in this sport weigh verified marks, a steady improvement curve, consistency (second-best near the PR) and state-meet place. That is one D1 coach's published view, not a rule [S35].
**Do:**
1. Confirm every meet posts to a public results site. Record each mark in C5 with meet, date, wind/conditions and the results link.
2. Note fully automatic (electronic) timing where it applies; Rutgers states it accepts FAT timing only [S22].
3. Add a progression line per event (9th, 10th, now).
**Done when:** every mark in C5 has a link that opens for a stranger.

### Stage 3. Target list from published marks (before 2027-06-15)
**Do:**
1. C2: 30 to 50 programs across D1, D2, D3, NAIA and JUCO. Every level is a destination.
2. For each program, look for its own recruiting-standards PDF (many track programs post one) [S21-S25]. Copy the program's own label and number for your event. If it has none, use last season's conference-final mark and the roster's best mark in your event, and write "no published standard."
3. Tier by your PR against that program's line: Reach (below the scorers or the line), Fit (within range), Sure (you would score now). Compare same label to same label only.
4. Ask the high school coach for an honest level read and a reference (E4).
**Done when:** C2 has 30 rows, each with a published line or "no published standard," and the date you read it.

### Stage 4. First contact (RULE 2027-06-15)
**Do:**
1. Send 5 messages the first week, 5 more weekly (E1). Marks and results link in the body.
2. Log each send in C3 the same day.
3. Follow up once at 14 days with a new mark or meet (E2). After two unanswered messages, move on [S35].
4. Answer any coach reply within 48 hours (RECOMMENDED).
5. Read replies with C7. A questionnaire or camp invite is data going to the program, not an offer [S38].
**Done when:** 20 messages logged, no reply unanswered, no follow-up more than 3 days overdue.

### Stage 5. Meets, camps, visits (2027-08-01 onward)
**Do:**
1. Score any paid camp on C8 before paying. Skip under threshold or over the C1 ceiling.
2. Check the NCAA track and cross country recruiting calendar for dead and quiet periods before booking a trip [S19]. No lane has read it for you.
3. Before an official visit: certification account open, transcript at the school, athlete on the visit list [S1 13.6.3]. Score the visit that night (C9).
**Done when:** every camp has a C8 row, spend is under C1, every visit has a C9 score within 24 hours.

### Stage 6. Offers, comparison, signing (2028-08-01 onward)
**Do:**
1. A verbal offer binds no one. A written D1 aid offer cannot come before 2028-08-01 [S1 13.9.3.1].
2. Track aid is often split across many athletes; programs publish ranges (for example "1% to 100%") [S24]. Ask for the exact dollar amount and years in writing; enter it in C10.
3. Ask compliance in writing: one year or multiple, and what is in writing about roster status. For opted-in D1 schools aid cannot be revoked for roster management, performance or injury unless the athlete chooses to transfer [S8]; ask what applies here.
4. Signing ends other schools' recruiting. Sign only when C10 is complete.
5. Request final amateurism certification from 2029-04-01 [S5].
**Done when:** C10 has no blank cell for the offer being accepted, and athlete and parent chose together.

## Part E. Scripts (athlete's voice)

**E1. First email.** Subject: `2029 | [Event] [PR] ([meet, date]) | [School], [ST]`
> Coach [Last name], I'm [Name], Class of 2029 at [School]. [Event]: PR [mark] at [meet, date], results: [link]. Progression: [9th] to [10th] to [now]. Next meets: [list]. GPA [x.x]. I would like to know what you look for in your [event group]. [Name], [phone].

**E2. Follow-up (14 days).**
> Coach [Last name], new mark: [event, mark, meet, date], results: [link]. Still interested in [School]. [Name]

**E3. After a camp or meet where you saw the staff (48 hours).**
> Coach [Last name], thank you for [camp/meet, date]. My results are here: [link]. [Name]

**E4. Asking the high school coach.**
> Coach [Last name], would you give me an honest read on the level you see for me in my event, and speak to a college coach if one calls? My list: [link]. Thank you, [Name]
