# The Commit Book: Baseball Parent Playbook
### Class of 2029 · built from rules read 2026-11-05 · written 2026-11-06 · playbook id `w10-baseball`

> Education, not legal advice. Nothing here promises an offer, aid, a roster spot or playing time. We do not contact coaches or your athlete. **RULE** = a bylaw we cite. **PROJECTED** = calculated from current bylaws, not yet published. **RECOMMENDED** / **YOUR PLAN** = our suggestion, not an NCAA deadline. Confirm every date with each school's compliance office.

**Who runs this:** the athlete sends the messages and takes the calls. The parent is project manager: calendar, budget, folder, Sunday check-in. Coaches describe a parent speaking for the athlete as a warning sign [S35; S40].

**How to use it:** one Stage per sitting. Each ends with a **Done when** line you can check. Worksheets are Part C; the Completeness Test is Part D.

## Part A. The picture in one page

Baseball runs on a **later clock** than most D1 sports. Do not use a June 15 date you saw for football or lacrosse.

**Before a date opens.** A family may write a coach, fill a questionnaire or attend a camp at any time; the dates limit what the coach may send or say [S26; S19]. A coach who cannot answer yet is following the rule, not rejecting you.

**Academic lines (D1).** 10 of 16 core courses finished, 7 in English, math or science, before the seventh semester [S1 14.3.1.1(b)]. 16 cores, 2.300 core GPA, graduate, final transcript [S1 14.3.1.1(a)]. D2 asks 2.2 core GPA [S7]. D3: the school sets its own bar [S7]. NAIA is a separate center; a freshman is eligible with a 2.3+ high school GPA, or two of three: 18 ACT / 970 SAT, 2.0 GPA, top half of class [S13; S14].

**Roster reality.** D1 settlement schools are capped at 34 baseball players [S9]. A cap is a ceiling on team size, not a count of open spots. Under the old model baseball had 11.7 equivalencies across at most 27 aided players; whether an opted-in school now aids more is a budget decision families cannot see [S8; S9]. D2 limit: 9.0 equivalencies [S11]. D3 gives no athletic aid [S7].

**Numbers and honesty.** No program or governing body we found publishes numeric baseball standards [S26; S27], and vendors disagree widely on D1 pitcher velocity. So this playbook never states a "D1 number." It shows how to get verified numbers and how to ask programs what they want.

**Context, not odds.** About 8.8% of high school baseball players appear on an NCAA roster in the same year (2.7% D1, 2.6% D2, 3.5% D3). That is a ratio of two headcounts for 2024-25, it leaves out NAIA and JUCO, and it is not your athlete's chance [S17].

## Part B. The Stages

### Stage 0. Home base (one evening, before 2027-08-01)
**Do:**
1. One shared folder: video link, transcript, schedule, showcase results, contact log (C3), target list (C2).
2. A fixed 20-minute Sunday slot. Athlete reads the log; parent asks "what is due this week?"
3. Write a season spend ceiling in C1 before choosing showcases. Parents report most regret over third-party "college coaches" showcases and paid services [S40].
**Done when:** folder exists, Sunday slot is on both calendars, C1 has a dollar figure.

### Stage 1. Lock the academics (now; recheck each semester)
**Do:**
1. Counselor checks every course against the school's NCAA core list. RECOMMENDED first check 2026-11-15 [S1 14.3.1.1].
2. Fill C4. Ask the counselor the date of the seventh semester's first day.
3. Open the Eligibility Center certification account, RECOMMENDED 2027-08-15 (110 USD domestic fee, waivers possible); needed before a D1 official visit [S4; S1 13.6.3]. If NAIA is on the list, register at PlayNAIA.org [S14].
**Done when:** C4 shows 10/7 reachable and the account number is in the folder. If not, book the counselor this week and ask in writing for the course plan that gets there.

### Stage 2. Get verified numbers (spring and summer 2027, YOUR PLAN)
**Why:** with no published standard, a coach must trust your numbers, and neutral-source numbers carry more weight than self-reported ones [S33; S34].
**Do:**
1. Pick one verified showcase for the season using the score sheet C8. Perfect Game says it records the 60-yard time and 10-yard split for every attendee [S34]. Ask any event for last year's list of college coaches present. No list, lower score.
2. Get pop time (catchers) or velocity measured by a neutral source and written down with date and event [S33].
3. Enter each number in C5 with date and who measured it.
**Done when:** every number in C5 has a date and a measurer. None is "self-timed."

### Stage 3. Video and profile (before 2027-08-01)
**Why:** video is the parents' main technical worry [S40]. Coaches search recruits online [S28; S29]. The measured link between posting film and offers is in football only, so treat it as suggestive for baseball [S28].
**Do:**
1. Cut a skills video of 5 minutes or less, best clips first [S33; S31]. Info card first: name, class, position, bats/throws, height/weight, school, club, contact, GPA, head coach contact.
2. Host it on a public link and test it in a private window. YouTube "unlisted" works with the link; "private" does not [S36; S37].
3. Run the social audit (C6).
**Done when:** a stranger can open the link on a phone, C5 fits one page, C6 has no open items.

### Stage 4. Target list (before 2027-08-01)
**Do:**
1. C2: 30 to 50 programs across D1, D2, D3, NAIA and JUCO. Every level is a destination.
2. For each: roster size, seniors leaving at the athlete's position, coach email from the official staff page, cost after likely aid, academic fit.
3. Tier them Reach, Fit, Sure.
4. Ask the high school or club coach for an honest level read and a reference (Script E4). If the coach will not help, ask a coach at a camp.
**Done when:** C2 has 30 rows, each filled or marked "unknown."

### Stage 5. First contact
**Timing:** D2, D3 and NAIA schools can hear from you now and may answer now [S2; S15]. D1 coaches may reply from 2027-08-01 [S1 13.1.3.1.1].
**Do:**
1. Send D2, D3 and NAIA messages first, 5 a week (E1). From 2027-08-01 add D1.
2. Log each send in C3 the same day.
3. Follow up once at 14 days with a new number or clip (E2). After two unanswered messages, move on [S35].
4. Answer any coach reply within 48 hours (RECOMMENDED).
5. Read replies with C7. A questionnaire or camp invite is interest data going to the program, not an offer [S38].
**Done when:** 20 messages are logged, no reply is unanswered, no follow-up is more than 3 days overdue.

### Stage 6. Showcases, camps, visits (2027-09-01 onward)
**Do:**
1. Score every event on C8 before paying. Skip anything under your threshold or over the C1 ceiling.
2. At a college's own camp before 2027-09-01, staff cannot talk recruiting with you there; it is still an evaluation [S1 13.12.1.5.2].
3. Check the NCAA D1 baseball recruiting calendar for dead and quiet periods before booking any trip [S20]. No lane has read that calendar for you.
4. Before an official visit: certification account open, transcript at the school, athlete on the visit list [S1 13.6.3]. Score the visit that night (C10).
**Done when:** every event has a C8 row, total spend is under C1, every visit has a C10 score dated within 24 hours.

### Stage 7. Offers, comparison, signing (2028-08-01 onward)
**Do:**
1. A verbal offer binds no one. A written D1 aid offer cannot come before 2028-08-01 [S1 13.9.3.1].
2. Baseball aid is often partial. Ask for the exact dollar amount and duration in writing and enter it in C11.
3. Ask compliance in writing: one year or multiple, and what is in writing about roster status. For opted-in D1 schools the adopted rule says aid cannot be revoked for roster management, performance or injury unless the athlete chooses to transfer [S8]; ask what applies at this school.
4. Signing ends other schools' recruiting. Sign only when C11 is complete.
5. Request final amateurism certification from 2029-04-01 [S5].
**Done when:** C11 has no blank cell for the offer being accepted, and athlete and parent chose together.

## Part E. Scripts (athlete's voice)

**E1. First email.** Subject: `2029 [Pos] | [B/T] | [Verified number + event] | [HS/Club] | Video`
> Coach [Last name], I'm [Name], Class of 2029 [position] at [School], playing for [club]. Verified: [number, event, date]. Skills video: [public link]. Summer schedule: [link]. GPA [x.x]. I would like to know what you look for at [position]. [Name], [phone]. High school coach: [name, contact].

**E2. Follow-up (14 days).**
> Coach [Last name], new [number or clip] from [event/date]: [link]. Still interested in [School]. Schedule: [link]. [Name]

**E3. After a showcase or camp (48 hours).**
> Coach [Last name], thank you for [event, date]. My numbers from it are [list, source]. Updated video: [link]. [Name]

**E4. Asking the high school or club coach.**
> Coach [Last name], would you give me an honest read on the level you see for me, and speak to a college coach if one calls? My list: [link]. Thank you, [Name]
