NAIAno recruiting calendar and no limit on communication[S15]

Before a date opens. A family may write a coach, fill a questionnaire or attend a camp at any time; the dates limit what the coach may send or say [S26; S19]. Silence before the date is the rule, not a "no."

Academic lines (D1). 10 of 16 core courses finished, 7 in English, math or science, before the seventh semester [S1 14.3.1.1(b)]. 16 cores, 2.300 core GPA, graduate, final transcript [S1 14.3.1.1(a)]. D2 asks 2.2 [S7]. D3: school sets its bar [S7]. NAIA: separate center; a freshman is eligible with 2.3+ GPA, or two of three: 18 ACT / 970 SAT, 2.0 GPA, top half of class [S13; S14].

Roster reality. D1 settlement schools: cross country 17 per gender, indoor and outdoor track 45 per gender [S9]. A cap is a ceiling, not open spots [S8]. D2 limit: 12.6 equivalencies for track and cross country combined [S11]. D3 gives no athletic aid [S7].

What programs publish. Track is the one sport of the three where programs publish numbers. The spread is large and labels differ (target, scholarship consideration, walk-on consideration), so lines from different programs are not like-for-like and levels overlap. Examples as published (men's 400m): Rutgers "consider for full aid" 46.20 [S22]; Eastern Michigan scholarship 47.50 [S24]; Brown walk-on consideration 48.70 [S21]; Oral Roberts target 49.6 [S23]; MIT D3 recruiting line 50.50 [S25]. Programs say the number is not the decision: Oral Roberts states that achieving the marks does not guarantee a scholarship or roster spot [S23]; Rutgers lists academics, team need and consistency at standard [S22]. Use the program's own PDF for your event, same label to same label. Third-party "D1 top / D1 low" tables are vendor composites, not program standards [W7-recruitiq 1C].

Context, not odds. About 5.4% of high school track athletes appear on an NCAA roster in the same year (1.9% D1, 1.5% D2, 2.0% D3). A ratio of two headcounts for 2024-25, leaving out NAIA and JUCO; not your athlete's chance [S17].

Dated calendar (each row cites its bylaw)

2026-11-15Recommended (not NCAA)
Have the counselor check every course against the NCAA list
Map completed and planned classes against your school's NCAA-approved course list. Not every class counts. This is our recommended date, not an NCAA one.
If missed: Problems found in 12th grade are often too late to fix.
D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.1 · https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
2027-06-15Opens
Calls, messages and off-campus contact open together
Changed October 7, 2026: track and cross country off-campus contact now opens June 15 after sophomore year, the same day as calls and texts. Older guides still say August 1.
If missed: None for starting later.
D1 Bylaws 13.1.1.1.9, 13.1.3.1, 13.4.1 · https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
2027-08-01Opens
Official and unofficial visits may begin
August 1 of junior year for official visits and unofficial visits involving the athletics department.
If missed: Visiting too early is a violation.
D1 Bylaws 13.6.2.1.1, 13.7.1.1 · https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
2027-08-15Recommended (not NCAA)
Open the NCAA Eligibility Center certification account
D1 and D2 recruits need a certification account ($110 domestic, fee waivers possible) before any D1 official visit. The free Profile Page isn't enough. Recommended date, not an NCAA deadline.
If missed: Without it, a school can't host you on an official visit.
D1 Bylaw 13.6.3 · https://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/eligibility_center/Student_Resources/Registration_Checklist.pdf
2028-08-01Opens
Written scholarship offers may arrive
A written athletics aid offer can't come before August 1 of senior year.
If missed: None. Compare any offer against the full net price.
D1 Bylaw 13.9.3.1 · https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
HARD DEADLINE
Finish 10 of your 16 core courses before senior year
Division I requires 10 NCAA-approved core courses, 7 of them English, math or science, finished before the first day of the seventh semester. Those grades lock. Your school sets the exact date.
If missed: Can block qualifier status and first-year competition. Senior-year courses can't repair it.
D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.1(b) · https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
2028-11-08PROJECTED (not yet published)
Projected signing opens
Second Wednesday in November, calculated from current rules. Not yet published.
If missed: No penalty for signing later.
D1 Bylaw 13.02.13.1(f) · https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
2029-04-01Window
Final amateurism certification request opens
Fall 2029 enrollees can request final amateurism certification in their Eligibility Center account from April 1. It's when the window opens, not a cutoff.
If missed: Not an automatic loss, but waiting can delay clearance to compete.
Eligibility Center procedure · https://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/eligibility_center/Student_Resources/How_to_Request_Final_Amateurism_Certification.pdf
HARD DEADLINE
Graduate and get final certification
Finish all 16 core courses with at least a 2.3 core GPA, graduate, and have the school send the final transcript to the Eligibility Center. A transcript GPA isn't the same as a core GPA.
If missed: Can delay or block first-year competition. A verbal commitment isn't clearance.
D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.3 · https://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/eligibility_center/Student_Resources/IE_Brochure.pdf
