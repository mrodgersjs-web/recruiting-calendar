# 03 - Men's basketball outreach research (class of 2029; D1, D2, D3, NAIA)
Prepared 11 Nov 2026. "Accessed" = 2026-11-11 for every URL below. Reader: boy, class of 2029, 10th grade now.

Source tiers: COACH = coach said it publicly (anonymous ones flagged). SERVICE = company/data site publishes it, method often unpublished. DATA = counted from official rosters or NCAA documents. FOLKLORE = repeated rule of thumb with no source. UNCONFIRMED = not sourced; the source type that would settle it is named.

## 0. Biggest caveat
No public source gives wingspan, hand size, 3/4-court sprint, lane agility or vertical norms for D1/D2/D3/NAIA recruits or rosters. The only sourced testing numbers are NBA Draft Combine averages (pro prospects). Any "college norm" for those tests is UNCONFIRMED. Settling source: a published college or showcase combine dataset with sample sizes (school strength-staff test sheets, G League Elite Camp, a named showcase's results).

Derived by me from the table: wingspan minus height is about +3.75" (PG), +3.75" (SG), +3.0" (SF), +4.25" (PF), +5.25" (C). A teen with wingspan at or above height plus 3" is "long"; this is arithmetic, not a coach rule.
Cross-check: Kel'el Ware 2024 combine, hand 9.5 x 10.25, wingspan 7'4.5", lane 10.97, 3/4 sprint 3.29, max vert 36 (Inside the Hall, 14 May 2024) https://www.insidethehall.com/2024/05/14/roundup-kelel-ware-measurements-interview-at-nba-draft-combine
Warning: PoinT GO says "college and high school benchmarks are typically 10-20% lower" with no source: FOLKLORE. https://research.poin-t-go.com/en/sports/basketball/basketball-combine-testing
Protocol note (PMC, Cui et al. 2019): combine height is WITHOUT shoes, so "barefoot" is the standard; the study found drafted players beat undrafted ones in height, wingspan, verticals, lane agility and 3/4 sprint. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6820507 A second paper says combine tests mostly do NOT predict draft position (exceptions: hand width and height for frontcourt; verticals for backcourt). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7601684

## 2. Stats coaches weight, by position

### 2a. Position thresholds (SERVICE, no method published, folklore-grade)
Go Big Recruiting position pages (URLs in 1b), accessed 2026-11-11:
- PG: elite D1 10+ pts, 6-8 ast. D1 8-12 pts, 6 ast. Lower D1/D2/NAIA 8-10 pts, 4-6 ast. D3 8-12 pts, 6 ast.
- SG: elite 15+ pts. D1 12-15+ pts. D2/NAIA 10-12+ pts. D3 10-12+ pts.
- SF: elite 10-12+ pts, 8+ reb. D1, D2/NAIA, D3 all 10+ pts.
- PF: elite 10+ pts, 8-10 reb, 1-2 blk. D1 10+ pts, 6-8 reb. D2/NAIA and D3: "10+ games good overall statistics" (no number).
- C: elite D1 12-15+ pts, 8-10+ reb, 1-3 blk. D2/NAIA and D3: no number.
Reliability: PG rows do not step down by level (D3 asks 6 ast, D2 asks 4-6), so these are not measured thresholds. No sourced threshold exists for assist-to-turnover, FG%, 3P%, FT%, steals, blocks, minutes or usage at any division: UNCONFIRMED. Settling source: HS stat lines (MaxPreps / school sites) of signed D1, D2, D3, NAIA class-of-2025 and 2026 signees, n>=30 per cell.

### 2b. What coaches say about stats
- SERVICE (NCAA Scholarship Guide, 4 Oct 2026): "College coaches don't recruit points per game"; body-fits-position is the first filter. https://www.ncaascholarshipguide.com/blog/mens-basketball-grade-by-grade-height-benchmarks
- COACH (anonymous D1 assistant, American Athletic Conference, via The Basketball Embassy, 24 Mar 2020): not interested in "a 6A player drop 25 points on a 2A team"; would rather see four or five quality shots against the best team in the district. https://www.thebasketballembassy.org/posts/2020/3/24/everything-college-coaches-are-looking-for-in-a-recruits-game-film
- COACH (Tad Boyle, Colorado HC, Denver Post, 3 Jan 2015): "absolute fanatic about assist-to-turnover ratio" (about his team, not a recruit cutoff; snippet only). https://www.denverpost.com/2015/01/03/to-colorado-coach-tad-boyle-its-about-assist-to-turnover-ratio/
- COACH (HS coach Kevin Furtado, Hudl, 19 Apr 2024): A/TO is "the number one stat for my guards"; a guard averaging 5+ turnovers will see limited minutes. https://www.hudl.com/blog/evaluating-the-effectiveness-of-guards-with-hudl-assist
- SERVICE (BVN Boys Basketball, 29 Sep 2026): 5-for-8 from three in two games does not make a shooter; give a season of attempts; do not invent metrics the team does not track; give role and league context. https://www.bvnboysbasketball.com/what-stats-matter-college-basketball-recruiting.html
- COACH (Tom Brown, former HC/assistant): HS stats "matter far less in recruiting rooms than most families realize"; coaches ask "Does this skill translate? Does this body hold up? Who can he guard?" (URL in 1e.)

### 2c. Efficiency numbers a HS player can honestly have
Only if the team or MaxPreps/Hudl tracks them: eFG% = (FGM + 0.5 x 3PM) / FGA; assist-to-turnover ratio; turnover % (Hudl Assist computes it) https://www.hudl.com/blog/how-hudl-assist-delivers-basketball-coaches-game-defining-insights . Print attempts with every percentage (example: 38% on 27 of 71, 18 games). PER or usage: no source says college coaches ask for them at HS level - UNCONFIRMED.

## 3. Team record, rankings, awards, "varsity as a freshman"
- Awards: SERVICE/ex-coach (TSA Recruiting, 24 May 2023): all-state and all-conference "mean next to nothing" because they are relative. https://tsarecruiting.com/individual-awards-mean-nothing-for-college-recruiting/ NCSA and AllAmerican Bowl list all-state/all-region inside a D1 profile (SERVICE profile, not a coach rule). https://www.ncsasports.org/mens-basketball/recruiting-guidelines
- Rankings: SERVICE (Scorability, 29 Apr 2026): rankings do not matter for most athletes. https://scorability.com/recruiting/how-college-recruiting-actually-works COACH: a page quoting "Self" says "we really don't care what others think... we know better what fits for us" (first name not in snippet - UNCONFIRMED it is Bill Self). https://kiwihoopers.com/evaluate-recruits-beyond-the-star-rankings Whether any national ranking of the class of 2029 exists: UNCONFIRMED (settle with 247Sports / ESPN / Rivals class pages).
- Team record: no source found that coaches weight it; context only: UNCONFIRMED. Settling source: recruiting-coordinator interviews. What IS sourced: name the league and opponent level (BVN above).
- "Varsity as a freshman": only SERVICE profiles ("varsity starter 4 years" for D1: NCSA, AllAmerican Bowl https://www.allamerican-bowl.com/post/how-to-get-recruited-to-play-men-s-college-basketball). No coach statement found. A claim without a roster or game-log link = noise. State it as a fact with a link (school roster, MaxPreps, Hudl).
- Noise (sourced): relative awards; star rankings; giant stat tables (BVN); free throws, warm-ups and dunk-contest clips on film (anonymous AAC assistant); a big number against weak opposition.

## 5. First email

### 5a. Timing facts that change the plan
- D1 men's basketball: coaches may call, email or text starting June 15 after the sophomore year (NCAA.org, 26 May 2026). For the class of 2029 that is June 15, 2027. https://www.ncaa.org/eligibility-center/recruiting/basketball-certification/june-scholastic-basketball-events
- CONFLICT: the NCAA D1 MBB recruiting-process PDF (2022-23) says recruits may call coaches anytime but "may not communicate with coaches in any other way (such as text message or email)" before then; SportsRecruits (26 Oct 2026) says contact-period limits bind coaches, not recruits. Verify with NCAA compliance before a D1 cold email before June 2027. https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/compliance/recruiting/calendar/2022-23/2022-23D1REC_MBBRecruitingProcess.pdf and https://sportsrecruits.com/resources/contacting-college-coaches/ncaa-contact-periods
- Girls' D1 basketball starts June 1 after sophomore year (SportsRecruits), boys June 15: the shared deck must not use one date for both.
- NAIA: no contact restrictions (IMG Academy, 14 Jul 2025). https://www.imgacademy.com/resources/articles/how-to-contact-college-coaches-to-get-recruited D3 off-campus contact after sophomore year (thenil.app compilation, weaker source). D2 men's basketball rule: UNCONFIRMED (settle with NCAA D2 manual).

### 5b. Field list (my recommendation, built from the sources above)
Subject, 50-75 characters (NCSA https://www.ncsasports.org/recruiting/contacting-college-coaches/email): Name | 2029 position | height | school, ST | Film. Example: Alex Smith | 2029 PG | 6'1" | Geneva HS, IL | Film
Body, in order:
1. Greeting by the coach's correct name; use the recruiting coordinator if listed.
2. Name, class 2029, position, barefoot height, weight, school, city/state, AAU club.
3. Level and role: varsity/JV, league or classification, starter or rotation.
4. Two or three stats with games and attempts, labelled by season.
5. Academics: GPA with scale (unweighted), intended major; test scores only if taken.
6. One highlight link and one full-game link (Hudl or YouTube, public).
7. Upcoming schedule: dates, gym, jersey number and color.
8. HS coach and AAU coach email/phone.
9. One sentence specific to this program (a real fact).
10. One ask: "Would you watch the film and tell me what you need to see?"
11. Player email and phone; parent contact only if the coach asks.
Optional only if third-party measured: wingspan, vertical.
Leave out: home address, DOB, SSN; 40 or sprint times unless timed by a named tester; award lists; star or "ranked first" claims without a linked ranking; more than three stats; multiple video links; attachments; adjectives ("elite," "dominant"); dream-school praise; scholarship questions; weighted GPA without scale.

### 5c. Critique of the girls' deck model email (format check only)
Quoted: "Subject: Madison Weber, 2029 PG, Geneva Township, 14.2 GPA, 22 points a game ... I am 5 foot 7 ... I shoot 38% from three in the league. My class of 2029 is ranked first in the state." The "..." means only fragments were seen.
- "14.2 GPA" is impossible on a 4.0, 5.0 or 100 scale. Probably a typo for 4.2 or 3.9. State the scale: "3.9 unweighted (4.0 scale)". Coaches read this as a carelessness signal.
- Subject is 67 characters (counted), inside the 50-75 range. The problem is content: no state (Geneva Township is not locatable), no height, no film flag; 22 ppg has no level or competition context.
- "5 foot 7" should be 5'7" and say barefoot, with weight.
- "38% from three in the league": no makes/attempts, no season, "the league" undefined. Use "38% (27-71), 2025-26, [conference]".
- "My class of 2029 is ranked first in the state": unverifiable, source unnamed; ambiguous between academic class rank and a recruiting ranking. Delete, or name the publication with a link (academic: "1st of 210 in class").
- Missing: film link, schedule, coach contacts, AAU team, a program-specific line, an ask.
- Timing: the girls' D1 start date (June 1) differs from boys (June 15).

## 6. Decision-changing findings
1. D1 cold email may be pointless until June 15, 2027 (rule conflict in 5a).
2. Real D1 center median is 6'11", above the service tables' 6'8"-6'10".
3. D1 and D2 guard medians are within an inch (6'4" vs 6'3"); position matters more than level.
4. A 6'0"-6'2" guard lands 24% D1 / 28% D2 / 48% D3.
5. No public wingspan, hand, sprint, agility or vertical norms below NBA level.
6. No sourced stat threshold for A/TO, FG%, 3P%, FT% at any division.
7. Service stat thresholds are folklore-grade; coaches say context and competition beat raw points.
8. Print attempts with every percentage.
9. Awards, rankings, "varsity as freshman": noise unless linked and verifiable.
10. Film: one highlight under about 4 minutes plus one or two full games; no free throws or warm-ups.
11. Unedited-quarter and music rules: UNCONFIRMED.
12. "14.2 GPA" and "ranked first in the state" are credibility hits in the girls' email.
