# 04 Women's basketball: measurables, stats, summer, academics, film, first email

Prepared 11 Nov 2026. All URLs accessed 11 Nov 2026 unless noted. Reader: US girl, class of 2029 (10th grade now), targets D1/D2/D3/NAIA women's basketball.

Evidence tiers (never mixed):
- COACH = a coach said it publicly.
- SERVICE = a recruiting service or vendor publishes it (NCSA, AASR/Go Big, StudBud, blogs). Marketing pages, mostly undated.
- OFFICIAL = NCAA/NAIA/CDC/peer-reviewed/league data.
- FOLKLORE = forum lore or a number with no traceable source.
- UNCONFIRMED = not sourced; the source type that would settle it is named.

Headline caveat: no coach in any source I retrieved published a numeric height, wingspan, sprint or vertical minimum. Every per-position number below is a SERVICE number or a derived figure and is labeled so.

Finding: at the top of the women's game guard wingspan is about equal to barefoot height (0 to +2 inches), not the +4 to +5 boys' recruiting lore teaches. Bigs sit about +3 to +5. A D1 guard does not need long arms; she needs not to be short for the level.
Status: derived from a handful of named pros in a publication. No coach publicly states a women's wingspan minimum: UNCONFIRMED. Settling source: measured combine or camp sheet (WNBA/USA Basketball tryout anthropometrics) or a staff's own roster sheet. The IX lists many women's wingspans as "N/A" (https://www.theixsports.com/features/2026-wnba-draft-board-v1), i.e. often unmeasured.

### 2b. What the level actually requires beyond stats (SERVICE, NCSA)
https://www.ncsasports.org/womens-basketball/recruiting-guidelines : PG top D1 = highest national AAU circuits (Nike EYBL, adidas Gauntlet), ESPN national ranking, four-year varsity starter; low D1/top D2/top NAIA = 3-4 years high-level AAU, 3-4 year varsity starter, All-State/Region/Conference; low D3/low NAIA = 2 years local/state club, upperclassman starter. Their D1 summary: "nationally ranked on ESPN or On3 ... best player on HS and AAU team ... typically early verbal offers (before junior year)."
Reading: the per-game stat floor (10 PPG/4 APG) is cheap to clear; the competition level is what separates D1 from D2. Stats without circuit level do not draw a D1 look. Position-specific skill the services stress: PG passing, floor leadership, ball handling; SG shooting range; bigs rebounding and running the floor.

## 3. Summer circuit reality

### 3a. The calendar that governs what staffs can watch (OFFICIAL)
NCAA D1 women's basketball 2026-27 calendar (updated 30 Jul 2026): https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/compliance/recruiting/calendar/2026-27/2026-27D1Rec_WBBRecruitingCalendar.pdf
- Oct 1, 2026 to Feb 28, 2027: evaluation period, scholastic activities only.
- Jun 17 (noon) to Jun 19 (6 pm), 2027: scholastic events approved by NCAA and NFHS.
- Jul 9-12 and Jul 23-26, 2027: evaluation, certified nonscholastic events only. These are the two July 2027 weekends her club season should target (after 10th grade).
- Apr 23-25 and May 14-16, 2027: evaluation, but only seniors/2-year PSAs; others scholastic only. NCSA summary: https://www.ncsasports.org/ncaa-eligibility-center/recruiting-rules/evaluation-period
Women's certified events run Thursday-to-Saturday in June and 8 am to 10 pm daily in July: https://www.ncaa.org/eligibility-center/recruiting/basketball-certification
Athletes at certified events must hold an active NCAA BBCS account and coaches need a USAB Gold License: https://underarmournext.com/basketball/circuit/

### 3c. What to say about summer in the email
One line, three facts: club + circuit level, the July 2027 weekends, and where the schedule lives. Example frame: "This summer I play 16U for [club] on [circuit]; we are scheduled for [event] July 9-12 and July 23-26 (schedule: [Exposure link]). I am registered in the NCAA BBCS." Also add June plans (HS team camp, your camp). Reiss asks for the schedule explicitly (2aDays link above).
Timing warning: D1 coaches may not call or send recruiting messages to her before June 1 after sophomore year (Bylaw 13.1.3.1.5 as read by NCSA, SportsRecruits, and Oriel): https://www.ncsasports.org/womens-basketball/recruiting-calendar-rules , https://sportsrecruits.com/resources/contacting-college-coaches/ncaa-contact-periods (26 Oct 2026), https://orieladmissions.com/when-can-college-coaches-contact-you/ (23 Sep 2026). Conflict: VCU compliance shows Sept 1 of junior year (undated page) https://athleticscompliance.vcu.edu/coaches--staff/recruiting , and the NCAA's June scholastic page uses June 15 as the general basketball date https://www.ncaa.org/eligibility-center/recruiting/basketball-certification/june-scholastic-basketball-events/ . Working date June 1, 2027; verify in the current D1 manual. A November email will likely get no personal reply from D1 staffs, so tell the family to expect a form reply and to write anyway; recruits may call or write coaches anytime. The email's job is to be on file when the window opens.

### 4c. What a first-contact email needs so the coach can run the academic check without a second email
(My recommendation, built on FieldLevel and Pathley guidance, SERVICE: https://www.fieldlevel.com/resources/recruiting-guide/getting-recruited , https://app.pathley.ai/recruiting-guide/womens-basketball)
1. Unweighted GPA with scale ("3.6 unweighted, 4.0 scale"); weighted only if labeled.
2. Core-course status ("8 NCAA core credits finished; on track for 10/7") and current math level.
3. Class rank or "school does not rank".
4. Test status: PSAT/SAT/ACT score, or "first attempt planned [month]", or test-optional if true.
5. Intended major.
6. NCAA Eligibility Center profile status (free), school name and city/state, counselor name and email, link to an unofficial transcript PDF.
With these the coach can check admissions fit, the 2.3/2.2 floor and the 10/7 pace from one message.

Spec to print: highlight 2:00 max, best plays first, player marked in the first clips, plain cuts; one full game linked (toughest opponent; second game if available); steady, wide, elevated camera (exact angle beyond AASR's "no zoom" is UNCONFIRMED as a coach statement); link only, no attachment, no login; Hudl or YouTube unlisted, tested in an incognito window.
Music: no source I retrieved says music helps or hurts. Coaches explicitly dislike special effects and slow motion. Treat music as UNCONFIRMED; default to none or very low. Settling source: a coach interview that addresses audio.

## 6. First-email field list, and the critique of the printed model

### 6a. Recommended fields (in order)
1. Subject: Name | 2029 | position | school/club | state
2. Greeting by coach last name; never "Dear Coach" blast.
3. One program-specific sentence (a real reason: style, major, roster need). Reiss: no blast emails.
4. Identity: school, city/state, club and circuit level, class, position.
5. Size: barefoot height; weight optional; wingspan only if measured.
6. Last-season line: per-game stats plus the competition level; shooting splits with attempts.
7. Academics: the six items in 4c.
8. Summer 2027 plan and schedule link (3c).
9. Film: one highlight link (2:00) and one full-game link.
10. Contacts: HS coach and club coach name/email/phone (coaches verify), player and parent email.
11. Close with a question that invites a reply (camp, questionnaire), thanks.

### 6b. Leave out
Home address, birth date, ID numbers, her cell number (give a parent email); attachments; unmeasured size claims; rankings with no link ("ranked first in the state"); other offers or "committed" talk; scholarship asks; praise with no specifics; sprint/vertical/hand size with no named measurer; piles of social handles; a GPA on a made-up scale.

### 6c. Critique of the printed model
Printed: "Subject: Madison Weber, 2029 PG, Geneva Township, 14.2 GPA, 22 points a game ... I am 5 foot 7, I run the team, and I shoot 38% from three in the league. My class of 2029 is ranked first in the state."
- GPA 14.2: impossible. Unweighted GPA tops out at 4.0 and weighted systems usually near 5.0. It reads as a typo or a fake and makes a coach doubt every other number. No scale is given, and it is in the subject line.
- Size: 5'7" alone is believable. It is about the 90th to 95th percentile of all 15-year-old girls (CDC 75th 5'5.4", 95th 5'7.9"): https://www.lifemeasure.com/height-charts/girls/15-years-old and matches the PG floor services print (5'7" low D1/high D2, 5'8" high D1). The problem is the framing: no "barefoot", no wingspan, and "I run the team" is a claim, not a stat. Replacement: "5'6" barefoot, wingspan 5'6", measured by my club coach".
- Stat line: 22 PPG is statewide-leader territory. Wisconsin's 2025-26 top-guard list (Bound/WIAA via SI, 24 Feb 2026) has seniors Natalie Kussow 26.4, juniors Jada Seubert 24.7 and EllaMay Cooper 24.1, and its sophomores at 13.5 to 18.0 PPG with 1.9-3.3 APG (Quinn Lodes 18.0, Peyton Cochenet 16.8, Kinsley Ketterhagen 16.1, Brooklyn Luehring 13.5): https://www.si.com/high-school/wisconsin/vote-who-is-wisconsin-high-school-girls-basketball-s-top-guard-of-2025-26-01kj6023yq5h . Those are the state's best; a contributor who is not a statewide recruit scores about 7-11 PPG as a sophomore (my estimate, not a sourced figure).
- 38% from three "in the league": no attempts and no sample; 38% on 21 attempts is noise. League stats are not game film.
- "Ranked first in the state": no linkable first-in-state ranking exists for a 2029 sophomore at this point that I could find: UNCONFIRMED; a named ranking site would settle it. If she means class rank, say "class rank" with the number and a transcript link.
- "Geneva Township": Wisconsin uses "town" for rural municipalities; "township" is Illinois/Michigan usage. UNCONFIRMED whether a Wisconsin municipality uses this name. Use the high school's name and city.
Believable replacement for a Wisconsin 10th-grade PG contributor (illustrative; weights and every number below must be replaced with the real player's):
"Subject: Madison Weber | 2029 PG | [High School], WI | [Club] 16U"
"I am 5'6" barefoot, 128 lb, wingspan 5'6" (measured 3 Nov 2026 by my club coach). As a freshman I averaged 8.9 points, 3.6 assists, 2.8 turnovers and 1.9 steals in 22 minutes a game for [school] (14-9); I shot 33% from three on 61 attempts and 71% at the line. My unweighted GPA is 3.6 (4.0 scale); I have 8 NCAA core credits finished, I take Algebra II, and I plan to study nursing. This July I play 16U for [club] at [events/dates]; schedule: [link]. Highlight (1:50): [link]. Full game vs [opponent]: [link]. My HS coach is [name, email]."
Ranges to use: unweighted GPA 3.4-3.8; points 7-11; assists 2.5-4; threes 30-35% on 40+ attempts; free throws 65-75%; steals 1.5-2.5. These ranges are my estimate anchored to the Wisconsin SI list and the AASR floors, not a sourced standard.
