# 06 - Email form and etiquette (first email, football and basketball)

Prepared 11 Nov 2026. All "accessed" dates are 11 Nov 2026. Lane: FORM and etiquette, not stats.

Labels:
- [COACH] a named coach or staffer said it. [COACH-VOICE?] page says a coach wrote it but identity is unchecked or the page sells an email tool.
- [SERVICE] a recruiting service, school or vendor publishes it. [FOLKLORE] unnamed sources, no method, SEO-farm or message-board.
- UNCONFIRMED = no source found; the line names the source type that would settle it.
Much search-ranked advice is vendor-written. Nothing below is a controlled test.

## 1. What coaches say publicly

Volume and reading (accessed 11 Nov 2026):
- [COACH] Charles Walker, UMass recruiting coordinator/LB coach, SB Nation, 30 Jan 2017: "I got 75 emails that I might have to skim through ... 300 emails by the end of the week, so I'd randomly check one or a couple just based off measurables." He also describes the winning pitch as one line like "6'5, 230, runs a 4.7, plays defensive end." https://www.sbnation.com/college-football-recruiting/2017/1/30/14421904/college-football-coaches-scouting-ranking-recruits-players
- [COACH-VOICE?] Tom Fabian, Thirty Second Timeout (says he is a college assistant coach; page sells an email tool), 21 Sep 2026, basketball: "Most of them get deleted in about four seconds"; staff gets "a handful ... to a few hundred" a week; "We scan." https://thirtysecondtimeout.com/Emailer
- [COACH-VOICE?] Football Scholarship Podcast, 30 Oct 2026, D2 "Coach Parker": host says coaches read "50 emails a day"; "I'm a hard worker" is laughed at, proof is not. https://gonextplay.com/post/d2-college-coach-shares-how-to-get-recruited-for-college-football
- [COACH-VOICE?] Premier Athletes, former D1 baseball coach, 24 Oct 2025: 30 to 40 recruit emails a day; copy-paste is easy to spot. https://www.premierathletes.co/articles/why-coaches-delete-your-emails-and-how-to-fix-it
- [COACH] UNA/UAB volleyball coach (speaker not named in excerpt), Better Together podcast, undated: 20 to 30 a day, 100+ before a qualifier; fall is slower. Wrong sport, weak match. https://www.podscan.fm/podcasts/better-together-2/episodes/the-recruiting-playbook-what-college-coaches-want-you-to-know
- [SERVICE] NextCommit own data: about 46% of athlete emails opened (tracking pixel, so inflated by Apple Mail prefetch). https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/coach-email-response-rates-2026
- [FOLKLORE] pulserevops.com, 7 Oct 2026: "200 to 1,000 unread a week", an unlinked "Big Ten OC told On3". Not checkable. https://pulserevops.com/knowledge/q11072
Verdict: coaches read, but skim in seconds. No reliable weekly count for football or basketball staffs exists in public; named evidence says roughly 20 to 100 a day, or 75 to 300 a week (Walker, 2017).

Length: under about 200 words (Fabian [COACH-VOICE?]; college-coaches.com [SERVICE] https://www.college-coaches.com/guides/how-to-contact-college-coaches); "5 paragraphs max" (SCOUT, 26 Mar 2026 [SERVICE] https://app.yourfirstscholarship.com/blog/how-to-email-college-football-coaches-complete-guide-2026). No source measured length against reply rate: UNCONFIRMED; a vendor with send logs could settle it. Playbook rule: 120 to 180 words, one phone screen.

Subject line: a label, not a greeting.
- Fabian example: `2027 | 6'3" Guard | Wilmington, DE | 3.8 GPA`. "Interested in your program" "tells us nothing". [COACH-VOICE?] (Fabian URL above)
- 50 to 75 characters, mobile cuts off longer: NCSA [SERVICE] https://www.ncsasports.org/recruiting/contacting-college-coaches/email ; under 75: IMG Academy, 21 Jul 2025 [SERVICE] https://plus.imgacademy.com/resources/articles/emailing-college-coaches-how-to-stand-out ; under ten words: QB Factory, 6 Nov 2026 [SERVICE] https://qbfactory.com/2026/11/06/how-to-email-college-football-coaches-for-recruiting/
- Hype subjects ("Future Star", "Must-See Athlete") may be ignored or filtered: FieldLevel [SERVICE] https://www.fieldlevel.com/resources/recruiting-guide/emailing-coaches/
- Order a coach checks (a service's model, not a measurement): subject, first two lines, "is it templated", then video, then academics. AthlyAI, 1 Aug 2026 [SERVICE] https://athlyai.com/blog/do-college-coaches-read-recruiting-emails

What makes them watch the film:
- Link works on first click and is not locked [COACH-VOICE?] Fabian; FCP basketball coach: private YouTube that needs access "I did not request access" https://floridacoastalprep.com/mistakes-high-school-players-contacting-college-coaches/
- Best plays first. Signing Day Sports recruiter "Ryne" (13 Jun 2022) [COACH]: 90 seconds to two minutes, "frontload" the best plays https://thewire.signingdaysports.com/articles/an-athletes-guide-college-football-recruiting-process/ . Fabian: watch about 90 seconds, best 3 to 5 minutes first, spotlight yourself, no music.
- A reason to care: Premier Athletes coach moved on from an email with a link but no program fit.

What makes them delete (details ranked in section 4): cannot tell who/where/class in four seconds; dead or locked link; wrong school name or visible Cc list; inflated numbers; parent voice; empty claims.

## 3. Timing, contact rules, follow-up

Best day/hour (published data disagree; treat as a tiebreaker):
- [SERVICE, measured] NextCommit, Apr 2025 to Sep 2026, hours US Central: all sports 5 to 9 pm drew most replies, 7 to 9 am fewest. Football: best Thursday/Friday, 11 am to noon; best months Jan, then Nov and Dec. Men's basketball: Thursday/Friday, about 1 pm (also 4 and 8 pm); best month July. 47% of replies within 24h, 81% within 7 days. https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/coach-email-response-rates-2026
- [SERVICE, own data, no sample shown] NCSA: 4 to 8 pm, Tue to Sun. https://www.ncsasports.org/recruiting/contacting-college-coaches/email
- [SERVICE, no data shown] Vantage: Tue to Thu, 8 to 10 am; says timing is the "smallest lever". https://vantage-recruiting.com/guides/when-to-email-college-coaches . SCOUT: Tue to Thu, 7 to 9 am or 1 to 3 pm. ARI Athletics (2021, surveyed students, not coaches): Tue to Fri 3 to 5 and 7 to 9 pm. https://ariathletics.com/blog/the-best-time-to-send-an-email/
- [COACH] R. Lopez: avoid game days and the day-off after (football Saturdays/Sundays). https://rlopezcoaching.com/recruiting-tips/college-coaches-responding-to-my-emails/
- Playbook default (inference): weekday, Thu or Fri, football 11 am to noon Central, basketball about 1 pm Central, converted to the coach's time zone; never game day.

Contact rules (what they change about the email). Student-initiated email is allowed any day (NCSA https://www.ncsasports.org/ncaa-eligibility-center/recruiting-rules/recruiting-calendar ). A dead period bars in-person contact only; calls, texts, email stay allowed; a recruiting shutdown stops all correspondence (Bylaw 13.02.5, as read by Oriel Admissions, 26 Sep 2026, [SERVICE] https://orieladmissions.com/ncaa-recruiting-calendar/ ; primary NCAA text not opened here, so confirm at ncaa.org).
- D1 football coaches may begin sending email on 15 Jun after sophomore year (NCSA https://www.ncsasports.org/football/recruiting-rules-calendar ). Men's basketball D1: ScoutSync lists 15 Jun after sophomore year, not confirmed against NCAA text https://scoutsync.com/guides/ncaa-recruiting-calendars . D2, D3, NAIA: no timing limit (NextCommit https://www.nextcommit.ai/blog/ncaa-recruiting-calendar-2026-2027 ).
- Effect on the email: before the D1 date, a silent coach is not a "no". Do not ask for a call. Ask to be added to the board or for the questionnaire link, and name the date you will update. Pathley: "no reliable published percentage" exists, so read silence by rules first. https://www.pathley.ai/blog-posts/college-coach-response-rate-guide
- Today (11 Nov 2026): men's basketball dead period 9 to 12 Nov, next 24 to 26 Dec; football FBS evaluation period through 28/29 Nov (sources differ by one day), dead 30 Nov to 31 Dec, early signing 2 Dec (Oriel; NCSA https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/compliance/recruiting/calendar/2026-27/2026-27D1Rec_FBSRecruitingCalendar.pdf ). No football or men's basketball shutdown appears for the weeks ahead. Inference: signing week is busy; send after 16 Nov (basketball) and avoid 30 Nov to 4 Dec (football).

Follow-up cadence (no coach gives a number; vendors range widely):
- 7 to 10 days then 4 to 6 weeks, new info each time: NextCommit; SCOUT (up to 3 over 4 to 6 weeks).
- 10 to 14 days: IMG; AthlyAI (once). About two weeks, once: Fabian [COACH-VOICE?]. 2 to 3 weeks: QB Factory. Every 3 to 4 weeks: FieldLevel. Only with a real reason: EXACT Sports https://exactsports.com/how-to-follow-up-with-college-coaches-expert-tips-examples
- [COACH] volleyball coach: "You don't have to email me every week. I can't respond."
- Playbook rule: one follow-up at 10 to 14 days with something new (film, score, schedule); then monthly only with news; stop after 3 with no reply.

## 4. Mistakes that get an email ignored, ranked (by how many coach-voice sources name them)

1. Cannot tell who/where/class/position in four seconds. Reason: "can this kid play here, and can I tell right now?" (Fabian; Walker; Premier).
2. Dead, locked, or missing film, or film link only. Reason: "A dead or permission-locked link is the single most common self-inflicted wound" (Fabian); "film link alone gets deleted quickly" (Jones); FCP.
3. Mass blast: wrong school name, other schools visible in To/Cc, no program detail. Reason: "we know exactly how much you care about ours" (Fabian). Counter-view: one volleyball coach forgives a wrong name; a general-interest email that "might have went out to 20 other schools" is fine if it is short and true.
4. Inflated or unsourced numbers. Reason: "the measurement is coming" (Fabian). A college coach on Howcast: an omitted bad 40 is assumed bad https://howcast.com/videos/505270-how-coaches-view-a-players-measurables-football-recruiting
5. Parent writing in the player's voice. Reason: "We can always tell" (Fabian).
6. Empty claims ("hard worker"). Reason: "it proves nothing" (Coach Parker, via podcast host).
7. Basketball: offense-only film (Jones). Several recruits in one email (Jones).
8. Video attachments clog inboxes (Lopez).
9. Email from a recruiting platform: 94% of 100+ coaches preferred a private email; under 5% liked service emails (Athlete College Advisors, via Future 500 ID Camps, 27 Apr 2020, method unseen) [SERVICE] https://future500idcamp.com/recruiting-when-not-on-the-field-making-sure-coaches-get-and-read-your-emails

## 6. Template skeleton (grade-8 words; reason after each line)

SUBJECT: `[Class] [Pos] [First Last], [Ht]/[Wt], [HS], [ST]` e.g. `2028 QB Jake Swift, 6'2 195, Plano West, TX` (basketball: `2028 G Jake Swift, 6'3, 3.8 GPA, Wilmington DE`). 45 to 70 characters. Reason: a coach sorts by this alone on a phone.

BODY, in this order:
1. `Coach [Last Name],` Reason: right person, spelled right.
2. `I am [Name], a [class] [position] at [School] in [City, ST]. I am [ht] and [wt].` Reason: answers who, what, where at once.
3. `I like [School] because [one real, checkable fact: a scheme, a game, a major, a staff fit].` Reason: shows it is not a blast.
4. `[2 or 3 numbers, each with source and date].` Reason: coaches check numbers first, and a source makes them believable.
5. `My GPA is [x]. [SAT/ACT]. I want to study [major].` Reason: academics is a first filter.
6. `Highlights ([length]): [link]. Full game vs [team]: [link].` Reason: one click to proof, then depth.
7. `My next games: [date, place, number].` Reason: a coach can watch in person at no cost.
8. `My coach is [name], [phone/email].` Reason: coaches call the coach.
9. `[One easy question: Which camps will your staff run this winter?]` Reason: gives the coach a simple reply.
10. `Thank you, [Name]. [own phone] [own email] [public X/IG]. Data sheet below.` Reason: the athlete is the voice; handles let them verify.

ONE-PAGE DATA BLOCK (same text in the email body; optional PDF copy):
1 Name, class, school, city/state. (identity)
2 Position (primary, secondary); hand/foot. (fit)
3 Height, weight, wingspan (basketball) with date and who measured. (size filter)
4 Tested numbers: football 40, shuttle, vertical; basketball vertical, lane agility; each with timing type, event, date. (trust)
5 Last season: 3 to 5 stat lines, team record. (use)
6 Academics: core GPA, test, major, NCAA Eligibility Center ID if registered. (eligibility)
7 Film: highlights link, full-game link, update date. (proof)
8 Next 30 to 45 days: games, camps, number. (live look)
9 References: head coach, position/club coach, name and email. (verify)
10 Contact: athlete phone and email; parent/guardian name and phone, labeled "after you reply" (see Section 2 conflict). Leave out home address and birth date. (reach)
11 Social: public handles. (check)
12 Updated: date. (freshness)
