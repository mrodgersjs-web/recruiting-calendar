# Testable release checklist
2026-11-06. OPEN means no acceptable evidence; FAIL means current contradiction. Pass requires a saved receipt, environment, timestamp, tester and exact artifact/version. All payment tests below are sandbox-only with synthetic adult data. Never exercise live payments. Owners are recommended roles, not assignments accepted by a person.

## Checkout
- [ ] C01 **FAIL, P0, product owner:** one approved price/term sheet drives card, trial text, API, receipt and Stripe Price. Test every SKU, currency, annual/one-time interval, discount and tax treatment; exact equality required. Reject a mismatched external price id. Founding cap tested at 99/100/101 and concurrency, or remove cap claim.
- [ ] C02 **OPEN, P0, commerce engineer:** sandbox account and test-key guard tested, live keys and live session ids rejected; PREORDER cannot make missing files sellable without a separately approved, explicit delivery-date preorder flow. Evidence includes negative fixtures and deployment mode read-back, never raw secrets.
- [ ] C03 **PARTIAL, P0, commerce/counsel:** unchecked checkbox blocks mouse/keyboard activation; server-side required Stripe terms consent; retain exact disclosure version, amount, interval, timestamp and consent receipt. Marketing opt-in remains separate. Test no-consent and direct API paths, accessible labels/errors, keyboard and narrow mobile layout.
- [ ] C04 **OPEN, P0, QA:** real Stripe sandbox success, cancel, decline, 3DS, abandoned session, duplicate click and retry. One intentional order results in one entitlement; abandoned/unpaid order grants none. No live charge allowed.
- [ ] C05 **FAIL, P0, billing engineer:** authenticated billing link from site and receipt. Another synthetic account cannot open the first account's portal by replacing customer id. Reject unauthenticated requests and disallowed methods. Cancellation persists, confirms correct end date and prevents next sandbox invoice.
- [ ] C06 **OPEN, P0, counsel/support:** finalize terms/refund/privacy/auto-renewal, real monitored contact and required notices; no .example address or draft policy. Test refund of first purchase and renewal within 30 days and clear out-of-window handling.

## Fulfillment and recurring service
- [ ] F01 **FAIL, P0, editor/QA:** for each sellable SKU compare a manifest to every page, worksheet, worked example and promised format. Render PDFs; open CSV/XLSX/DOCX in intended apps; test formulas, links, print and accessibility. 100% promised files required, not a status string or file-exists check.
- [ ] F02 **OPEN, P0, commerce QA:** sandbox purchase -> confirmation -> actual download with correct bytes/MIME/name/edition. Test unpaid, bad id, foreign slug, path traversal, expired/revoked entitlement and refunded/disputed order. Define post-cancel rights to dated files separately from ongoing service.
- [ ] F03 **FAIL, P0, backend:** signed webhook persists receipt/order/entitlement; duplicate idempotent; out-of-order, replay, invalid signature, missing secret, 5xx and retry tested. Reconcile Stripe sandbox orders against fulfillment records; zero lost/double grants.
- [ ] F04 **FAIL, P0, lifecycle engineer:** welcome, payment failure, trial/renewal notice and cancellation actually delivered to approved internal inbox with provider ids and content versions. Test unsubscribe/transactional distinction, bounce and suppression. Derive notice schedule from counsel-reviewed requirements; comments about 7 days are not a scheduler.
- [ ] F05 **OPEN, P0 subscription, service owner:** two synthetic parent accounts save/reopen/edit tracker without crossover; alert clock crosses 30/7-day boundaries, sport/class/timezone filters and changed/cancelled date cases. Prove delivery, not just job execution. Annual rules refresh has named owner, due date and correction process.
- [ ] F06 **OPEN, P0 paid services, delivery lead:** Pathway quarterly review, school-list review, written questions, Audit session/12-month plan and club seats each have sample completed output, intake, capacity cap, appointment path, support/turnaround SLA and substitute/refund process. Hide paid availability until demonstrated.
- [ ] F07 **RISK/OPEN, P1, growth engineer:** synthetic parent capture returns a resolving, correct sport/class PDF or explicitly dated generic fallback. Preserve intended tool slug/source, consent and preference without oversubscribing. Verify duplicate subscriber and failed-provider behavior. Do not submit to production during this audit.

## Claims
- [ ] Q01 **FAIL, P0, editor/product:** inventory every feature/price/availability claim against output or operating receipt. Replace BUILT=file-exists with DRAFT / QA-PASSED / DELIVERABLE / LIVE-FULFILLED stages. No paid CTA for incomplete entitlement.
- [ ] Q02 **OPEN, P0, rules editor:** each calendar rule has authoritative URL, exact bylaw, sport/division/class applicability, effective year, read date, reviewer and expiry. Random calendar outputs checked against source; all high-risk eligibility/signing/NIL rules independently reviewed. "Every" requires full population, not a sample.
- [ ] Q03 **OPEN, P0, counsel/editor:** every real athlete is adult now; facts sourced and read-back verified; no invented quotes or implied use of product. Commercial adjacency reviewed. Generated person not presented as named athlete; visible illustration/non-affiliation labels survive mobile/carousel/story states. Source and license record for every image/video.
- [ ] Q04 **FAIL/OPEN, P0 before tax/NIL sale, qualified reviewer:** review NIL/tax draft with effective-date sources and exceptions. Remove or support blanket tax classification, set-aside safety claim and unsupported numerical thresholds. Educational disclaimer does not cure a false factual instruction.
- [ ] Q05 **OPEN, P1, research/editor:** evidence for staff-expectation and inbox-testing claims, or remove them. Coach benchmarks report denominator, sampling, observation date and uncertainty. No scholarship/roster guarantees, fabricated users, testimonials, scarcity or outcomes.

## Minor safety and privacy
- [ ] M01 **PARTIAL, P0, safety owner:** adult parent/guardian customer boundary explicit at each intake. Test child-self-identification path: do not continue collection; document deletion/escalation. Do not collect date of birth merely to add an age gate. Counsel determines age-assurance/COPPA obligations.
- [ ] M02 **OPEN, P0 new hosted features, privacy engineer:** data map for tracker/film/profile/family seats; purpose, fields, processor, access roles, retention, deletion and logs. Default private; no searchable minor profiles, location/routine or contact information. Local downloadable CSV must not silently become hosted child data.
- [ ] M03 **OPEN, P0, security QA:** parent A/athlete A cannot see family B; invitations/seat revocation tested; staff least privilege and access audit; URLs do not expose private identity; download token leakage/reuse bounded. Use synthetic adults only in test fixtures.
- [ ] M04 **OPEN, P0 new messaging/review services, safeguarding lead:** no autonomous coach/adult-to-minor contact or publishing. Parent exact-text approval for any future outbound flow; film upload consent, link expiry, content-report/block/escalation and staff safeguarding policy demonstrated before accepting uploads.
- [ ] M05 **FAIL/OPEN, P0, support/privacy:** functioning privacy/report mailbox, request identity check, deletion across processors/backups by documented SLA; test synthetic export/delete and verify actual processor removal. No sensitive child details in logs or analytics.

## Release rule
No real payment release while any applicable P0 is FAIL/OPEN. All claimed benefits need evidence; omitted features must be removed from the sold promise, not hidden in a global "planned" footnote. QA and counsel signoffs are distinct. This checklist does not authorize edits, deployment, customer contact or payment.
