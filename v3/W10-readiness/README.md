## Priority findings
1. **P0 fulfillment:** no paid PDFs, no full subscription entitlement lifecycle. Webhook verifies a signature and logs an event but does not persist orders, deduplicate events, deliver email, revoke access, or handle recovery. `capture.js` has an empty send branch even if SEND_ENABLED is true. Keys alone will not complete delivery.
2. **P0 offer/price consistency:** live price card $99/yr versus $149/year trial disclosure and API price. Founding "first 100" has no quota enforcement found in inspected commerce code. A supplied Stripe price id is used without validating its amount/currency/cadence against the disclosure. Pathway $499/year, Audit $1,500 and Club $299/year are advertised but not resolved checkout offers. Keep these clearly planned and unsellable until staffed and tested.
3. **P0 cancellation/security:** live `/account` and `/billing` both 404, despite promised billing-page cancellation. `portal.js` accepts a caller-supplied customer id without an ownership check or method guard. It is test-key-only today, but must not become a live customer portal in this shape. Session-id possession is the current PDF access credential; durable owner-authentication and post-refund/cancellation policy are not demonstrated.
4. **P0 consent/legal/support:** checkbox and Stripe terms consent are implemented in current code, an improvement over W3's older review. However API does not record the page checkbox's version/time; final terms remain "Draft for review. Not yet in effect." The support address is `hello@therecruitingcalendar.example`. Privacy request/refund promises cannot rely on that address. Counsel must review actual final auto-renewal notice and consent design, athlete-name commercial adjacency and NIL/tax content.
5. **P1 free delivery:** capture constructs `${PDF_BASE_URL}/${sport}-class-2029.pdf`. At production `/downloads`, all three sport-specific PDFs returned 404; the general `/downloads/recruiting-calendar-class-2029.pdf` returned 200 PDF. Actual PDF_BASE_URL is not inspected, so this is a demonstrated deployment-path risk, not proof of a broken configured response. Capture also ignores requested class year when constructing the PDF path.
6. **P1 notification routing:** W8 notify forms pass plan=giveaway with a source slug. Capture stores only sport/class/plan in first_name and ignores source. It therefore does not preserve which tool was requested. New W8 tool slugs are absent from resolver and capture's plan allowlist. A successful save would not prove a correct future notification.
7. **P1 contents vs labels:** `nil-tax.md` is 17 lines plus a CSV header, not a tested workbook with expense/1099 tabs. Its blanket self-employment statement, 25-30% "safe" set-aside, $600 form threshold and >$1,000 net-for-quarter estimate trigger need dated professional review; none should be treated as established tax advice by this assessment. `season-planner.md` is 19 lines with four CSV lines, while catalog promises windows pre-marked; validate actual sport/year completeness. Unsupported authority phrases include "log a D1 staff expects" and "subject-line bank tested against staff inboxes" (latter is PROPOSED). File presence is not feature parity.
8. **P1 minor safety:** live forms say parents/guardians and under-13 exclusion, collect limited fields, and there is no visible direct athlete messaging in sampled flow. These are useful boundaries, not verified COPPA compliance. Future hosted tracker, family seats, directories, profile audit and film review introduce identifiable minor data and adult-contact risks absent from today's simple email form. Do not inherit readiness from today's low-data preview.
