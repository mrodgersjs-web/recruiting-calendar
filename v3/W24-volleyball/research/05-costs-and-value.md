## 5. Paid recruiting services and profiles

What they do: they build a profile page, may edit a video, and send template emails to coaches on your behalf. What they do not do: promise an offer, and no source below shows a coach on record who says a paid tier got a girl recruited.

Price
- NCSA does not publish prices. Two reports give $1,320 to $4,200 for its paid tiers (Grant Magazine, Dec 2023) and $250 to $2,100 (Scholarships360, Oct 2025). The gap is large; neither says if it is per year. [DATA] https://rawrecruit.io/resources/compare/how-much-do-college-recruiting-services-cost Checked 2026-11-11
- A volleyball-specific explainer says parents report roughly $1,000 to $6,000 per year, "none confirmed by NCSA". The free tier includes a profile, coach directory and map, scholarship guidance, an SAT/ACT prep course. Paid tiers are named Champion, Elite, MVP, MVP+. ## 6. Visit weekends: who pays

- Official visit = the college pays. Unofficial visit = the family pays everything. Unofficial visits are unlimited, but in D1 and D2 each must come after the first allowed date for the sport. [OFFICIAL] http://fs.ncaa.org.s3.amazonaws.com/Docs/eligibility_center/Student_Resources/Official_Unofficial.pdf Checked 2026-11-11
- What a D1 school may pay on an official visit: transportation for the recruit plus up to two family members; lodging; up to three meals a day for the recruit plus up to four family members; up to $60 per person of entertainment; home-game tickets. D2: transportation for the recruit only. D3: transportation for the recruit. [OFFICIAL] http://fs.ncaa.org/Docs/eligibility_center/Student_Resources/CBSA.pdf ; https://ncaa.egain.cloud/kb/EligibilityHelp/content/KB-2224/What-is-an-official-visit Checked 2026-11-11
- Plain answer on airfare: a D1 school may fly the recruit and up to two family members; a D2 or D3 school may only pay the recruit's transportation. D1 visit activity must stay within 30 miles of campus, and lodging is generally two nights. [OFFICIAL] https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/compliance/d1/D1Comp_Visits101.pdf Checked 2026-11-11
- Rule change warning: in 2020 the NCAA punished Ohio women's volleyball for paying $4,408 of parents' airfare on official visits (then not allowed). Older guides (NCSA, 2017) say parents' flights cannot be bought. The current NCAA documents (Aug 2025, Feb 2026) allow two family members' transportation at D1. Use the newest NCAA sheet. [OFFICIAL] https://www.ncaa.org/news/2020/6/16/ohio-women-s-volleyball-program-provided-impermissible-recruiting-benefits.aspx ; https://www.ncsasports.org/ncaa-eligibility-center/recruiting-rules/official-visits Checked 2026-11-11
- One official visit per school; a school may finance only one. There is no published cap on how many schools a recruit visits; a Maryland club sheet says the number is unlimited, while a Florida golf association page says "a maximum of 5" (older rule). Sources conflict. [OFFICIAL] https://cdn2.sportngin.com/attachments/document/fc36-3166527/NCAA_26-27_Volleyball_Recruiting_Rules_-_Google_Docs.pdf ; https://www.fsga.org/sections/content/College-Preparation/102 Checked 2026-11-11
- First date for a D1 official visit in volleyball: most guides say August 1 before junior year; D2 June 15 before junior year (one source says D2 after June 15 of sophomore year); D3 January 1 of junior year. For a class of 2029 girl (sophomore in 2026-27) that points to August 1, 2027 for D1. Not read from the NCAA bylaw itself, so confirm with each coach. [OFFICIAL] https://orieladmissions.com/official-visit-vs-unofficial-visit/ ; https://volleyballdirectory.com/guides/official-vs-unofficial-visits ; https://www.fsga.org/sections/content/College-Preparation/102 Checked 2026-11-11
- Many programs cannot pay: "A lot of programs can't afford to pay for official visits so don't be sad if they only offer you an unofficial, it doesn't mean that they aren't truly interested." D3 budgets are thin; many recruits use unofficial visits. [COACH] https://jvavolleyball.org/recruiting-solutions-4-things-to-know-upperclassmen-starting-the-contact-and-evaluation-period ; https://cdn2.sportngin.com/attachments/document/fc36-3166527/NCAA_26-27_Volleyball_Recruiting_Rules_-_Google_Docs.pdf Checked 2026-11-11
- Dollar price of an unofficial visit weekend for a Wisconsin family: no published source found. See "Could not settle". [UNCONFIRMED] none Checked 2026-11-11

What the same money buys, by my arithmetic:
- A camp plus a film edit = $255 (Marquette satellite $200 + Fiverr $55) up to $760 (prep camp $310 + $450 VolleyVideo edit). That is roughly 6 to 17 percent of a $4,350 club fee. [DATA] sources above Checked 2026-11-11
