## 1. Video spec from coaches on record

### 1.1 Junior Volleyball Association (JVA) coach survey, by Patty Costlow (Munciana club recruiting coordinator)
Source: https://jvavolleyball.org/video-recruiting-process-simple-significant  (page dated 2017-05-16 in search index). Checked 2026-11-11.
Coaches are anonymous, labelled by level. Old (2017) but the only multi-level coach survey found. Tag each [COACH] (anonymous, level as labelled).

- [COACH, D1 high-major, anonymous] "A highlight video included in the FIRST email with a DIRECT link is the best thing you can do to get yourself an immediate evaluation with time-strapped coaches." Same coach: "It may be months before we see you live if we are just checking courts of people who wrote letters, but provided no context (video!)." Checked 2026-11-11.
- [COACH, D1 low-major, anonymous] Watches "how technically sound their game is like, how fast their arm is ... and also body language." Checked 2026-11-11.
- [COACH, D2, anonymous] "We use highlights to generate lists of who we need to evaluate in person or request more film. Raw film clips allow us to evaluate the kid in a more realistic evaluation." Checked 2026-11-11.
- [COACH, D3, anonymous] "A 3-5 minute highlight video is imperative." and "If the first few clips of a highlight video are impressive, I will gladly send an email and begin requesting more film and the athlete's playing schedule". Checked 2026-11-11.
- [COACH, NAIA, anonymous] "We would absolutely prefer a highlight video over a skills video." Looks for "technique, athleticism, do they see the block (if they are hitter), defensive movement and team interaction". Checked 2026-11-11.
- [COACH, anonymous] "Highlights from matches - NOT practice drills." Controlled-drill clips "do not give them a good sense of your overall athletic ability, decision making and reactions to errors". Checked 2026-11-11.
- [COACH, anonymous] Show "a few seconds before and after - I want to see your body language, communication with teammates and reactions". Checked 2026-11-11.
- Clips by position per the survey author [SERVICE/club recruiting coordinator, summarizing coaches]: Middles: hit, block, handle a free-ball pass, serve. Outsides: hit, block, PASS, serve and dig. Setters: set in and out of system, dig, serve, block. DS/Libero: PASS, PASS, dig, serve and set. Checked 2026-11-11.
- Unedited film [COACH, anonymous]: "A few highlights along with any amount of rotational play will suffice." Another: "A complete set, edited only for timeouts." Another wants to see "pass to transition, hit shots against a block and/or defense, and block or dig a transition back". Checked 2026-11-11.
- Don'ts list [COACH, as relayed by JVA]: "DON'T send an intro email with a 'please let me know if you'd like to see a video' is useless. If you have the video, send it." / "DON'T include special effects, slo-mo or giant arrows / circles, just show us who you are once" / "DON'T send video that require sign-ins." / "DON'T send full game footage where they are touching the ball infrequently, there is a good chance the coach will not reply." Checked 2026-11-11.

### 1.2 "Coaching Volleyball" blog, a college coach's list
Source: https://coachingvb.com/volleyball-recruiting-videos-a-coachs-thoughts/ (index date 2020-09-02). Author named only as "a coach" on the page; name not confirmed. Checked 2026-11-11. Tag [COACH].
- [COACH] "I don't need to see a whole bunch of serves. A couple good examples of your technique is plenty." Also "I want to see your serve mechanics. I don't care about the outcome."
- [COACH] "I don't need to see more than a handful of reps in each of your major skills in a highlights type video. Just show me your best stuff."
- [COACH] "When it comes to hitting, highlights are fine. For everything else (blocking, passing, defense, setting) I want more. Movement is an important factor".
- [COACH] "Highlight clips of passing and digging aren't really of all that much value - especially the flashy ones. For all I know, you shanked all your other opportunities."
- [COACH] "Clips of your coach/teammate/etc. serving you lollipops or hitting balls straight at you to dig also don't give me much to go on." Also "Reps from live play - even if it's a practice game - are always MUCH preferred."
- [COACH] "Make sure I can tell which one is you! ... At a minimum, tell me at the beginning which one is you so I can easily pick you out. And if it's regular game footage, telling me where you start is useful too."
- [COACH] "don't put a pause in the middle of the primary skill you're showing me. For example, a spike approach."
- [COACH] "Setters, if you play front row I need to see at least a little bit of your blocking."
- [COACH] "I do not care about overpass kills."

### 1.3 Crossroads of America Volleyball (COA, an Indiana club) video guidance
Source: https://www.coavolleyball.com/video . Checked 2026-11-11. Tag [COACH] (club staff, not college). Author not named.
- [COACH, club] "Keep the videos in the 3-5 minute range (anything longer likely won't get viewed)". Put the best clips first "to grab their attention".
- [COACH, club] Intro slide: "Name, Number, Position, Graduation Year and maybe one or two things about the player should be plenty".
- [COACH, club] "Don't worry about music overlays - coaches will probably mute these when watching anyway and you risk battling copyright restriction issues".
- [COACH, club] Camera "at the end line on the side of the court your team is playing on"; "Get the entire court in frame"; "Use a tripod"; "Don't zoom in and out".
- [COACH, club] "Don't worry if your team score a point on the play or not. It's ok if it didn't end perfectly." (This differs from the Art of Coaching advice below that every clip be a kill.)
- [COACH, club] Serving is "included but not emphasized" for every position. Libero/DS: serve receive range, defensive range, vision. Hitters: serve receive to attack, block to attack, back row attacks for 6-rotation outsides. Middles: serve receive to attack, block/transition to attack, blocking footwork to both sides.
- [COACH, club] "Some coaches may ask for unedited film at some point... Make sure to save footage of some good (competitive) games".
- [COACH, club] Sequence: send intro emails first, then video: "Don't start with sending them a video though - make sure you've gone through and done some introductory emails". (Conflicts with the JVA D1 coach who wants the video in the FIRST email. See "Could not settle".)

### 1.4 The Art of Coaching Volleyball (coaching-education site)
Source: https://www.theartofcoachingvolleyball.com/making-a-highlight-video-that-will-get-college-coaches-to-your-court (index date 2018-12-21). Page author not named in the fetched text. Checked 2026-11-11. Tag [COACH] (unnamed).
- [COACH] "The video should be 2-4 minutes long."
- [COACH] "When you send intro emails to college coaches, you should always have a link to your YouTube video in the body of the email."
- [COACH] "You don't need to send coaches a full set or match unless they specifically ask for it. (If they do ask, it means they plan to make time to watch it...)"
- [COACH] Intro slide: "Player's name, jersey number, height, grad year, position, GPA (if it's mid to upper 3.0 to 4.0+; if not, leave it out), club name, official team name."
- [COACH] Organize by skill with a label slide before each skill. "Have the video in regular speed and not slow motion."
- [COACH] "Keep the sound on because it's good to hear the players talking on the court". (Conflicts with "music no" views only on game sound vs added music; added music is not recommended by anyone found.)
- [COACH] Camera at floor level, end line, whole net and court in frame, ball in view; a parent films so the player is not distracted.
- [COACH] "The level of play in the video is important in some ways, but in other ways it's not!"

## 2. Highlight reel vs film, and the "did not do homework" mistakes

- Two-video model [SERVICE, NCSA]: "Volleyball players need to create two different types of videos: one that shows the athlete doing repetitions ... plus a video of a full game." Skills video "no more than 3-5 minutes". Full game "should shorten it by removing dead time (side changes, time outs, substitutions, etc.)". https://www.ncsasports.org/womens-volleyball/how-to-get-recruited . Incentive: NCSA sells paid recruiting packages. Checked 2026-11-11.
- Highlight vs film, a D2 coach (anonymous) [COACH]: "We use highlights to generate lists of who we need to evaluate in person or request more film. Raw film clips allow us to evaluate the kid in a more realistic evaluation." https://jvavolleyball.org/video-recruiting-process-simple-significant . Checked 2026-11-11.
- Highlight vs film, NAIA coach (anonymous) [COACH]: "we also recognize that they are a highlight video and the recruit is going to pick their best performances." Same URL. Checked 2026-11-11.
- Coach blog [COACH]: "just because it's a kill that doesn't mean it's a good attack"; "When it comes to hitting, highlights are fine. For everything else (blocking, passing, defense, setting) I want more." https://coachingvb.com/volleyball-recruiting-videos-a-coachs-thoughts/ Checked 2026-11-11.
- Conflict on clip choice [COACH vs COACH]: Art of Coaching says every hit, set and block shown should be a point ("All hits shown should be kills") while the CoachingVB coach says kills and aces alone do not show skill and wants mechanics and movement. Both URLs above. Checked 2026-11-11.
- NCSA claim on attention [SERVICE]: "Generally, coaches will watch the first 10-25 seconds of a recruit's volleyball highlight video" https://www.ncsasports.org/womens-volleyball/recruiting-video-highlights (excerpt). Another NCSA page: "college coaches watch a recruiting video for about 25 seconds" https://www.ncsasports.org/mens-volleyball (excerpt, "According to NCSA data"). No method or sample size published in the excerpts. Incentive: NCSA sells video and recruiting services. Treat as [FOLKLORE]-grade. Checked 2026-11-11.
- Mistakes that signal no homework:
  - Wrong school or coach name, form letter. [COACH, D2, EXACT Sports page, author is a D2 volleyball coach per page text "I'm looking for..."; name not confirmed] "Make sure you spell the school name correctly and address it to the correct coach... You don't want to make it feel as if you're sending the same exact email to 10 different schools, and just changing the name of the coach". https://exactsports.com/volleyball-recruiting-how-to-get-recruited-for-college-volleyball (excerpt). Incentive: EXACT Sports sells recruiting services. Checked 2026-11-11.
  - Video that needs a login; email that only says "let me know if you want to see a video"; full-game footage where she barely touches the ball. [COACH via JVA] https://jvavolleyball.org/video-recruiting-process-simple-significant . Checked 2026-11-11.
  - Only one skill shown (set after set). [SERVICE] https://universityathlete.com/resource-center/articles/highlight-reel-tips-for-athletes . Checked 2026-11-11.
  - Highlights of coach-fed or drill reps. [COACH] https://coachingvb.com/volleyball-recruiting-videos-a-coachs-thoughts/ . Checked 2026-11-11.
  - Coach "Kurt Vlasich" (Claremont-Mudd-Scripps, D3) [COACH, named]: "I can tell the email that dad sent with your name on it." and "I like the personal touch." https://www.usatodayhss.com/story/sports/high-school/2018/02/09/recruiting-column-claremont-mudd-scripps-volleyball-coach-kurt-vlasich-talks-recruiting/76877883007 (excerpt, 2018). Checked 2026-11-11.

## 4. Email form for volleyball

### 4.1 Subject line
- [COACH, club, unnamed] "When you email a college coach, be sure to ALWAYS include name, position, grade year in the subject line." Spell out the position: "Do NOT say 'I am a OH/RS/MH' in your email. Coaches will not know what you are". Use the real birth-certificate name because University Athlete lists that name: "If you put Bella coaches may not be able to find you". https://www.coavolleyball.com/recruitingtips . Checked 2026-11-11.
- [SERVICE, ProActive Volley, Dianne DeNecochea, former Tennessee player and pro beach player, sells recruiting coaching] Format: "YOUR NAME, GRAD YEAR, POSITION, HEIGHT (if helpful), GPA (if 3.5 and above), (NOTABLES - jump touch 10 ft. and up, etc.) - Competing at XXX Qualifier". https://www.theartofcoachingvolleyball.com/why-you-need-to-email-college-coaches-and-how-to-do-it . Checked 2026-11-11.
- [SERVICE, Balltime recruiting guide] "NAME, HS GRAD YEAR, POSITION, and something about you that stands out athletically or academically (ONLY IF IT IS GOOD)". Example given uses a Madison, WI player and a Madtown club. https://recruiting.balltime.com/getting-started/emailing-college-coaches . Balltime is owned by Hudl, which sells video plans. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [SERVICE, Volleyball Directory, directory site] Example "2029 OH, 6-0, [Club] 16 Elite, [Event] Court 14, Sat 9am". https://volleyballdirectory.com/guides/how-to-email-a-college-coach . Checked 2026-11-11.
- [SERVICE, NCSA] "ideally between 50-75 characters ... Lengthy subject lines tend to get cut off." https://www.ncsasports.org/recruiting/contacting-college-coaches/email . Checked 2026-11-11.

### 4.2 Body contents
- [COACH, club, unnamed] Hitters: "coaches want your height and position and approach touch - if you don't have the approach jump, ask someone to measure it for you." Include "your club schedule or link to club schedule". "ALWAYS include a video link." Use "a safe" address like name plus VB or class year at gmail. https://www.coavolleyball.com/recruitingtips . Checked 2026-11-11.
- [SERVICE, Balltime] Coaches skim for: video link; grad year and position ("different programs have very specific needs in each graduating class"); height and approach touch; academic interest; "Connection to that school/program". Send from the recruit's own address, not a service or parent address. https://recruiting.balltime.com/getting-started/emailing-college-coaches . Checked 2026-11-11.
- [SERVICE, ProActive Volley] Intro email order: interest in school; self (grad year, position, height, club, hometown, awards); academics (GPA, class rank, activities, major); why this school; tournament schedule as bullets; ask "do they need your position in your grad year"; sign off with club coach contact. Same Art of Coaching URL as 4.1. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [SERVICE, Volleyball Directory] Order: one personal line; who you are in one sentence; film link high up; where to see you live; academics with NCAA Eligibility Center ID if on D1/D2 path; club coach name, email, phone; short sign-off. ### 4.3 What to leave out
- Fluff: [SERVICE, Balltime] "I am a great teammate and love to compete!" are statements "anyone can make"; add a story instead. Same Balltime URL. Checked 2026-11-11.
- Weak openers: [COACH, club] "when the school is 8-23" do not say "congrats on a great season"; wrong coach name, wrong mascot, or a major the school lacks "will get an email deleted". https://www.coavolleyball.com/recruitingtips . Checked 2026-11-11.
- Slang and texting lingo: [COACH, club] "some coaches will cross you off the list if you use slang or do not refer to them as 'Coach ...'". Same URL. Checked 2026-11-11.
- Attachments: [SERVICE] "Never attach video files". https://varsitydraft.com/recruiting/highlight-videos Checked 2026-11-11.
- Group sends / visible list in To line [SERVICE, Learn Volley, Volleyball Directory]. URLs above. Checked 2026-11-11.

## 5. Follow-up rules

For the class of 2029 the D1 and D2 "June 15 after sophomore year" date is 15 June 2028 (her sophomore year is 2027-28). Dates per USA Volleyball below.

- Rule that frames everything [OFFICIAL, USA Volleyball, national governing body]: "NCAA Division I and II college coaches cannot directly communicate with recruits or their families until June 15 after the recruit's sophomore year." and "NO RESTRICTIONS for when a recruit can communicate with college coaches at any level... the college coach is restricted on replying". https://usavolleyball.org/resource/college-recruitment-timeline . Checked 2026-11-11.
- The NCAA 2026-27 D1 women's volleyball calendar is published by the NCAA; it shows a dead period Nov 9-12, 2026 and a contact period Sept 1 - Nov 30 (excerpt). [OFFICIAL] https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/compliance/recruiting/calendar/2026-27/2026-27D1Rec_WVBRecruitingCalendar.pdf . Checked 2026-11-11.
- Cadence, ProActive Volley (Dianne DeNecochea, advisor, sells coaching) [SERVICE]: freshman or sophomore before June 15: "keep emailing every 2 weeks. First an intro email, then check-in/update emails, with and without video." Junior or senior: "email every week to every two weeks". "If you haven't heard anything back after 5-8 emails ... you can drop the school". https://proactivevolley.com/what-if-college-coaches-dont-respond-try-this . Checked 2026-11-11.
- Same author [SERVICE]: send "Check-In/Update Emails" before tournaments "if they need your position in your grad year OR if they haven't responded yet"; "always be the last one to make contact." https://www.theartofcoachingvolleyball.com/why-you-need-to-email-college-coaches-and-how-to-do-it . Checked 2026-11-11.
- Learn Volley [SERVICE]: follow up "Every 3-6 weeks with news". https://learnvolley.com/recruiting/email-college-coaches . Checked 2026-11-11.
- A club coach [COACH, club, unnamed]: "Email often. Coaches get lots of emails a day, so don't email one time and then assume they are not interested." Short videos "after each event is ideal". https://www.coavolleyball.com/recruitingtips . Checked 2026-11-11.
- A college volleyball coach blog, 2011 [COACH, author unnamed in excerpt, old]: "we can literally get 500 emails a week and we sometimes just hit the delete button when we know we won't be recruiting someone" and "I literally have to send 2 or 3 emails, and then call to get a response". http://www.collegevolleyballcoach.com/2011/03/double-recruiting-questions.html (excerpt). Checked 2026-11-11.
- Reply speed by the recruit [COACH, D3, named Vlasich]: "I shouldn't have to wait a week to hear back from you." https://www.usatodayhss.com/story/sports/high-school/2018/02/09/recruiting-column-claremont-mudd-scripps-volleyball-coach-kurt-vlasich-talks-recruiting/76877883007 . ProActive Volley [SERVICE]: reply "either the same day or the next day." Checked 2026-11-11.
- Mass emailing [COACH, club]: "get the coaches name correct, the mascot, and know if they have your specific major." https://www.coavolleyball.com/recruitingtips . [SERVICE, Hudl]: "Send a customized email to each contact" https://www.hudl.com/support/volleyball-recruiting-guide/guides/email-college-coaches . ## 6. Published numbers on email volume and reply rates

### 1.6 "Tips and Tricks to Recruiting - Perspective from a College Coach" (COA club page)
Source: https://www.coavolleyball.com/recruitingtips . Author is described as a college coach's perspective; name not on the fetched text. Checked 2026-11-11. Tag [COACH, unnamed].
- [COACH] Cover page with "name, position, graduation year, jersey number in video"; if the film is from BallerTV "filming high from the side, put the jersey color for coaches". "Put the best plays first. A coach may only have 10 - 15 seconds to watch".
- [COACH] "This video can be a minute or two long - it is better to put together short videos after every tournament than to wait until the end of the year".
- [COACH] "Do not use slow motion". "Have a full game ready to send to coaches if they ask for it. Coaches like to see how you respond to mistakes, how you interact with your teammates after good and bad points".
- [COACH] Serve receive and defense: "If you are a Libero - please include passing and defense." "If you are a 6 rotation player (or DS/L) - include defense and passing." Serving: "some coaches like this and some don't. I would not put many clips in."
- [COACH] Attackers: "include variety in your videos. I once got a video where the recruit literally hit the same shot in every clip."
- [COACH] Setters: "Do not include only kills, or sets from the perfect pass position. The college game is 70% out of system". Include "dumps and jousts", setting every position, and defense.
- [COACH] Music is split: "Some coaches listen on mute - but some like to know your personality and music choice - but do not have explicit songs." And parents: sideline voices on the audio can be heard, "they will let your coaches know what you are saying." (Other sources above say music does not matter or is muted. Added music is optional at best.)
- [COACH] Skills videos: "some coaches love them, some hate them" and "We would rather see you against a block than on an open net."
