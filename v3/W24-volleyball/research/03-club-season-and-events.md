## 0. Who she is on the club calendar (plain-English key)

- [OFFICIAL] USA Volleyball sets club age by birth date, not grade. In 2026-27 the 16 and Under division is players born on or after July 1, 2010; 17s are born on or after July 1, 2009. A sophomore in 2026-27 who was born July 2010 to June 2011 is therefore a "16s" player this season (my reading of the chart; a girl born July-Aug 2011 or later would be 15s). Source: https://usavolleyball.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/USAV-2026-2027-Age-Definition-Chart.pdf . Checked 2026-11-11
- [OFFICIAL] USA Volleyball's own rule of thumb in one club's guide: the age division is the age the athlete will be on June 30, 2027. Source: https://ssprodst.blob.core.windows.net/library/427/67a841ec-5e7a-4d3b-a7a5-9344e57b1235-10-30-2026-03-57-25-243.pdf (club parent guidebook quoting USAV). Checked 2026-11-11
- [OFFICIAL] This season's chart has a girls-only waiver: an 11th grader born on or after July 1, 2008 may play 17s instead of 18s (stated reason: recruiting concerns for 11th-grade girls). Checked 2026-11-11

- [COACH] A writer who says he is a former Division I head coach (site: "The College Volleyball Coach"; author's name not given on the page): "College coaches do not put a lot of weight on high school volleyball. We will speak to the contrary to be politically correct, but the reality is club volleyball is 99% of our recruiting and evaluation focus mainly because of the examples I provided above." And: "When I was a DI head coach, my only thoughts related to my recruits and high school volleyball were, 1) Free touches on the ball, 2) Please don't get hurt." Source: http://www.collegevolleyballcoach.com/2017/06/high-school-volleyball-varsity-team.html . Checked 2026-11-11
- [COACH] Same writer, same page: "In the fall, college coaches are focused on their own season ... and save for a few elite or next door college teams, coaches are not concerned with the high school season/players. We are looking for year over year skill development and this development is mostly influenced by club and not high school." Source: http://www.collegevolleyballcoach.com/2017/06/high-school-volleyball-varsity-team.html . Checked 2026-11-11
- [COACH] Same writer, 2017: "the vast majority of college volleyball recruiting and evaluations occur during the club season. Source: http://www.collegevolleyballcoach.com/2017/09/multi-sport-athlete-and-college.html . Checked 2026-11-11
- [COACH] Same writer, 2010: "My belief is it does NOT hurt the recruiting process to not play high school volleyball. ... college coaches view club volleyball as the elite training and most of us don't have time during our season/high school season to get out and watch/evaluate PSA's." Source: http://www.collegevolleyballcoach.com/2010/01/ncaa-womens-volleyball-recruiting.html . Checked 2026-11-11
