## 1. How football recruiting is timed
Three things open at different times. Don't treat them as one date.

| What opens | When (Class of 2029) | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Coach calls, texts, emails to your athlete | 2027-06-15 (after sophomore year) | D1 Bylaws 13.1.3.1, 13.4.1 |
| Off-campus, in-person contact | 2028-01-01 (junior year) | D1 Bylaws 13.1.1.1.6, 13.1.5.3 |
| Paid (official) visits | 2028-04-01 to 2028-06-25, then from Sept 1 of senior year | D1 Bylaws 13.6.2.1.7, 13.6.3 |
| Written aid offer | On or after 2028-11-15 (senior year) | D1 Bylaw 13.9.3.3 |

Unofficial visits that involve the athletics department are allowed at any age, subject to the recruiting calendar's dead periods.

**New for 2027 (FBS only).** Bylaw 13.1.5.3 for bowl subdivision football was adopted 10/7/26, effective 1/1/27: a school gets four recruiting opportunities (contacts and evaluations) per prospect from January 1 through May 31, no more than one a week (Monday to Sunday). Of those four, two contacts may be anywhere and two only at the athlete's high school (Bylaw 13.1.5.3.2). A school visit counts as an opportunity for every prospect at that school (Bylaw 13.1.4.3). FCS stays on the older rule: two opportunities per contact period after January 1 of junior year. Limits apply to the whole program, not per coach.

What this means for you: a spring visit from a coach is scarce. Have film, transcript and schedule ready before January 1, 2028.

## 2. The two academic lines you can't fix late
**Line 1, the 10/7 lock.** D1 requires 10 of the 16 core courses, 7 of them English, math or science, finished before the first day of the seventh semester. Those grades lock. Your high school sets the exact date. (D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.1(b))

**Line 2, the finish.** 16 core courses with at least a 2.300 core GPA, graduate, and the final transcript sent to the Eligibility Center. Core GPA isn't the transcript GPA. (D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.1(a); course criteria Bylaw 14.3.1.3)

The 16: English 4 years, math 3 (Algebra I or higher), natural/physical science 2, one more English/math/science, social science 2, four more academic courses (Bylaw 14.3.1.1(a)).

Full worksheet: see the companion playbook *Eligibility: the 2 Hard Deadlines*.

## 4. Target list in three tiers
Aim for 30 to 50 programs across levels. Fill one row each.

| Tier | What it means | How many |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Level you'd love; you need a coach to say you fit | 8 to 12 |
| Fit | Level where your film, size and grades match recent rosters (you check the roster pages) | 15 to 25 |
| Sure | Programs where you'd likely play and the school fits academically. Include D2, D3, NAIA, JUCO | 8 to 12 |

For each: division, position need (count seniors at your position on the roster), position coach name and staff email from the official staff page, school's academic fit, cost after likely aid (D3 = no athletic aid).

## 5. Scripts
**First email (athlete sends, after 2027-06-15).** Subject: `2029 [Position] | [Height/Weight] | [HS], [City, ST] | Film`
> Coach [Last name], I'm [Name], a Class of 2029 [position] at [HS] in [City, ST]. [Height], [weight]; [one verified stat or measurable with source/date]. My junior film is here: [link]. GPA [x.x]; core courses on track. I'd like to learn what you look for at [position] and whether I should attend your camp. Schedule: [link]. Head coach: [name, phone, email]. Thank you, [Name], [phone].

**Follow-up (14 days, no reply).**
> Coach [Last name], following up with new film from [game, date]: [link]. Still very interested in [School]. [Name]

**First call checklist (athlete leads, parent listens).** Ask: Where do I fit on your board at my position? How many [position]s are you taking in 2029? What would you need to see from me next? What's the academic profile of your signees? When do you hold junior days and camps?

Don't ask a coach for an offer on a first call. Write down exactly what was said and when.

## 6. The junior-year official visit window (2028-04-01 to 06-25)
Before the visit: EC certification account open; transcript sent to the school; you're on their visit list (D1 Bylaw 13.6.3). Visit checklist: meet the academic advisor; ask how many players at your position are returning; ask for the aid terms in writing (they can't write an aid offer until 2028-11-15); score the visit the same night (tracker below).

## 7. Written offers and signing
- A verbal "offer" is not binding on you or the school.
- Written aid offers can't come before 2028-11-15 of senior year; you can't sign the acceptance until the signing period (D1 Bylaw 13.9.3.3, 13.02.13.1).
- Signing ends other schools' recruiting of you. Read the offer sheet in the *Offers & Signing Day* playbook before signing.
- Ask compliance: is this aid for one year or multiple? What happens if the head coach leaves? What settlement-related benefits, if any, are in writing?
