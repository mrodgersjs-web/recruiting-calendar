### Walk-On Playbook
- Contents: Preferred vs tryout walk-on; Roster limits after House; What to get in writing; Admissions timeline

### JUCO to D1

### Late Bloomer Playbook

### Highlight Reel & Social Playbook
- Contents: Reel structure by position; One-page profile; Social audit; What not to post; Coach follow tracking (manual)

### Camp ROI Playbook
- Contents: Camp types; When camp recruiting talk opens (by sport); Cost budget; Post-camp follow-up

### NIL for High Schoolers
