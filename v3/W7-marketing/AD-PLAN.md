- Supporting pages: /compare/, /playbooks/late-bloomer/, /story/<slug>/

Claim rules applied to every line: no scholarship or offer promises; no "guaranteed"; no false deadlines or countdowns; any rule or date must match site/data.js with its bylaw; no counts we can't source (we don't say "thousands of families"); stories are named adult athletes with sourced facts or labeled composites.

Meta rules (2026): audience is parents/adults 25+; **no targeting of anyone under 18** and no age-under-18 interest proxies; no "Are you a junior?" second-person to the athlete (we address the parent). Use broad + interest targeting (parenting, high school sports, the sport) and Advantage+ placement; Special Ad Category not required (not credit/housing/employment). No ages, grades or other personal traits implied about the viewer ("Are you a single mom?" violates personal-attributes policy; we say "Doing this on your own?" style only if it does not assert the trait; safer to avoid).

Kill rule default (from plan §5.2 benchmarks: Meta sports lead CPL $15.49-$19.30, CPC ~$1.07): after $60 spend or 3 days, kill if CTR < 0.8% or cost per free signup > $20; for the $9 offer kill if no purchase after $100.
