## 1. NCAA Division I baseball: when a coach may call, text, email, DM

- [OFFICIAL] Calls from a coach: "In baseball, telephone calls to an individual (or the individual's family members) may not be made before August 1 at the beginning of the individual's junior year in high school." After that date calls are at the school's discretion. Bylaw 13.1.3.1.1 (adopted 4/26/23). Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] The general D1 rule for other sports is June 15 at the end of sophomore year (Bylaw 13.1.3.1). Baseball is later than the general rule. Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Calls TO a coach are also restricted in baseball: "an institutional staff member may not receive telephone calls from an individual (or the individual's family members) before August 1 at the beginning of the individual's junior year in high school." Bylaw 13.1.3.2.3 (adopted 4/26/23). Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Email, text and other electronic correspondence, and mailed recruiting materials: in baseball a school "shall not provide recruiting materials, including general correspondence related to athletics, or send electronic correspondence" until August 1 at the beginning of the junior year. Bylaw 13.4.1.1 (adopted 4/26/23). General rule for other sports is June 15 after sophomore year (Bylaw 13.4.1). Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [UNCONFIRMED] Social-media direct messages: the manual's term is "electronic correspondence". I did not pull the sub-bylaw that lists DMs by name. The NCAA recruiting page says only "Messages through social media may be part of the recruiting process." Source: https://www.ncaa.org/eligibility-center/recruiting . Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] No calls during the school's own baseball game (from team report time to dismissal). Bylaw 13.1.3.2.1. Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [DERIVED] For the class of 2029, "August 1 at the beginning of the junior year" is 1 August 2027. This date is computed from the bylaw text and the class year; the NCAA does not print it. Source: Bylaws 13.1.3.1.1, 13.1.3.2.3, 13.4.1.1, D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.

## 2. NCAA Division I baseball: in-person off-campus contact and the contact limit

- [OFFICIAL] Off-campus contact: "In baseball, off-campus recruiting contacts shall not be made with an individual (or the individual's family members) before September 1 at the beginning of the individual's junior year in high school." Bylaw 13.1.1.1.1 (adopted 4/18/18, revised 4/26/23 and 6/24/26). The general rule for other sports is August 1 of junior year (Bylaw 13.1.1.1). Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [UNCONFIRMED] What Bylaw 13.1.1.1.1 said before the 6/24/26 revision was not read. Pages written before June 2026 may show a different baseball date.
- [OFFICIAL] Baseball-specific limit: "each institution is limited to seven recruiting opportunities (contacts and evaluations combined) per prospective student-athlete per year ... Beginning September 1 of a prospective student-athlete's junior year of high school, not more than three of the seven opportunities may be off-campus contacts each year, including contacts with the prospective student-athlete's family members." A contact during an official or unofficial visit does not count. Bylaw 13.1.5.2 (revised 4/15/26). Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] How the seven are counted: evaluations from September 1 through May 31 count (except one on the same day as a permitted contact); "From June 1 through August 31, evaluations do not count against the annual number of recruiting opportunities." Contacts count in every month. Bylaw 13.1.5.11. Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] On a day when a permitted in-person off-campus contact happens, staff may make unlimited calls to that prospect. Bylaw 13.1.3.2.2. Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [DERIVED] For the class of 2029, "September 1 at the beginning of the junior year" is 1 September 2027 (computed, not printed by the NCAA). Source: Bylaw 13.1.1.1.1. Checked 2026-11-11.

## 3. NCAA Division I baseball recruiting calendar, 2026-27 (the only year published)

- [OFFICIAL] Where the dates live: Bylaw 13.17 no longer prints sport calendars; it says to "refer to the recruiting calendars published on ncaa.org" (revised 10/7/26). Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Baseball has its own D1 calendar with four labels: contact period, quiet period, dead period, recruiting shutdown. The 2026-27 baseball calendar has NO period labelled "evaluation period". Source: D1 Baseball Calendar. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Contact periods 2026-27: August 1-16, 2026; September 11 - October 11, 2026; March 1 - July 31, 2027. Source: D1 Baseball Calendar. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Quiet periods 2026-27: August 17 - September 10, 2026; October 12, 2026 - February 28, 2027. Source: D1 Baseball Calendar. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Dead periods 2026-27: November 9-12, 2026; January 7-10, 2027; May 31 - June 7, 2027; June 19-21, 2027; July 3-5, 2027. Source: D1 Baseball Calendar. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Recruiting shutdowns 2026-27: November 24-29, 2026; December 22-27, 2026. Source: D1 Baseball Calendar. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [DATA] Method note: the PDF's text layer lists the date ranges grouped by label (3 contact, 2 shutdown, 2 quiet, 5 dead). I matched dates to labels from that grouping and did not view the colour grid. The same mapping appears on NCSA's listing. Source: D1 Baseball Calendar; https://www.ncsasports.org/baseball/recruiting-rules-calendar . Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Definitions printed on the calendar: contact period = staff may "make in-person, off-campus recruiting contacts and evaluations". Quiet period = in-person recruiting contacts "only on the member institution's campus. No in-person, off-campus recruiting contacts or evaluations". Dead period = no in-person contacts or evaluations on or off campus and no official or unofficial visits. Recruiting shutdown = "no form of recruiting (e.g., contacts, evaluations, official or unofficial visits, correspondence or making or receiving telephone calls) is permissible." Source: D1 Baseball Calendar. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] So calls and messages continue in a dead period but stop in a recruiting shutdown. Source: definitions on the D1 Baseball Calendar. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] The November dead period sits on the week of the signing date: the 2026-27 initial signing date for "All Other Division I and II Sports" is November 11, 2026. Source: https://www.ncaa.org/eligibility-center/recruiting . Checked 2026-11-11.
- [UNCONFIRMED] 2027-28 and 2028-29 baseball calendars (the junior and senior years of the class of 2029, including the summer before senior year) are not published. Do not reuse the 2026-27 dates for those years.
- [OFFICIAL] Summer pattern in the one published year: the whole of March 1 - July 31 is a contact period broken by three dead periods, and August 1-16 is a contact period. Evaluations made June 1 - August 31 do not count toward the seven-opportunity limit (Bylaw 13.1.5.11). Source: D1 Baseball Calendar; D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.

## 4. NCAA Division I baseball: camps (a baseball wrinkle)

- [OFFICIAL] Recruiting talk at camps: "In baseball and women's lacrosse, recruiting conversations during an institutional camp or clinic ... are not permitted between an institution's coach or off-campus recruiter and a participating individual before September 1 ... at the beginning of the individual's junior year in high school." Bylaw 13.12.1.5.2 (revised 6/24/26, 6/29/26 effective 8/1/26). Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] During a quiet period a baseball staff member may work "only at the institution's camps or clinics that occur on the institution's campus or at a facility regularly used by the institution for practice or competition." Bylaw 13.12.1.2.1 (adopted 5/4/20, revised 6/24/26). Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Outside a quiet period a baseball staff member may work a privately owned camp if it runs under the same rules as a school camp (open to any and all entrants, publicly advertised at least 14 days before). Bylaw 13.12.2.2.2. Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.

## 5. NCAA Division II (all sports, baseball included)

- [OFFICIAL] Communication: "Communication with a prospective student-athlete or their relative or legal guardian(s) shall be made at any time." Bylaw 13.1.3.1 (revised 7/23/24 effective 8/1/24). The only timing limit in that bylaw is on boosters (not before June 15 preceding junior year, Bylaw 13.1.3.1.1). Source: D2 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] In-person contact: "Contact shall not be made with a prospective student-athlete or a prospective student-athlete's relatives or legal guardians before June 15 immediately preceding the prospective student-athlete's junior year in high school." Bylaw 13.1.1.1. Source: D2 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] No baseball contact/quiet/evaluation calendar was found in D2 Bylaw 13. The dead period found is the signing-date dead period: "The period 48 hours before 7 a.m. on the date for signing in the applicable sport" (Bylaws 13.02.10.1.1 and 13.02.10.1.2, revised 1/16/26 effective 8/1/26). Calls and messages stay allowed in a D2 dead period (Bylaw 13.02.10). Source: D2 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] D2 signing date for baseball: "First Date for All Other Sports. The second Wednesday in November." Bylaw 13.02.10.1(c). Source: D2 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Tryouts are allowed in D2: a school may hold one tryout per prospect, on campus or at its normal practice site, "beginning June 15 immediately preceding the prospective student-athlete's junior year in high school." Bylaw 13.8.2.1. Source: D2 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [DERIVED] For the class of 2029, "June 15 immediately preceding the junior year" is 15 June 2027 (computed). Source: D2 Bylaws 13.1.1.1, 13.3.1.2, 13.8.2.1. Checked 2026-11-11.

## 6. NCAA Division III

- [OFFICIAL] Calls, email, texts: "There are no restrictions on the timing for electronic communication (e.g., telephone call, electronic mail, Instant Messenger, text messages or facsimiles) to prospective student-athletes." Bylaw 13.02.10.1 (adopted 8/4/21). Source: D3 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] In-person off-campus contact: not "until the prospective student-athlete has completed their sophomore year in high school." Bylaw 13.1.1.1. Source: D3 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] No contact with a prospect on a competition day until the prospect is released by the coach; no electronic messages at the competition site from staff who are attending. Bylaw 13.1.3.1. Source: D3 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.

## 8. Junior college (NJCAA national rule)

- [OFFICIAL] Off-campus recruiting: "Athletic department staff may visit a prospective student-athlete at any location for recruiting purposes." - [OFFICIAL] No contact is allowed with an NJCAA athlete who is in season at another member college or who "has signed an NJCAA Letter of Intent." Section 4, D.1. Source: NJCAA Bylaws (10-27-26), p. 53. Checked 2026-11-11.

## 9. Official (paid) and unofficial visits, by level

### Division I baseball
- [OFFICIAL] First paid visit: "In baseball and women's lacrosse, an individual may not be provided an expense-paid visit earlier than September 1 ... of the individual's junior year in high school." Bylaw 13.6.2.1.2 (revised 6/24/26, 6/29/26 effective 8/1/26). Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] One paid visit per school before October 15 after high school graduation (a second is allowed after that date; one extra after a head-coach change). Bylaws 13.6.2.2 and 13.6.2.2.1. Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [UNCONFIRMED] Whether a prospect has a cap on the total number of D1 official visits across all schools: Bylaw 13.6.2.2.1 points to "Bylaw 13.6.2.2.2" but that text was not found in my search.
- [OFFICIAL] Before a paid visit the prospect must give the school a transcript, register with the NCAA Eligibility Center and be on the school's institutional request list. Bylaw 13.6.3. Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Who pays what: the school may pay the prospect's actual transportation (Bylaw 13.5.2.1); "not ... more than two consecutive nights of lodging" (Bylaw 13.6.4); lodging and meals for the prospect "and up to four family members" within 30 miles of campus (Bylaw 13.6.6); entertainment for the prospect and up to four family members within 30 miles (Bylaw 13.6.7.1). Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Tickets on a paid visit: "a maximum of five complimentary admissions to a home athletics event", general seating only, no press box, special box or bench area. Bylaw 13.6.7.2. Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] First unofficial visit with athletics involvement: "In baseball and women's lacrosse, an unofficial visit with athletics department involvement (e.g., contact with athletics department staff, athletics-specific tour, complimentary admission) shall not occur ... before September 1 ... at the beginning of the individual's junior year in high school." Bylaw 13.7.1.2. Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Unofficial visits are unlimited in number and at the family's own expense (Bylaw 13.7.2). The school "may not pay any expenses or provide any entertainment except a maximum of three complimentary admissions" to a home event; none in a dead period. Bylaw 13.7.3.1. Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] No visits of either kind in a dead period or a recruiting shutdown. Source: D1 Baseball Calendar definitions. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [UNCONFIRMED] Whether the school may pay family members' travel on a D1 official visit was not read.

### Division II
- [OFFICIAL] First paid visit: not "earlier than June 15 immediately preceding the prospective student-athlete's junior year in high school." Bylaw 13.3.1.2. One paid visit per school (Bylaw 13.3.1.1). Length: "shall not exceed 48 hours" (Bylaw 13.3.3). Source: D2 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] A transcript (official or unofficial) is required before a paid visit. Bylaw 13.3.2.1(a). Source: D2 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Unofficial visits: unlimited, at own expense (Bylaw 13.4.1). The school may give complimentary admissions to a home event for the prospect and those with the prospect; no bench-area seating (Bylaw 13.4.2.1.1). No count of tickets is printed in the passage read. Source: D2 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] No official or unofficial visits during the signing-date dead period (Bylaw 13.02.10). Source: D2 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.

### Division III
- [OFFICIAL] First paid visit: not "earlier than January 1 of the prospective student-athlete's junior year in high school." Bylaw 13.6.1.1.1. One paid visit per school (Bylaw 13.6.1.1). Length: not more than 48 hours (Bylaw 13.6.2). Source: D3 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Unofficial visits: unlimited, and "A prospective student-athlete may make unofficial visits before January of their junior year in high school." Bylaw 13.7.1. Complimentary admissions to a home event are allowed on official and unofficial visits (Bylaws 13.6.5.2, 13.7.2.1). Source: D3 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.

### NAIA
- [OFFICIAL] The school pays travel, meals or lodging for a visiting prospect only if that is "a part of the general institutional policy in procurement of other students with special talents and not only for the express purpose of securing athletes." No dollar limit; it must be similar to what non-athletes get. NAIA Handbook 2025-2026, Bylaws Article II, Section C, item 3 and casebook "Expenses Paid by Institution". Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] No NAIA start date for visits was found in Article II, Sections C-D. Source: NAIA Handbook 2025-2026. Checked 2026-11-11.

### NJCAA
- [OFFICIAL] First paid visit: "starting on June 1 prior to their junior year in high school." One paid visit per prospect per school per recruiting year. Not more than 48 hours. School may pay for the prospect "plus two guests": round-trip direct transportation, meals, lodging, and entertainment at the scale of normal student life in the local community. Section 4, B.1.a-B.1.f. Source: NJCAA Bylaws (10-27-26), p. 52. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Tickets: on an official visit the college "may not provide a prospective student-athlete with admission to institution's athletic events in a manner other than that of the general public." Section 4, B.1.f.(4).(a). Source: NJCAA Bylaws (10-27-26), p. 52. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] Unofficial visit: no expense or benefit; "up to two unofficial visits that include athletic participation with team or current student-athletes"; no limit on unofficial visits without athletic participation. Section 4, B.2. Source: NJCAA Bylaws (10-27-26), p. 52. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [DERIVED] For the class of 2029, "June 1 prior to the junior year" is 1 June 2027 (computed). Source: Section 4, B.1.a. Checked 2026-11-11.

## 10. Rules found that differ from what recruiting pages often print

- [OFFICIAL] Division II communication is open "at any time" (D2 Bylaw 13.1.3.1, effective 8/1/24). June 15 before junior year is the D2 date for in-person contact, paid visits and tryouts, not for calls or texts. I did not capture a named recruiting page printing the older rule in this pass. Source: D2 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] D1 baseball "dead period" and "recruiting shutdown" are different things: messages are allowed in the first and banned in the second. Source: D1 Baseball Calendar. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] In D1 baseball a prospect phoning the coach before August 1 of junior year does not get around the rule: the coach may not take the call (Bylaw 13.1.3.2.3). Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] "Unofficial visits can happen any time" is not the D1 baseball rule: an unofficial visit with athletics department involvement cannot happen before September 1 of junior year (Bylaw 13.7.1.2). Source: D1 Manual. Checked 2026-11-11.
- [OFFICIAL] The NAIA's own pages disagree on tryouts (two days per school vs two days in a whole career); both are printed in section 7.
