What we could not settle

Each line below is tagged UNCONFIRMED in this book's research, or is a place where two sources disagree.

  • His own calendar and his own signing date. The 2027-28 and 2028-29 Division I sheets are not published. Each PROJECTED date in Chapter 3 is arithmetic, and each can move.
  • The camp rules. Division I Bylaw 13.12 was read by title only. So were the exceptions at 13.11.2 and 13.11.3, and the camp headings at 13.1.3.1.13 and 13.4.1.10 to .12.
  • Social media messages. Bylaw 13.4.1 says "electronic correspondence." The words "direct message" are not in the Division I Manual. The NCAA guide has a row for social media that was not copied out.
  • A season-long ban on contact. None was found. Only the match-day rules exist.
  • The Eligibility Center fee. $110 and $170 in the checklist. $120 and $180 on the live screen.
  • NAIA tryout days. Two days at each school on one NAIA page. Two days in a career on another. The rule's own text is in the 2026-2027 handbook. This book has not yet printed which reading it backs.
  • Junior college contact and visits. The national rule was not read at its source. One member college's 2026 paper quotes a paid visit from 1 June before junior year, one a year, 48 hours at most. A 2017 brochure says after junior year, two days and two nights. A SERVICE site says there is no recruiting calendar. Two-year bodies outside the NJCAA were not checked.
  • How many NAIA and junior college teams. No official count was found. The one figure seen was a 2020 slide from one regional soccer body: 214 NAIA, and 160 and 81 in two junior college divisions. It is too old to print in Chapter 2.
  • Two team counts. 827 NCAA men's teams in 2024-25. 823 projected for 2026-27. Both are official.
  • Two club counts. 310 on the league's page. 318 on a parent site.
  • How many paid visits he may take in all. No heading in Division I Bylaw 13.6.2 sets a total. The one limit read is one visit a school.
  • Amateur rules below Division I. The Division II sentence on pay before college was not found. The junior college rule was not opened. The Division II and III rules on outside teams were not read.
  • Where the pro rules meet the college rules. How the league's draft rule and NCAA Bylaw 12.2.3.2.6 work for one player. What the 11 amateur roster slots in MLS NEXT Pro pay. How FIFA training compensation lands on a US family. The second-tier pro leagues were not read at all.
  • Other gaps. A newer two-year transfer share. A Division II roster limit. The rest of the Division II "60 total awards" sentence. What men's soccer graduates go on to do. Any change to the Division I roster number after 2026-27. The rules of other boys club leagues, and if an academy player may also play for his high school.
