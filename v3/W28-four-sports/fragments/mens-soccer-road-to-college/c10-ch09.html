9. Checklist by grade

One list for each year. He is in 10th grade now, so start there, and look back at 9th for anything not yet done. Each line names the chapter that has the rule.

9th grade

  • Open the free Profile Page at the Eligibility Center. The NCAA's guide says to register before ninth grade. (Chapter 7)
  • Plan all 16 core courses with his counselor. That means four years of English and three of math at Algebra I or higher. (Chapter 7)
  • Sign nothing with a pro club or its academy until a parent has read the pay line. (Chapter 7)
  • Ask your state association if he may play club during the high school season. (Chapter 4)
  • Start one folder for match video and one for report cards.

10th grade: this year

  • Write to Division II, Division III and NAIA coaches now. They may write back today. (Chapter 3)
  • Build his list across all five levels. Count the seats on each roster page. (Chapter 2)
  • Do not pay for a "tryout" with a Division I staff. (Chapter 4)
  • Ask the club for its full-year fee sheet, in writing. (Chapter 5)
  • Get the email in Chapter 6 ready before June.
  • Open the paid Eligibility Center account before his first paid visit. (Chapter 7)
  • On 15 June 2027, send the full round. That date is worked out from the rule. (Chapter 3)

11th grade

  • From 1 August 2027, Division I coaches may meet him off campus and pay for a visit. That date is worked out from the rule. (Chapter 3)
  • Send each Division I school his transcript before a paid visit. (Chapter 3)
  • Tell him the match-day rule before his first big event. (Chapter 4)
  • Use Division II tryouts with care: one at each school. Count his NAIA tryout days. (Chapter 4)
  • Book no visit for 8 to 11 November 2027 until the new sheet is out. (Chapter 3)
  • From 1 January 2028, ask Division III schools for a paid visit. (Chapter 3)
  • Ask each coach the roster question: the 28, or the old 9.9? (Chapter 2)
  • In the spring, count his core courses. He needs 10 done before senior year, seven of them in English, math and science. (Chapter 7)

12th grade

  • Ask each serious Division I or II school if it has asked the Eligibility Center to certify him. (Chapter 7)
  • In September 2028, ask for the amount, what it covers and the signing date, in one email. (Chapter 3)
  • Ask the five questions in Chapter 7 before he signs anything.
  • Book no visit for 6 to 9 November 2028. (Chapter 3)
  • The fall signing date for his class works out to Wednesday 8 November 2028. Read the 2028-29 sheet when the NCAA prints it. (Chapter 3)
  • If the offer is from a junior college, sign inside 21 days or ask for a new letter. (Chapter 7)
  • If a pro club calls, read the last box in Chapter 7 before he answers.
  • Finish all 16 core courses and graduate.

The one-page version

Know the roster number. Know the dates. Send one honest email. Ask for the paper. Sign nothing that pays him more than his real costs. Everything else in this book is detail for one of those five.
