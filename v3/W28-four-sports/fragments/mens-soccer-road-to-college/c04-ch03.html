The seven dates this book worked out

None of these is printed by the NCAA for his class. Each is arithmetic from the rule next to it. The two November dead periods lean on two things at once. One is the second-Wednesday rule. The other is the 2026-27 sheet's own wording: "Monday through Thursday of the initial week for the fall signing date for athletics aid agreements." They are the ones most likely to move.

  • 15 June 2027: Division I Bylaws 13.1.3.1 and 13.4.1; Division II Bylaws 13.1.1.1, 13.3.1.2 and 13.8.2.1.
  • The last day of 10th grade: Division III Bylaw 13.1.1.1.
  • 1 August 2027: Division I Bylaws 13.1.1.1, 13.6.2.1.1, 13.7.1.1 and 13.1.5.1.
  • 8 to 11 November 2027: Division I Bylaw 13.02.13.1(f), and the wording on the 2026-27 Division I calendar.
  • 1 January 2028: Division III Bylaw 13.6.1.1.1.
  • 6 to 9 November 2028: the same two sources as November 2027, and Division II Bylaws 13.02.10, 13.02.10.1.1 and 13.02.10.1.2.
  • Wednesday 8 November 2028: Division I Bylaw 13.02.13.1(f) and Division II Bylaw 13.02.10.1.

What a missed date costs

A boy who has not signed up with the Eligibility Center may not take a Division I paid visit (Bylaw 13.6.3). A visit booked inside a dead period cannot happen. A boy who is short of 10 core courses before his seventh semester misses the Division I 10/7 rule, and no summer of soccer fixes that. Put the dates on one page and the page on the fridge.
