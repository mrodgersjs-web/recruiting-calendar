6. What to send, and what good video looks like

One short email, from her, with two links. Division II, Division III and NAIA coaches may answer it at any time. Before 15 June after her sophomore year, a Division I coach may only send a short reply that does not recruit her. It can explain the rules or point her to admissions. From that day the coach may answer in full. Division I Manual, Bylaws 13.4.1 and 13.4.1.13.

Everything in this sample is invented: the player, the school, the coach, the numbers and the links. Sample emails in this book name no real school, club, league, camp or service.

Why it is written this way

  • She writes it. It goes out from her own mailbox, in her own words.
  • Class year first. Every date in the rules hangs on her class year. A coach needs it before anything else.
  • Grades next. Division I and Division II each ask for 16 core courses. Chapter 7 has the rest.
  • The NCAA ID. She needs an Eligibility Center account before a Division I school may pay for a visit or send an offer. Division I Manual, Bylaws 13.6.3 and 13.9.2.
  • The roster question. At a settlement school the limit is 28 players. The answer tells her which rule the school expects to be under. Schools choose each year, so she asks again before she enrolls. See Chapter 2.
  • The time zone. She asks where the coach's clock is. She does not name a place.
  • No phone call to Division I yet. Before 15 June after sophomore year, a Division I coach may not take her call. Division I Manual, Bylaw 13.1.3.2.5.

What good video looks like

No rule book says what a film must hold. This book read no NCAA, NAIA or NJCAA paper that sets a length or a format. It also holds no survey of soccer coaches on video. So the list below is this book's own view, and nothing more. The best guide is the coach: ask what the staff wants to see.

  • Open with her name, class year, position, shirt number and shirt color.
  • Put her best clips first.
  • Use game film. Show a few seconds before and after her touch.
  • Mark her on the screen before each clip starts.
  • Keep a full game ready to send when a coach asks.
  • Use one link with no login. Test it on a phone that is not signed in.
