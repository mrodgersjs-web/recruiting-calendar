Nothing else is printed for this sport. The calendar lists four dead periods for women's soccer and no other kind of period. It prints no quiet period, no evaluation period and no recruiting shutdown. So in 2026-27 a Division I coach may watch and meet recruits from 12 August to 15 December, except 9 to 12 November, and again from 7 January to 27 July. The limit of seven chances in Chapter 4 still applies. The calendar gives a reason for the November dates only.

Two things these dates do to your money

The August trip. Many guides say visits open on 1 August of junior year. For women's soccer in 2026 that day was inside the 1 to 11 August dead period. The first day a Division I coach could meet a girl in person was 12 August. Phone and text stayed open the whole time. Do not pay for flights until the new calendar is out.

The holiday showcase. From 16 December to 6 January a Division I coach cannot watch or host. So holiday showcases in that window go largely unwatched by Division I staff. If the goal is Division I eyes, that entry fee and that hotel bill buy very little.
