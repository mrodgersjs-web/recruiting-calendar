What we could not settle

  • Which schools opted in. No list was found from the NCAA or the College Sports Commission of the schools outside the defendant conferences that opted in for 2026-27. Ask each school.
  • Her own calendars. The Division I calendars for 2027-28 and 2028-29 are not published. So the exact dead periods for her junior and senior years are not known. The calendar also prints no rule for three of its four dead periods.
  • Her own signing date. The 8 November 2028 date is this book's sum from the second-Wednesday rule. No 2028-29 signing sheet exists yet.
  • How many NAIA teams there are. No official count by sport was found. No official average squad was found for NAIA or junior college, and no split of the 264 junior college teams by division.
  • The NAIA tryout limit. Two NAIA pages disagree: two days at each school, or two days in a career. NAIA rules on who pays for a visit outside a tryout were not found.
  • The NAIA's academic criteria. The NAIA page says aid to an "academically gifted" student can sit outside the 12. It prints no grade or score for that.
  • Junior college first contact. No NJCAA rule on the first day for calls, texts or email was found in the text read. Outside guides say a recruit must finish junior year before she signs. That bylaw was not found either.
  • Test scores. A College Board page still shows a scale of grades and test scores. The NCAA brochure text read for this book lists classes, timing and grades only. This was not settled.
  • Verbal offers. A search of Division I Bylaw 13 found no rule that names them.
  • Division II and Division III. No soccer recruiting calendar was found for Division II. The rules on playing for pay were read for Division I only.
  • How many players come from junior college. The NCAA prints it by sport on a page this book did not open. That is why no such figure is in this book.
  • Club and showcase costs. No cost per family was found on an official public page. Overnight camp prices were not collected. No official figure was found for how many college players came through a club.
  • Video. No rule book and no coach survey read for this book sets a standard for film. The list in Chapter 6 is this book's own view.
  • The pro league's fine print. The league's papers give two limits for players under 18 on one team, four and two. The 2026 rules for those players were not read.
  • Other pro leagues. No pay floor or roster size was found for the second league. Its own pages disagree on its calendar. A third league has start years of 2026, 2027 and 2028 in print. Pay in leagues abroad was not looked up.
  • Teams added since 1972. No count with a 1972 start was found, so none is printed.
