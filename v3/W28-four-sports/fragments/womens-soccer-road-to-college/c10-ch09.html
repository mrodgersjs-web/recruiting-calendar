9. Checklist by grade

One list for each year. A class of 2029 player is in 10th grade now, so start there and look back at 9th for anything missed. Dates marked PROJECTED come from Chapter 3.

9th grade

  • Start counting her 16 core courses. Division I gives eight semesters from the start of ninth grade.
  • Open the free Profile Page with the NCAA.
  • Put the four dead periods on the family calendar.
  • Know who may write to her now. Division II, Division III and NAIA coaches may. A Division I coach may not.
  • Start a list of schools at all five levels.

10th grade

  • Note 15 June 2027, PROJECTED. It is the first day a Division I coach may call, text or email her. Before that day the coach may only send a short reply that does not recruit her. It can explain the rules or point her to admissions.
  • Do not phone a Division I coach before that day. The coach may not take the call.
  • Write the email in Chapter 6 and build her film.
  • Skip any holiday showcase bought only for Division I eyes. The dead period runs 16 December to 6 January.
  • Move up to the NCAA certification account before her first Division I paid visit.
  • Note 1 June 2027, PROJECTED. A junior college may pay for a visit from that day.
  • Ask her club for last year's full bill before the next season starts.

11th grade

  • Note 1 August 2027, PROJECTED. By rule it is the first day for Division I visits and meetings away from campus. Check the new calendar before booking.
  • Before a paid visit, send her transcript and ask to be put on the school's request list.
  • Take one paid visit per school. There is no cap on the number of schools.
  • Ask each Division I school if it opted in to the settlement for the year she would enroll. Schools choose each year.
  • Note 1 January 2028, PROJECTED. Division III schools may pay for a visit from that day.
  • Have ten core courses done before her seventh semester starts.
  • Ask each coach how many roster spots moved after the transfer windows.

12th grade

  • Note 1 August 2028, PROJECTED. A Division I school may send the offer in writing from that day.
  • Fill in the eligibility questionnaire before a Division I offer can be sent.
  • Note 8 November 2028, PROJECTED. It is the first day she may sign, from 7 a.m.
  • Read the offer before that day: the dollars, the years, and the last day to sign.
  • Ask the NCAA for her final athletics certification. That request opens in senior year.
  • For Division III, know that the signing form is a keepsake. It does not bind her.
  • For junior college, the letter of intent may be signed from 1 November.
  • Plan a short summer. A Division I player can be due on campus in mid to late July.

Every year, the same three checks

Check her classes against the NCAA's core-course list with her counselor. Check her club and school dates against the dead periods. And check this book's PROJECTED dates against the newest NCAA calendar and signing sheet. Her high school dates come from your state association.
