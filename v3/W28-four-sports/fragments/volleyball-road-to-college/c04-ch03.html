What a missed date costs

A late transcript upload delays certification, and a certification that is not final means a signed paper may not hold. A missed regional qualifier can cost the whole summer's showcase in Las Vegas, and bidding down from another event can run as late as the first week of May. A girl who is not measured, not registered and not on a schedule by the spring of 11th grade starts the year that decides everything with a handicap no money can fully fix.
