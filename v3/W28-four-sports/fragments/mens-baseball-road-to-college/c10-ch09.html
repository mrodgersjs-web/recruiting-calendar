9. Checklist by grade

One row per year, in order. Cross items off. If a year slips, the fix is most often more schools and one more month, not more money.

YearDo this
9th grade
done		Play, and keep every grade. Ask his counselor which of his classes the NCAA counts as core courses. Spend nothing on recruiting.
10th grade
this year		Read Chapter 2 before you pay for anything. Build the list in Chapter 5, with all five levels on it. Open the free NCAA Profile Page account. Film his games from a stand. Measure him, and write down the tool, the date and who measured. Send the Chapter 6 email to Division II, Division III and NAIA coaches, who may answer at any time. Do not have him phone a Division I coach. Do not buy a Division I camp to be recruited.
Summer after
10th grade		From 1 June 2027 a junior college may pay for a visit. From 15 June 2027 a Division II coach may meet him, pay for a visit and try him out. Once 10th grade ends, a Division III coach may meet him off campus. On 1 August 2027 send the email to Division I coaches and ask for a call time. All four dates are PROJECTED from the rules in Chapter 3.
11th gradeFrom 1 September 2027 a Division I coach may meet him and host a visit. Before a paid Division I visit, send a transcript, register with the Eligibility Center, and get on the school's request list. From 1 January 2028 a Division III school may pay for a visit. Both dates are PROJECTED. Send his grades each term and his schedule before each event. In spring, count his core courses with his counselor. Ten must be done before 12th grade. Look up the 2027-28 Division I calendar when it is printed.
Summer after
11th grade		Send one update with new video and new numbers. Ask each staff the Chapter 2 questions: did you opt in, and how many players will you add? Ask what the offer is, in writing. Look up the 2028-29 Division I calendar when it is printed.
12th grade,
fall		Ask each recruiting school to request his file at the Eligibility Center. From 1 November 2028 he could sign a junior college letter, if the chart keeps its date. From Wednesday 8 November 2028 he may sign a written offer of athletics aid. Both dates are PROJECTED. Get the five answers in Chapter 7 before he signs.
After signingFinish all 16 core courses and graduate. Make sure the Center has what it needs for his final certification. Without it he may practice for up to 45 days and may not play. At an opted-in Division I school, ask again whether he is on the roster due by 1 December.

Three things to keep on one page

Keep the roster question, the dates and his schedule on one page. The roster question is in Chapter 2. The dates are in Chapter 3. The schedule is his own, with fields and times, sent before each event. Each paid item in Chapter 8 comes after those three.
