The Division I baseball calendars for 2027-28 and 2028-29 are not printed yet. Those are his junior and senior years. So this book prints no contact, quiet, dead or shutdown dates for them. Do not reuse this year's dates.

What a missed date costs

A file that is not final keeps him out of games. Without a final certification a player may practice for no more than 45 days, and he may not play. Those are Bylaws 12.1.1.1.3 and 12.1.1.1.3.1. A player with no transcript, no Eligibility Center account, or no place on the request list gets no paid Division I visit. And the 10 core courses are counted before 12th grade starts, then locked.
