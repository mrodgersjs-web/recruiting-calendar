What we could not settle

Three items below carry the mark UNCONFIRMED. It means a claim that this book has no document for yet.

  • Her own calendars. The 2027-28 and 2028-29 Division I softball calendars are not published. Neither are the signing dates for those years. Every date for her junior and senior years is PROJECTED.
  • The 2028 signing week. The date of 8 November 2028 is this book's arithmetic from the second-Wednesday rule. The dead period around it is not worked out, because no rule text for it was read.
  • Texts and direct messages in Division I. UNCONFIRMED The manual says only "electronic correspondence" and names no list of channels. So this book does not print them as covered, or as free. Ask the school's compliance office how it reads the term. Division I Manual, Bylaw 13.4.1.
  • How many official visits she may take in all. One Division I bylaw points to another for that limit. The second bylaw's number does not appear as a heading in the manual text read. No total cap was found.
  • Which Division I schools opted in. No list was found of schools outside the five defendant conferences that chose the settlement rules. Ask each school.
  • Designated Student-Athletes. The commission's page says players given that title in summer 2025 do not count against the 25-player limit. The text this book read does not say what the title is for.
  • NAIA tryouts. The handbook says two days per school. Two NAIA web pages say two days in a whole career. Not settled.
  • NAIA test scores. The policy prints two SAT lines, 1130 and 1270, and 1140 and 1280. Not settled.
  • NAIA letters of intent. The handbook has no such bylaw. Whether one NAIA conference adds its own letter or its own recruiting rule was not checked.
  • The subject split of the 16 core courses. It is a picture table in the guide and was not read again for this book.
  • Junior college team counts. UNCONFIRMED The NJCAA's statistics site was turned off and its new team list did not load. The only number in hand is one company's 364.
  • The NAIA team count. A hand count found 194, and 192 with games played. One company says 197. The dates differ, which may be why.
  • Two-year colleges outside the NJCAA. Their rule books were not opened.
  • How big travel softball is. The governing body prints two sets of numbers. No count was found for the travel-ball membership bodies.
  • Players recruited per team per year. No association publishes it. Only the squad arithmetic in Chapter 2 exists.
  • A softball-only family cost, and travel team dues. Not found. The spending figures in Chapter 8 are for all sports.
  • The camp colleges' levels. The camp pages do not print the division, so Chapter 8 leaves it out.
  • Measures by position. Company targets only. The companies disagree, and none prints how many players it measured. The coaches' group's own test sheet was not opened.
  • Which positions commit first. No source with authority was found.
  • Pro pay, other pro leagues, and the Olympics. UNCONFIRMED None could be checked.
  • How junior college seasons count against the five years. Not read. Ask the school.
  • What former players do next. No softball-only data was found on coaching, medicine or other careers.
  • A newer high school handbook. The 2024-25 edition was the newest one that could be opened.
