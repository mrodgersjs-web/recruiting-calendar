9. Checklist by grade

One row per stretch of time, in order. Cross items off. Dates in 2027 and 2028 are PROJECTED, as Chapter 3 explains, so check them again when the new calendars are printed.

WhenDo this
9th grade
done		Keep every grade. The 16 core courses count from the start of ninth grade. The NCAA guide says to register with the Eligibility Center before ninth grade. If that did not happen, do it this month.
10th grade
this year		Open the Eligibility Center account. Ask the counselor which of her classes are NCAA-approved core courses. Build her list at all five levels, the way Chapter 5 shows. Write to Division II, Division III, NAIA and junior college coaches, who may answer now. Do not read Division I silence as a no. Get her measured, with the tool and the date written down. Check event dates against the six fall weekends and the summer contact period before the team pays.
Summer 2027From 1 June a junior college may pay for a visit. From 15 June a Division II coach may meet her in person, and a Division II school may pay for a visit. Play in the summer contact period. A Division I coach's trips to watch her from 1 June through 31 August do not count against that school's seven.
11th grade
fall 2027		At 1 p.m. Central on 1 September 2027, Division I softball opens. Send the Chapter 6 email that week. Take calls. Do not book a visit inside a dead period. Before a paid Division I visit, send a transcript, have the certification account paid for, and be on the school's request list.
11th grade
winter and spring		From 1 January 2028 a Division III school may pay for a visit. Count her core courses with the counselor: ten of the 16 must be done before 12th grade starts, seven of them in English, math and science. Division I coaches may watch her school team this spring. They may also watch regional and state championship play, on dates set by your state association.
Summer 2028The June dead period and the summer contact period move with the Women's College World Series. Read the printed dates before booking travel. From 1 August 2028 a school may hand her a written offer about payments. She may not sign it yet.
12th grade
fall 2028		Ask each school for its number in writing in September. The junior college signing date is 1 November. The first day to sign a written offer of athletics aid in Division I or II is the second Wednesday in November, worked out as 8 November 2028. Expect a dead period that week.
After she signsFinish all 16 core courses and graduate. Keep the core-course grade average at 2.3 or higher for Division I, or 2.2 or higher for Division II. If she signed a junior college letter, mark 15 June of her first college year. That is the day the college must say if it will sign her again.

Proof for each line is in the chapter that covers it: Chapter 3 for dates, Chapter 4 for contact and visits, Chapter 7 for the paper. All read 11 November 2026.

Three dates for the fridge door

First, 1 September 2027 at 1 p.m. Central, when Division I softball may first call and write. Second, June 2028, when the dead period ends on the day the college season ends. Third, 8 November 2028, the first day to sign. All three are worked out from the rules. None is printed yet for her years.
