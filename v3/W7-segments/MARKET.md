## Read
- Biggest serviceable segment: football by volume (~61.8K est. families), then girls volleyball (~40.7K, highest-paying big sport), baseball (~39K).
- Growth + pay sweet spot: lacrosse, girls volleyball/beach, boys volleyball, golf. Growth but low pay: girls wrestling, girls flag football (cheap sports, but new college pathways means families have no playbook, high information need).
