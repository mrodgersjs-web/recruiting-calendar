# CTA and pricing-page scripts
Prices from research/00-business-plan.md; Mike approved proposed prices showing (2026-11-05). Checkout is test-mode / email capture until live Stripe keys: copy must say so honestly.
Banned: false scarcity ("only 3 left"), countdown timers, "price goes up tonight", shame ("don't let your kid fall behind"), confirmshaming on decline, vague "a coach viewed you" teasers, fake member counts.
