# Master story script framework (every athlete story on the site)
Applies to real sourced adult athletes (every fact = URL + date) and labeled composites ("Composite story built from N sourced recruiting paths"). No minors, no invented quotes, no generated likeness of a real person (BRIEF rules 1-3).
Binding barrier it is built against: trust (LATTICE.md). So the story's job is to *show the rule working*, not to sell the dream.

## The seven beats
| # | Beat | Content rule | Mechanism (why it works) | Boundary conditions (when it fails / is not allowed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HOOK: the ordinary start** | One concrete, sourced fact: sport, home state, school size, class year. "A 5A public school in Ohio. Nobody had been recruited out of her program in X years" ONLY if sourced. | Similarity / identifiable case: a specific person is processed more than a statistic; parents self-match on sport + school size. | Fails if the athlete is elite-from-birth (no self-match). Never invent the "nobody" stat. Composite must say composite in the hook frame, not just the footer. |
| 2 | **STAKES: what the family didn't know** | The real uncertainty: level fit (D1/D2/D3/NAIA/JUCO), money, timing. Framed as a question the parent also has (BP pain list: "Can my kid get recruited at all, and at what level?"). | Problem recognition: moves the problem from latent to named, in the parent's own words (PV Desire 2). | No fear inflation. No "most kids miss out" without a cited figure. No shame ("parents who wait fail their kids"). |
| 3 | **TURNING POINT 1: a choice, not luck** | A decision the family made (contacted coaches directly, widened to D2/D3, filled questionnaires). Sourced or labeled composite. | Agency / self-efficacy: shows the outcome as a result of doable actions, which is what the DIY buyer wants (PV Desire 1). | If the source only shows the outcome, skip the beat rather than invent the choice. |
| 4 | **THE RULE THAT MATTERED** | One governing-body rule, with bylaw citation from site/data.js, shown at the moment it changed what they could do (e.g. the contact date for that sport). | Credibility through verifiable specificity: directly attacks the binding trust barrier; the parent can check it. Also sport-specificity (PV Desire 3). | Rule must be current and cited; if the rule changed since the athlete's year, show "rule then / rule now". Never imply the rule caused the outcome. |
| 5 | **TURNING POINT 2: the fit decision** | The final level/school choice and the factor that settled it (academics, aid, playing time), as sourced. | Realistic-fit framing reduces the status-anxiety that drives overspending on services (PV Desire 2). | Do not present D1 as the win and others as consolation. Every level is a destination (BRIEF). |
| 6 | **WHAT THIS STORY DOES NOT PROVE** | One honest line: "One path. Your kid's will differ. We don't contact coaches or your kid." | Two-sided message + expectation setting; inoculates against the "money grab" memory (PV Pain 2). Raises trust in skeptical audiences. | Must be short; a long disclaimer reads as legal cover. Always present, never collapsed. |
| 7 | **THE PLAYBOOK OFFER ("In this story")** | The 1-3 playbooks that map to the beats the reader just saw (beat 3 -> Coach Scripts/Tracker, beat 4 -> Recruiting Calendar, beat 5 -> Offer Comparison/Campus Visit). Price shown, one-time, what's inside, who runs it (the athlete). | Mapping offer to a just-seen step = implementation intention ("when X, I do Y with this tool"); low price shrinks co-payer veto. | Never claim the playbook produced the athlete's outcome. A real athlete never "used" our playbook unless that is sourced (it isn't). Wording: "the steps in this story, as a playbook". |

## Pacing on the scroll
Beats 1-2 above the fold of the story; beat 4 gets the strongest visual moment (the rule card with bylaw); beat 6 sits directly before beat 7, never after it. One CTA per story at beat 7, plus one quiet "save this story" at beat 4. No pop-ups, no exit-intent offers.

## Story QA checklist (gate before publish)
- [ ] Every fact has URL + date; composites labeled at top and on every image ("Illustration").
- [ ] No quotes unless verbatim + sourced; no private-person verbatim posts (paraphrase).
- [ ] Beat 4 bylaw matches site/data.js.
- [ ] Beat 6 present. Beat 7 makes no causal claim.
- [ ] Destinations across the set cover D1/D2/D3/NAIA/JUCO; sports and states diverse.
