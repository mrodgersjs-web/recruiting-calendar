1. Who still recruits late

Signing in sports other than football and basketball opens the second Wednesday of November (D1 Bylaw 13.02.13.1(f)); football regular signing opens the first Wednesday of February (13.02.13.1(e)). Schools set their own final signing dates, so many keep filling rosters into spring. Programs with seniors leaving, a coaching change, or a recruit who backed out often have late needs. Check rosters yourself.

2. The doors

  • D2: athletics aid exists, often partial. Contact rules are broader than D1.
  • D3: no athletic aid (see D3 Path).
  • NAIA: separate association, own eligibility center. TO VERIFY current NAIA rules.
  • JUCO: a two-year route (see JUCO to D1).
