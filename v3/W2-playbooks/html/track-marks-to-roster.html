1. Your marks are the resume

Coaches look at verified marks on results sites, a steady improvement curve, consistency (a second-best mark near your PR) and how you place at state (coach guidance; r/CrossCountry D1 coach thread, 2016, paraphrased). Most events don't need video. You look up each program's recent conference results and roster marks yourself. We don't publish "standards"; they change every season.

4. Target list from marks

For each program: event group, last season's conference-final mark in your event, the roster's best mark in your event, seniors leaving in your event, your PR and your trend. Tiers: Reach (your PR below the roster's scorers), Fit (within range of conference scoring), Sure (you'd score now). Include D2, D3, NAIA, JUCO.

5. Scripts

First email. Subject: 2029 | [Event] [PR] ([meet, date]) | [HS], [ST]

Coach [Last name], I'm [Name], Class of 2029 at [HS]. [Event]: PR [mark] at [meet, date], results link [link]. Progression: [9th] → [10th]. Next meets: [list]. GPA [x.x]. I'd like to know what you look for in your [event group]. [Name], [phone].

Answer every coach reply within 48 hours (recommended). Coaches report dropping athletes who don't respond.
