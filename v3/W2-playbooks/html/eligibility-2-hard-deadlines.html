1. Why these two

Most recruiting dates are openings. Starting later costs nothing. These two are different: miss them and senior-year classes can't repair the damage.

2. Deadline 1: the 10/7 lock

Before the first day of the seventh semester (start of senior year), finish 10 of 16 core courses, and 7 of those 10 must be English, math or natural/physical science. Those 10 grades lock for GPA purposes. Your high school's calendar sets the exact date. (D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.1(b))

Cost if missed: can block qualifier status and first-year competition.

3. Deadline 2: the 16-core finish

A qualifier graduates high school with a minimum 2.300 core GPA (4.000 scale) in 16 core courses (D1 Bylaw 14.3.1.1(a)):

AreaYears
English4
Math (Algebra I or higher)3

Computer science with significant programming may count as math or science where it meets graduation requirements (14.3.1.1(a)). A course must also meet the criteria in Bylaw 14.3.1.3 and appear on your school's NCAA list. The final transcript must reach the Eligibility Center.

A transcript GPA isn't the core GPA. Electives don't count.

4. The Eligibility Center account

  • Free Profile Page: fine early. It's not certification.
  • Certification account: $110 domestic (fee waivers possible). Required before any D1 official visit (D1 Bylaw 13.6.3). Our recommended date: August of the summer before junior year.
  • From April 1 of senior year: request final amateurism certification (Eligibility Center procedure).

5. Counselor meeting script (book it in 9th or 10th grade)

We're planning for NCAA Division I eligibility. Can we check every course my student has taken and plans to take against our school's NCAA-approved list? We need 10 core courses, 7 in English, math or science, done before senior year, and 16 total with a 2.3 core GPA. Which planned classes are not on the list? Who sends final transcripts to the Eligibility Center?

Bring the worksheet below. Leave with a written four-year plan.

7. Core GPA, step by step

  1. Use only the 16 rows above (on the list).
  2. Convert each grade: A=4, B=3, C=2, D=1 (your school's scale may weight honors; the Eligibility Center applies its own rules).
  3. Multiply by credit (full year = 1.0, semester = 0.5).
  4. Sum the points, divide by total credits. Target: 2.300 or higher. Leave margin.
