## 1. Camp types
- **College's own camp:** run by the school or its athletics staff (D1 Bylaw 13.12.1.1).
- **Showcase / third-party event:** run by a company; many colleges attend.
- **Prospect camp vs instructional camp:** prospect camps are evaluations in practice; the bylaw treats both as camps.

## 2. Rules every family should know
- A D1 school's camp must be open to any and all entrants, limited only by number, age, grade or gender (Bylaw 13.12.1.3), and advertised publicly at least 14 days before it starts (Bylaw 13.12.1.6).
- Staff working the camp generally can't recruit you during it, including written aid offers (Bylaw 13.12.1.5), except where the sport-specific exceptions below allow.
- No camp in a dead period or recruiting shutdown (Bylaw 13.12.1.5).

Before that date, a camp is still a chance to be seen. It's just not a conversation.

## 4. Budget
Set a season cap first. Cost = fee + travel + lodging + missed work. Score every camp before paying (sheet below). A camp at a school that isn't on your target list scores low.

## 5. Post-camp follow-up (within 72 hours, recommended)
> Coach [Last name], thank you for [camp, date]. I was the [position], #[number]. I worked on [drill] and would like to know what you'd want me to improve. Film: [link]. [Name], Class of [year].
Only send after your sport's message date (see the *Highlight Reel* playbook, section 6).
