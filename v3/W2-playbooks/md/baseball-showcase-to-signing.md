## 1. Baseball's different clock
Most D1 sports open coach messages on June 15 after sophomore year. Baseball opens later.

| What opens | When (Class of 2029) | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| D1 calls, texts, emails | 2027-08-01 (start of junior year) | D1 Bylaws 13.1.3.1.1, 13.4.1.1 |
| D1 off-campus contact | 2027-09-01 | D1 Bylaw 13.1.1.1.1 (rev. 6/24/26) |
| D1 official and athletics-involved unofficial visits | 2027-09-01 | D1 Bylaws 13.6.2.1.2, 13.7.1.2 (rev. 6/24/26) |
| Recruiting talk at a D1 camp | 2027-09-01 | D1 Bylaw 13.12.1.5.2 (rev. 6/29/26 eff. 8/1/26) |
| Written aid offer | 2028-08-01 (senior year) | D1 Bylaw 13.9.3.1 |
| Signing | PROJECTED 2028-11-08 | D1 Bylaw 13.02.13.1(f) |

D2 is earlier: in-person contact and official visits from 2027-06-15 (D2 Bylaws 13.1.1.1, 13.3.1.2). D3 official visits open 2028-01-01 (D3 Bylaw 13.6.1.1.1).

Roster reality: D1 programs in the House settlement are capped at 34 baseball players (D1 Bylaw 17.2, adopted 6/6/25 eff. 7/1/25). Fewer spots means fewer walk-on chances at those schools.

## 2. The showcase budget
Coaches look for verified numbers: exit and throwing velocity, pop time, 60-yard time (NCSA baseball guidelines; Perfect Game records the 60 and 10-yard split for all attendees). Before you pay:
- Who attends? Ask for last year's list of college coaches present. No list, lower score.
- What's verified? Timed by a neutral source, posted publicly.
- Is it a college's own camp? Then before 2027-09-01 the staff can't talk recruiting with you there (Bylaw 13.12.1.5.2). It's still an evaluation.
- Cap the season. Write a number before signing up for anything.

## 4. Coach scripts
**First email (after 2027-08-01 for D1; any time for D2).** Subject: `2029 [Pos] | [Bats/Throws] | [Verified velo/pop/60] | [HS/Club] | Video`
> Coach [Last name], I'm [Name], Class of 2029 [position] at [HS], playing for [club]. Verified: [measurable, event, date]. Skills video: [link]. Summer schedule: [link]. GPA [x.x]. I'd like to know what you look for at [position]. [Name], [phone]. HS coach: [name, contact].

**Follow-up (14 days).** New number or new clip, one line of interest, schedule link.

**Call questions.** How many [position]s in your 2029 class? How do you split aid across the roster? What would you need to see from me this summer?

## 5. Signing in November
- A verbal commitment binds no one.
- Written offers may come from 2028-08-01; signing opens PROJECTED 2028-11-08.
- Baseball aid is often partial. Ask for the exact dollar amount and duration in writing. Use the *Offers & Signing Day* comparison sheet.
