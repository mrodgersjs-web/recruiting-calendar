## 1. Verbal vs written
A verbal offer or commitment binds neither side. Only a signed athletics aid agreement or settlement-related benefits agreement does (D1 Bylaw 13.02.13.1).

## 4. What signing does
Signing generally ends other schools' recruiting of you and lets the school publicize your commitment (Bylaw 13.10.2). Before signing, the school may only confirm it is recruiting you (13.10.1.1).

## 6. Compliance questions
- Is this aid one year or multi-year, and renewable on what terms?
- Can aid be reduced for athletic reasons?
- Am I counted under the Bylaw 17.2 roster limit?
- What is in the settlement-related benefits agreement, and who pays it?
- What happens if I'm injured?
