## 1. How D3 aid works
Division III schools don't award athletically related aid. Aid is academic (merit) or need-based, and it must be awarded to athletes on the same basis as other students (D3 Bylaw 15, per site/data.js and NCAA D3 guidance). TO VERIFY: exact D3 Bylaw 15 section numbers against the 2026-27 D3 Manual (LSDBi 90011); we have checked only the D1 Manual text directly.

What this means for you: the coach can't hand you money. The coach can often help in admissions. Your grades and your aid application do the money work.

## 3. Net price, honestly
Use each school's federally required Net Price Calculator (on its financial-aid page). Enter the same family numbers every time. Then compare what's left to pay, not the sticker price or a "merit award".

## 4. Admissions support vs coach interest
Ask directly: will you submit my name to admissions as a supported recruit? Does support change my odds, or only timing? Should I apply Early Decision? ED is binding at most schools: you commit before you see competing aid.

## 5. Coach questions
- How many players at my position are you adding for 2029?
- How many first-years made the travel roster last year?
- Can I get a pre-read of my financial aid before ED?
- What does a typical athlete's week look like in season?
