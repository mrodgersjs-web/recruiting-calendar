C. Before you pay

Parent vetting checklist: Colorado SB24-113 and SafeSport

Colorado's Safer Youth Sports law (SB24-113) requires youth sports organizations to run pre-hire criminal history checks on coaches through a consumer reporting agency, to require annual mandatory-reporter training, and to adopt a prohibited-conduct policy. It also encourages annual abuse-prevention training. Ask every private trainer, facility or camp these questions before paying or leaving a minor athlete. Education, not legal advice. We have not verified any provider's safety.
