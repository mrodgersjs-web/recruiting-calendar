Hard eligibility & post-House roster rules

Governing-body text only. A roster limit is a ceiling on team size, not a count of open spots and not a scholarship count. Eligibility is pass/fail and separate from fit.

Rules that moved recently

Under the old D1 model baseball had 11.7 equivalencies across at most 27 aided counters; an opted-in school may now in principle aid all 34 rostered players. Whether it does is a budget decision no tool can see.
