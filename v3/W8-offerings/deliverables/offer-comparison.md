# Offer Comparison & Negotiation Toolkit
Six offers, one grid. See the real dollar number behind each letter.

## The grid (one column per offer)
| Line | What "scholarship" hides |
|---|---|
| Tuition covered % | In-state vs out-of-state rates change the real number |
| Fees covered | $1,500-$4,000/yr at many schools, rarely in the headline |
| Room + board | Covered fresh-soph only at some programs |
| Books | Ask the actual dollar cap |
| Stipend | Team/position specific; ask current players, not coaches |
| Multi-year protection | Year-by-year renewal is the default; ask which years are guaranteed |
| Escapes | What voids aid: transfer, medical, conduct, not being retained |
| Walk-on to scholarship path | Written criteria or vibes? Ask for last year's two examples |
| Redshirt terms | Who decides, when, and what happens to the money |
| Grad continuation | Does aid continue off-roster (grad assistants, medical)? |

## Net cost = (tuition+fees+room+books+required travel) - (aid + stipend + family-expected contribution). Do this math for every offer, same year, same list.

## 12 questions after the verbal offer
1. What is on paper and when? 2. Which years are guaranteed? 3. What voids it? 4. Who else is committed at my position? 5. What happens if the coach leaves? 6. What is the actual academic plan? 7. What does year-2 depth look like? 8. What is the medical protocol? 9. What are the NIL rules of the school? 10. What is your retention history for my class year? 11. What will you tell me that current players would correct? 12. What is the deadline and why?

## What actually moves
- Higher % before signing: works most with leverage (a second offer) and timing (before roster spots fill).
- Better academic package: often faster than raising athletic aid.
- Redshirt terms: cheapest ask, most families never ask.
- What almost never moves after signing day: year 3-4 guarantees.

## Worked example
Two offers: large program, 50% for 1 year, renewable, no stipend, projected net cost $11,800. Mid-major, 75% + fees, guaranteed 4 years, projected net cost $3,900. The family that compared net cost and escape clauses instead of prestige picked the mid-major and graduated with $14k less debt and a real roster year.
