# After the Roster: Career Transition Workbook
The 98% outcome, planned while you still have a trainer and a team.

## Identity inventory (translate roster skills into hiring-manager words)
Train schedule discipline -> manages own deliverables without supervision. Film study -> reads data before deciding. Team role change -> operates in changing structures. Captaincy -> first-line coordination of 10-30 people. Recruiting yourself -> sales cycle: prospect, pitch, handle objections, close. Write 10 lines, each one sentence, each provable by something you actually did.

## The plan, year by year
Junior year of college: one internship named (not "networking"): pick 3 target firms/offices, apply with the inventory above.
Senior year: graduation + the 90-day plan: the first job hunt runs like a season, applications tracked in a log, weekly review, film on your own interviews.
Year 1-2 out: convert sport-adjacent job (equipment rep, ops, coaching, sales at a sponsor) into a skill title, not just a badge.
Year 3-5: the sport network still pays: teammates are now managers. Re-ask, differently than you asked a coach.

## NIL crossover (work that pays while eligible)
Internships at team sponsors, compliance offices, broadcast/iWork side of your own athletic department, camp counseling at your old club. All of these can co-exist with NIL if disclosed: use the Starter Kit paperwork.

## Network map (one page, four columns)
Name | Where they are now | What they can vouch for | When I last spoke. Fill 50 rows from team, trainers, professors, camp coaches. Refresh at reunion/graduation windows. A reference is a relationship, not a signature line.
