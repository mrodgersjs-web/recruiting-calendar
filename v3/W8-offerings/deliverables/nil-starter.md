# The NIL Ledger: Starter Kit
Deal sheet, disclosure calendar and the 3 clauses that protect a first NIL deal.

## Before any deal
- Know your school's NIL policy (some restrict category, agency use, timing vs team sponsors). Print it. It outranks the cool deal.
- Know your state's rule (some states = contract disclosure, some = agent rules).
- Register the deal on your school's timeline, not the dealer's.

## Deal sheet fields to fill (and refuse to skip)
Parties (legal names), what exactly you do (posts? appearance? hours?), term (start/end dates), pay (amount, milestone or flat, due days after), usage rights (who can use your name/likeness, where, how long after the deal ends), exclusivity (what you cannot also do), image approval (you approve posts before they run), kill clause (both sides can exit), taxes (1099, you set it aside).

## The 3 clauses that matter
1. Pay-for-play disguise: if the "deal" is just for showing up to games and scales with minutes, a school/compliance officer may treat it as inducement. Tie pay to deliverables you actually produce.
2. Likeness scope: "all media forever worldwide" is a giveaway. Bound it: channels, term, category.
3. Exit terms: no auto-renew, 14-day kill either side, payment for work already delivered.

## Disclosure calendar (the 3 dates people miss)
- At signing of every deal: school-required disclosure form (yours has a deadline; it is not "seasonally").
- Before using team marks/marks with logos in your content: the school owns them.
- End of term: takedown obligation (some deals require you to delete within 30 days; do it, screenshot the delete).

## Local-dealer red flags
Car dealership "sign-and-spin" that pays in a car's "value" not cash; restaurant percentage-of-sales with no reporting; family-friend deals with no paper; anything where the payer wants a photo of you holding cash posted before the contract is signed.
