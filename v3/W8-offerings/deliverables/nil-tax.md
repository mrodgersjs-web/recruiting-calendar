# NIL Tax & Income Tracker
A spreadsheet that knows you are an athlete first: income log, set-aside %, 1099 prep.

## The one-page version
1. NIL income is self-employment income. No employer withholds. You pay it in April and quarterly if it is big.
2. Set aside a fixed % of every check the day it lands: 25-30% is a safe planning number; confirm with a preparer who has done athlete returns.
3. Keep receipts for what the deal cost you: mileage to appearances, equipment used for content, agent/compliance fees, a slice of phone/plan/internet.
4. The deal payer sends 1099-NEC (or MISC) by Jan 31. If you got paid $600+ and no form, chase it; the income is still taxable without the form.

## Tracker columns (CSV provided)
Date | Payer | Deal | Gross | Expenses against it (itemized tab) | Net | % set aside | Set-aside account | Quarterly due? (if >$1,000 net for the quarter, yes, talk to preparer)

## Hand-your-preparer folder (every Jan)
- 1099s (or a zero-1099 list with your own records)
- This CSV
- Expense receipts folder
- School NIL policy + state rule you filed under (they may need for characterization)
