# Season & Film Planner
One page per season: schedule, film nights, stats to track, the clip list a coach wants.

## Season grid (CSV provided)
Blocks: pre-season (2 weeks), season (week rows), post-season (Jan-Mar recruiting window).
Each game/tournament row: date, opponent, where, who is coming (list coaches invited), film plan, stat target.
Each film-night row: Sunday, 45 minutes, exactly this order: best 3 plays, one mistake, one rep that shows traits without the stat.

## Stats to track (what coaches in your sport actually read)
- Skill sports: not points. Efficiency, reps vs ranked competition, and the position drill number (shot %, pass completion, swing %, tackle rate).
- Time sports: progression curve vs state/regional qualifying standards.
- Grind sports: dual/SC line results, takedown conversion, riding time.
Every stat has a source (box score link, meet results PDF) or it is a rumor.

## The clip list a coach wants (order fixed)
1. Best single play, uncut, 10-20s. 2. Two more same-play-from-same-angle reps. 3. One play showing a trait the stat hides (communication, back-pedal, hand fight). 4. One mistake and the next-rep correction. No music, no logos, name-position-class-school first 3 seconds.

## End-of-season review (3 upgrades max, written)
1. One physical number to move next year (concrete test + date). 2. One film/technique habit. 3. One recruiting action (camp, circuit, school list change). Everything else is noise; 3 upgrades is how a junior year looks different from a sophomore year.
