# Local NIL Deal Kit
Find and close 3 local deals before the market does.

## Target list (build 50, work 10 at a time)
Archetypes that actually pay high-school/college athletes locally: car dealerships (game-day posts), restaurant chains (menu launch), gyms/boxing gyms (summer camps), barbershops (signature cut), supplement/smoke shops (state rule permitting), turf facilities/training complexes, local insurance agents (family angle), real estate teams (moving season), equipment stores (stick/ glove/bat launch), minor league teams (promo nights), car washes (team wash days), pizza spots (game night bundles), laundromats (college move-in), storage facilities (athletes' off-season), local banks (first-account, compliance-approved).

## The pitch (in person beats DM beats email, in that order)
One paragraph: who you are (school, position, class), audience (followers, local %, team/social reach), one specific idea (the exact post or night), the ask ($ or value, term, one deliverable line).
Template variants for all three channels in the kit.

## Deliverable menu (fair and bounded)
- 1 feed post + story set: 1.5 hours including edit and caption approval
- 10-15 min appearance: flat fee, your schedule windows
- Game-day activation (booth or banner): day rate
- Say no to: "exposure", open-ended "support all season", family member's business at family price (state it as a discount line, not a mystery).

## One-page contract checklist
Written or it did not happen: parties, deliverable, term, amount and due date, usage window after end, approval right on posts, takedown date, both-sides exit. Photo with the owner holding the signed page is the receipt and the social post.
