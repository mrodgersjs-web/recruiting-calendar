What we could not settle

  • The 2028-29 calendar. It arrives in July 2027. Every week in Chapter 3 that is marked PROJECTED is arithmetic from the rule text, and the autumn periods may move again.
  • One day in late November. The printed FBS calendar says the dead period starts 29 November 2026; the NCAA's recruiting page says 30 November. We print the PDF and flag the gap.
  • The FCS claim that a school may only sign 26 players a year. It is repeated everywhere and we could not find it in the current football bylaws. Do not plan around it.
  • The "July report day" rule. Several services describe a July date tied to a team reporting day. We found no such rule in the 2026-27 book. The nearest real items are a service-academy exception and the committees' decision to tie January to the transfer window.
  • How many of a team's 105 seats go to transfers. No association publishes that. We gave portal counts instead and said so.
  • Nothing official sets a size or a speed. No association publishes a football standard by position: not the NCAA, not the NAIA, not the NJCAA. Every target in Chapter 6 is a service's floor, printed as one.
  • No coach publishes a stat threshold. We found no named coach and no service stating a tackle, sack or reception number that gets a look at a given level. The chapter prints what staffs say they watch on film instead.
  • The junior college February date. The chart that carries it prints a note that the document is not final. Confirm with the college's own office before you plan on it.
