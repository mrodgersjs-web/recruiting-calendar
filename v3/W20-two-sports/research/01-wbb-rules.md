## A. The disputed first-contact date (Q1)

**1.** The dispute is a false binary: there are three different earliest dates, set by modality. In-person off-campus contact cannot begin before **September 1 of the player's senior year**; phone calls and email may begin **June 1 at the close of the player's sophomore year**.
RULE: Bylaw 13.1.1.1.5 (off-campus contact), 13.1.3.1.5 (telephone), 13.4.1.6 (electronic correspondence/recruiting materials)
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008 (2026-27 NCAA Bylaws, LSDBi report 90008, retrieved 11/11/26)
QUOTE: "off-campus recruiting contacts shall not be made with an individual (or the individual's family members) before September 1 at the beginning of the individual's senior year in high school." / "telephone calls to an individual (or the individual's family members) may not be made before June 1 at the conclusion of the individual's sophomore year in high school." / "shall not provide recruiting materials, including general correspondence related to athletics, or send electronic correspondence ... until June 1 at the conclusion of the individual's sophomore year in high school."

**2.** The "March 1 of the player's junior year" secondary source is **wrong outright**. No off-campus recruiting contact may be made at any point in a prospect's junior year, and the March contact period is restricted to seniors and two-year college PSAs.
RULE: Bylaw 13.1.1.1.5; 13.17.3(b),(d) (archived formula); 2026-27 calendar legend
SOURCE URL: https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/compliance/recruiting/calendar/2026-27/2026-27D1Rec_WBBRecruitingCalendar.pdf
QUOTE: "§ Contact Period MAR 1–31 — For seniors and two-year college PSAs only. Evaluation period for other PSAs for scholastic activities only."

**3.** The "June 1 after the sophomore year" secondary source is **correct but incomplete**: it is right for phone calls and email, and wrong if presented as the general off-campus-contact trigger (that is September 1 of the senior year).
RULE: Bylaw 13.1.3.1.5; 13.4.1.6; contrasted with 13.1.1.1.5
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "In women's basketball, telephone calls ... may not be made before June 1 at the conclusion of the individual's sophomore year in high school."

**4.** Corroboration from the prior cycle: in 2025-26 the March contact period ran "March 1 through April 1" and was also **seniors and two-year college PSAs only** — i.e. it fell in the junior year of the class of 2027, and that class got no off-campus contact then.
RULE: 2025-26 D1 Women's Basketball Recruiting Calendar (the formula Bylaw 13.17.3 still points to)
SOURCE URL: https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/compliance/recruiting/calendar/2025-26/2025-26D1Rec_WBBRecruitingCalendar.pdf
QUOTE: "March 1 through April 1 — CONTACT PERIOD (For seniors and two-year college PSAs only. Evaluation period for other PSAs for scholastic activities only.)"

**5.** Important for a 2026-27 guide: the June 1, 2026 phone/email trigger falls under the **2025-26** calendar, where June 1 sat inside a quiet period (April 7–July 31, 2026). Calls and email were permissible then; in-person off-campus contact was not.
RULE: 13.02.5.4 (quiet period); 2025-26 calendar
SOURCE URL: https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/compliance/recruiting/calendar/2025-26/2025-26D1Rec_WBBRecruitingCalendar.pdf
QUOTE: "April 7 through July 31, except as noted below: QUIET PERIOD"

**6.** Where the rules live now: Bylaw 13.17 was rewritten on 10/7/26 and the sport-by-sport calendar formulas (including 13.17.3) are **no longer printed in the Bylaws**. For 2026-27 the published ncaa.org calendar PDF is the operative document. The calendar PDF itself still carries a now-stale pointer: "See NCAA Division I Bylaw 13.17.3 for Women's Basketball Calendar Formula."
RULE: Bylaw 13.17 (Revised: 10/7/26)
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "For access to recruiting calendars and recruiting periods for other sports, refer to the recruiting calendars published on ncaa.org."

**7.** For reference only, the retired formula text (quoted from the 2025-26 manual, a school-hosted copy of the official NCAA manual): "(a) August 1 through August 31 [except for (1) below]: Quiet Period ... (b) September 1-30: Contact Period for seniors and two-year college PSAs only ... (d) March 1 through Wednesday prior to the NCAA Division I Women's Basketball Championship game: Contact Period for seniors and two-year college PSAs only ... (e) Thursday prior to the ... Championship game through the following Monday: Dead Period ... (f) Tuesday immediately following the ... Championship game through July 31 ...: Quiet Period."
RULE: Bylaw 13.17.3 (archived, 2025-26 manual)

## B. Quiet, dead and evaluation periods, 2026-27 (Q2)

**8.** The complete 2026-27 women's calendar, as published: **Aug 1–31, 2026 Quiet Period** (with **Aug 10–16 Recruiting Shutdown**); **Sept 1–30 Contact Period** (seniors + two-year college PSAs only; others = evaluation, scholastic only); **Oct 1, 2026–Feb 28, 2027 Evaluation Period, scholastic activities only**; **Dec 24–26 Dead Period**; **March 1–31, 2027 Contact Period** (seniors + two-year college PSAs only); **April 1–5 Dead Period**; **April 6–July 31, 2027 Quiet Period**, interrupted by: **Apr 22 Dead** (HS/2-yr PSAs only); **Apr 23–25 Evaluation (certified nonscholastic events only)**; **Apr 26 Dead** (HS/2-yr PSAs only); **May 3–9 Recruiting Shutdown**; **May 13 Dead**; **May 14–16 Evaluation (certified nonscholastic events only)**; **May 17 Dead**; **Jun 16 Dead**; **Jun 17 noon–Jun 19 6 p.m. Evaluation** (scholastic events approved by NCAA and NFHS, and intercollegiate events approved by the applicable two-year college governing body only); **Jun 20 Dead**; **Jul 8 Dead**; **Jul 9–12 Evaluation**; **Jul 13 Dead**; **Jul 22 Dead**; **Jul 23–26 Evaluation**; **Jul 27 Dead**.
RULE: 2026-27 D1 Women's Basketball Recruiting Calendar (updated July 30, 2026)
SOURCE URL: https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/compliance/recruiting/calendar/2026-27/2026-27D1Rec_WBBRecruitingCalendar.pdf
QUOTE: "Evaluation Period OCT 1 – FEB 28, 2027 — Scholastic activities only." / "‡ Evaluation Period — Certified nonscholastic events only."

**9.** Quiet period: in-person recruiting contacts are allowed only on campus; no in-person off-campus contacts or evaluations.
RULE: Bylaw 13.02.5.4
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "A quiet period is that period of time when it is permissible to make in-person recruiting contacts only on the institution's campus. No in-person, off-campus recruiting contacts or evaluations may be made during the quiet period."

**10.** Dead period: no in-person contacts or evaluations, and no official or unofficial visits to campus — but non-in-person communication (calls, email) is still allowed.
RULE: Bylaw 13.02.5.5
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "It remains permissible, however, for an institutional staff member to communicate with (other than in person) a prospective student-athlete during a dead period."

**11.** Evaluation period: coaches may assess academics and playing ability off campus, but no in-person off-campus recruiting contact may be made.
RULE: Bylaw 13.02.5.2
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "No in-person, off-campus recruiting contacts shall be made with the prospective student-athlete during an evaluation period."

**12.** Recruiting shutdown: the strictest — no recruiting at all, including correspondence and telephone calls.
RULE: Bylaw 13.02.5.6
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "A recruiting shutdown is a period of time when no form of recruiting (e.g., contacts, evaluations, official or unofficial visits, correspondence or making or receiving telephone calls) is permissible."

**13.** Contact period: in-person off-campus recruiting contacts and evaluations are permitted.
RULE: Bylaw 13.02.5.1
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "A contact period is a period of time when it is permissible for authorized athletics department staff members to make in-person, off-campus recruiting contacts and evaluations."

**14.** Women's basketball has **no November dead period** in 2026-27 (November is entirely inside the Oct 1–Feb 28 evaluation period). Men's basketball does: **Nov 9–12, 2026** dead period. Families often hear "the November dead period" and assume it applies here — for women it does not.
RULE: 2026-27 D1 Women's Basketball Recruiting Calendar vs 2026-27 D1 Men's Basketball Recruiting Calendar
SOURCE URL: https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/compliance/recruiting/calendar/2026-27/2026-27D1Rec_MBBRecruitingCalendar.pdf
QUOTE: "NOV 9–12" (men's legend)

## C. Official (paid) visits and unofficial-visit restrictions (Q3)

**15.** Official visits for women's basketball may begin **January 1 of the prospect's junior year of high school**.
RULE: Bylaw 13.6.2.1.6 (Revised: 5/11/18, 1/20/22 effective 8/1/22)
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "In women's basketball, an individual may not be provided an expense-paid visit earlier than January 1 of the individual's junior year in high school."

**16.** Men's comparison: an expense-paid visit may not occur before **August 1 at the beginning of the junior year** — a full five months earlier than for women.
RULE: Bylaw 13.6.2.1.5
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "In men's basketball, an individual may not be provided an expense-paid visit earlier than August 1 at the beginning of the individual's junior year in high school."

**17.** A prospect may take one official visit per school before October 15 following high-school completion, and one on or after October 15.
RULE: Bylaw 13.6.2.2
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "An institution may finance one visit to its campus for a prospective student-athlete before October 15 following completion of high school and one visit beginning October 15 following completion of high school."

**18.** Official-visit prerequisites: official transcript, NCAA Eligibility Center registration, and placement on the institutional request list.
RULE: Bylaw 13.6.3(a)-(c)
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "A prospective student-athlete must present the institution with a high school (or college) academic transcript"

**19.** No expense-paid visit during the **July evaluation periods** (July 9–12 and July 23–26, 2027) unless the prospect has signed or a deposit has been made; also no expense-paid visit during the NCAA College Basketball Academy.
RULE: Bylaw 13.6.2.1.6.1; 13.6.2.5
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "an institution may not provide an expense-paid visit to an individual during the July evaluation periods ... unless the individual has signed the institution's written offer of admission and/or financial aid"

**20.** Official and unofficial visits are barred during dead periods and recruiting shutdowns by the definitions themselves (so e.g. no visits during April 1–5, 2027, Aug 10–16, 2026, or May 3–9, 2027).
RULE: Bylaw 13.02.5.5; 13.02.5.6
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "it is not permissible to make in-person recruiting contacts or evaluations on or off the institution's campus or to permit official or unofficial visits by prospective student-athletes to the institution's campus."

**21.** Unofficial visits: in basketball and football, a visit with athletics-department involvement may occur **at any time**, subject to the recruiting calendar.
RULE: Bylaw 13.7.1.5 (Adopted: 7/1/19, Revised: 4/15/26)
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "an unofficial visit with athletics department involvement (e.g., contact with athletics department staff, athletics-specific tour, complimentary admission) may occur with an individual (or the individual's family members) at any time, subject to recruiting calendar restrictions"

**22.** The July unofficial-visit restriction for women: no unofficial visit during the July evaluation periods unless signed or a deposit made; and no unofficial visit during the NCAA College Basketball Academy.
RULE: Bylaw 13.7.2.2
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "a prospective student-athlete may not make an unofficial visit during the July evaluation periods ... unless the prospective student-athlete has signed the institution's written offer of admission and/or financial aid"

**23.** Unofficial-visit limits: a maximum of three complimentary admissions, individual-game basis, within a 30-mile radius, general seating only, and **no complimentary admissions during a dead period**. Number of unofficial visits is unlimited, at the prospect's own expense.
RULE: Bylaw 13.7.3.1; 13.7.2
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "the institution may not pay any expenses or provide any entertainment except a maximum of three complimentary admissions" / "may not be provided during a dead period"

**24.** During an unofficial visit, off-campus contact is permitted within one mile of campus boundaries.
RULE: Bylaw 13.7.5
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "Off-campus contact between an institutional staff member and a prospective student-athlete ... may occur during an unofficial visit within one mile of campus boundaries."

## D. BBCS rules for women (Q4)

**26.** What the Basketball Certification System is: all requests to certify an event or league, **and all team building**, are completed in the BBCS; it is administered by the NCAA Enforcement Certification and Approvals Group under policies set by the Men's and Women's Basketball Oversight Committees.
RULE: NCAA Basketball Certification webpage; Bylaw 13.18
SOURCE URL: https://www.ncaa.org/eligibility-center/recruiting/basketball-certification
QUOTE: "All applications to request NCAA certification of an event or league, and all team building, must be completed in the Basketball Certification System."

**27.** Permissible event windows differ for women and men. Women: **Spring 8 a.m.–10 p.m. daily; June noon Thursday–6 p.m. Saturday; July 8 a.m.–10 p.m. daily.** Men: Spring 8 a.m. on the first day to 4 p.m. on the last day; June noon Friday–6 p.m. Sunday; July 8 a.m.–10 p.m. daily.
RULE: NCAA Basketball Certification, "Permissible Event Times"
SOURCE URL: https://www.ncaa.org/eligibility-center/recruiting/basketball-certification
QUOTE: "**Spring** ... Men: 8 a.m. on the first day to 4 p.m. on the last day | Women: 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily" / "**June** ... Men: Noon Friday to 6 p.m. Sunday | Women: Noon Thursday to 6 p.m. Saturday"

**28.** Women's June evaluation period (June 17, 2027 noon – June 19, 2027 6 p.m.): live-activity evaluations are limited to scholastic and intercollegiate events approved by the NFHS, the NCAA (for non-NFHS scholastic associations) or the applicable two-year college governing body, organized and conducted exclusively by those bodies, and occurring somewhere other than a Division I campus.
RULE: Bylaw 13.1.7.6.2
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "During the June evaluation period, evaluations of live athletics activities are limited to: (a) Scholastic and intercollegiate events that: (1) Are approved by the National Federation of State High School Associations..."

**29.** Women's July evaluation period: an off-campus recruiter may attend institutional camps, international practice/competition, certified noninstitutional events, and the NCAA College Basketball Academy; evaluations on a Division I campus at nonscholastic events are prohibited.
RULE: Bylaw 13.1.7.6.3; 13.1.7.6.4
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "During the July evaluation period, an off-campus recruiter may attend institutional basketball camps ... noninstitutional organized events ... that are certified per Bylaw 13.18 and the NCAA College Basketball Academy."

**30.** Outside June and July, women's evaluations are limited to: regularly scheduled high school/prep/two-year college contests and practices; scholastic activities at the prospect's own school; certified nonscholastic events on the third weekend of April and the third weekend of May; and international practice and competition.
RULE: Bylaw 13.1.7.6.1(a)-(d)
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "Evaluations at certified nonscholastic events (per Bylaw 13.18) during the Friday, Saturday and Sunday of the third weekend in April and the Friday, Saturday and Sunday of the third weekend of May"

**31.** Host-family documentation for BBCS: to use a host family or boarding-school address, the address must be in the same locale as the school the athlete attends, the athlete must have been present within the first 30 days of the fall or spring semester, must have remained enrolled at the same school for the entire academic year, and must expect to complete the year there. For U.S. athletes the proof is a transcript showing attendance within the first 30 days of the fall or spring semester plus grades to date; for non-U.S. athletes, transcript, I-20 and F1 visa.
RULE: NCAA Basketball Certification, "Athlete Address Residency Documentation"
SOURCE URL: https://www.ncaa.org/eligibility-center/recruiting/basketball-certification
QUOTE: "The athlete was present within the first 30 days of the fall or spring semester. The athlete remained enrolled at the same school for the entire academic year."

**32.** Men's vs women's BBCS differences, from the ECAG requirements: (a) the **May event restriction** — no part of a men's event may begin before 8 a.m. on the first day of the May evaluation period or after 4 p.m. on the last day — applies to men only; women may play 8 a.m.–10 p.m. daily; (b) **June scholastic event certification is men's basketball only** ("Scholastic Event Certification ... (MBB only)"); (c) **contact restrictions differ by month**: a men's basketball coach may call/text/email a prospect's family in May, June and July, whereas a women's basketball coach may **not** call, text or email the prospect or the prospect's family in April, May, June or July.
RULE: ECAG Basketball Certification Requirements and Procedures (Event Schedule Restrictions; Separation of NCAA Coaches Requirements; ECAG Subgroup Descriptions)
SOURCE URL: https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/enforcement/ecag/junschbb/ECAG_BasketballCertificationRequirementsandProcedures.pdf
QUOTE: "May Men's Basketball Event Restrictions. During the month of May ... no part of the event may begin prior to 8 a.m. on the first day of the evaluation period or after 4 p.m. on the last day" / "Scholastic Event Certification ... (MBB only)" / WBB table: "April ... No calls/texts/emails."

**33.** Women's recruiting-spend limits: each institution is limited to **seven recruiting opportunities (contacts and evaluations combined) per prospect per year**, and women's basketball staff may not exceed **65 recruiting-person days** per year (measured August 1 through July 31); April–July evaluation activity does not count against the person-day limit.
RULE: Bylaw 13.1.5.5; 13.1.7.6
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "each institution shall be limited to seven recruiting opportunities (contacts and evaluations combined) per prospective student-athlete per year"

## E. Signing periods (Q5)

**34.** The formula: the Basketball Early Signing Period is **the second Wednesday (7 a.m.) in November through the following Wednesday**; the First Date for Basketball Regular Signing is **the Wednesday (7 a.m.) in April one week after the NCAA Men's and Women's Basketball Championships**.
RULE: Bylaw 13.02.13.1(a)-(b)
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "(a) Basketball Early Signing Period. The second Wednesday (7 a.m.) in November through the following Wednesday. (b) First Date for Basketball Regular Signing. The Wednesday (7 a.m.) in April one week after the NCAA Men's and Women's Basketball Championships."

**35.** Published 2026-27 dates: **Early period Nov 11, 2026 – Nov 18, 2026**; **Regular period first date April 14, 2027**, final date set by institutional policy.
RULE: NCAA Eligibility Center, "Signing Dates"
SOURCE URL: https://www.ncaa.org/eligibility-center/recruiting
QUOTE: "Division I Basketball — Early Period — **Initial signing date:** November 11, 2026 **Final signing date:** November 18, 2026" / "Division I Basketball - Regular Period — **Initial signing date:** April 14, 2027 **Final signing date:** Based on institutional policy for awarding scholarships."

**36.** Derivation check for April 14, 2027: the 2027 women's title game is **Sunday, April 4, 2027** (Columbus, Nationwide Arena) and the men's is Monday, April 5, 2027; the first Wednesday in April in the week that follows is April 14. The same championship date independently confirms the women's calendar: bylaw formula (d) ends the March contact period on "Wednesday prior to the ... Championship game" (March 31), (e) creates a dead period "Thursday prior ... through the following Monday" (April 1–5), and (f) starts the quiet period "Tuesday immediately following" (April 6) — exactly the published calendar.
RULE: NCAA 2027 Women's Final Four facts; Bylaw 13.17.3(d)-(f) (archived)
SOURCE URL: https://ncaaorg.s3.amazonaws.com/championships/resources/media/DIWBB/2027WFF_QuickFactsColumbus.pdf

**37.** There is **no late signing period** for basketball. The bylaw provides only the early (November) and regular (April) periods; the regular period's closing date is set by each institution's scholarship-awarding policy, not by the NCAA.
RULE: Bylaw 13.02.13.1(a)-(b); 13.02.13.1.1; NCAA Eligibility Center signing dates
SOURCE URL: https://www.ncaa.org/eligibility-center/recruiting
QUOTE: "**Final signing date:** Based on institutional policy for awarding scholarships."

**38.** Two signing-adjacent rules worth a family guide: a midyear high-school graduate may sign from the first day of the applicable signing period through the institutional final date; and a prospect who signed only one of an athletics aid agreement or settlement related benefits agreement during the early period may sign the other with the same institution any time after the early period closes.
RULE: Bylaw 13.02.13.1.1; 13.02.13.1.2
SOURCE URL: https://web3.ncaa.org/lsdbi/reports/getReport/90008
QUOTE: "may sign an athletics aid agreement and/or a settlement related benefits agreement with the same institution at any time after the conclusion of the applicable sport's early signing period."
