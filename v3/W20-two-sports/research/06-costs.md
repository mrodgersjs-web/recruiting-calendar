## Practical ordering

If I were sequencing this for a family, the only unavoidable spend is the AAU membership ($22-$24) plus the club fee ($700-$4,400). Everything above that is optional exposure, and the published evidence (NCSA's own guide) says spending more did not reliably change outcomes. The single highest-leverage line item is travel, and the single cheapest high-value service is a $50-$95 per-game film breakdown.
